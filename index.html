Methodology

View methodology ▾Hide methodology ▴

Universe

  • English-only PSA 10. Every card is the English print, graded PSA 10. Japanese, Chinese, Korean and other languages, other graders (BGS, CGC, SGC, TAG…) and other grades are excluded.
  • 65 constituents: manga rares, key SP / Wanted / Gold / Signature / alt arts, prize and promo cards, and current meta leaders. Cards under a $100 anchor are left out of the tiers (1 currently has no usable anchor).

Same-spec variant rules

A sale or listing only counts for a card if it is the exact same spec: same card number and same variant. In particular:

  • Manga ≠ AA / SEC (and Red Manga ≠ regular Manga, Gold ≠ Silver).
  • Online ≠ Offline Regionals.
  • Errata ≠ pre-errata.
  • EN ≠ JP.
  • Foil ≠ non-Foil (e.g. P-043 Convention).
  • Winner ≠ participation (and Finalist, Top 64, etc. are their own specs).
  • Reprints (PRB "The Best") only count where the constituent itself is the PRB print. Lots, bundles, raw / "PSA 10 ready", proxies and customs are dropped.

Market anchor

  • The anchor is the weighted average of the (up to) 5 most recent same-spec PSA 10 sales within 180 days, weights 5-4-3-2-1 (newest heaviest).
  • Sales come from PriceCharting's PSA 10 completed-sales feed (eBay sold items). Sales below 0.4× or above 2.5× the median of the 10 most recent kept sales are dropped as outliers. Two cards without a usable PriceCharting page use PSA's "similar items sold" data instead.
  • 30-day / 90-day levels: median of same-spec sales within ±15 days of that date (excluding the 5 anchor sales) when there are at least 2; otherwise PriceCharting's monthly PSA 10 value.

How the index is calculated

  • Price-weighted, fixed basket: level = 1,000 × Σ anchornow ÷ Σ level90 days ago, over the cards that have all three points (anchor, 30-day level, 90-day level). Today that is 56 cards. The 30-day-ago level uses the same basket.
  • Being price-weighted, the index is dominated by the most expensive grails. The equal-weight version is the geometric mean of (anchor ÷ 90-day level) × 1,000 over the same basket.
  • Each refresh appends a new level. Levels are only comparable across refreshes when the basket size (n) is unchanged.

Price tiers

  • Grail $5,000+ · Premium $1,000–4,999 · Core $100–999, by the card's anchor. Under $100 (or no anchor): excluded.

eBay listings

  • Pulled from the eBay Browse API (read-only). All listings refresh about every 3 hours (one search per tracked card plus one per card number). Auctions ending within 24 hours refresh about every 30 minutes. Ended auctions are hidden in your browser.
  • Filters: item located in the US; price ≥ $100; graded-card condition; title says PSA 10; no raw, "ready", proxy, custom, lot, other grader or other grade words; no Japanese or other-language wording; the title must carry the constituent's exact card number and pass its variant rules (above); seller feedback ≥ 20 and ≥ 98% positive. Buy It Now prices far under the spec's floor (below ~35% of anchor) are dropped as likely wrong variant or fake.
  • Buy Now: Buy It Now listings at or below the anchor (up to +10%), sorted by price incl. shipping vs anchor.
  • Auctions: sorted by end time, soonest first (not by current-bid discount, since the final price is usually much higher). Each shows the current bid, the anchor, and a "Good value up to" max bid = 90% of the anchor minus listed shipping, rounded down to $5 (the 10% margin covers price noise and the risk of a thin anchor; it does not include sales tax). Auctions whose current bid is already above that max are not shown.
  • Slots per tier: Grail 20, Premium 30, Core 50 in total, split half Buy Now and half auctions; slots one side cannot fill go to the other. At most 10 rows per card.
  • Detail check: Buy Now rows ≥ 30% below anchor and title-flagged rows (for example "SR" on a SEC card number, OP13 regular Manga where "red" may be missing, EB03 titled 2025) are opened via eBay's item API. Grader, grade, language, card number, rarity, year and description are checked, and mislabelled listings are removed. Rows that pass say "detail-checked". The PSA cert itself is not verified.
  • Other rows are auto-filtered from the listing title. Unless a row says "manually reviewed", check the PSA label, cert and language on the listing before buying. A few constituents are not covered by the listings feed.

中文简介

OPChase 是只收录英文版 PSA 10 的海贼王卡牌（OPTCG）指数，目前跟踪 65 张卡（因不足 100 张，名称为 OPChase Index）。锚价 = 最近约 5 笔同版本 PSA 10 成交的加权平均（新成交权重更高）；版本严格区分：Manga≠AA/SEC、线上≠线下地区赛、勘误≠勘误前、英文≠日文、闪≠非闪、冠军≠参与奖。

按锚价分三档：Grail $5,000+、Premium $1,000–4,999、Core $100–999（<$100 不收录）。每档列出美国 eBay 上低于或接近锚价的 PSA 10 挂单（一口价与拍卖），按相对锚价的折扣排序。所有挂单均为按标题自动筛选，拍卖按结束时间排序，并给出“Good value up to”（锚价的 90% 减运费）作为出价上限。下单前请自行核实 PSA 标签与 cert。
