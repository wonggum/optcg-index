How we verify listings

What OPChase checks, where the data comes from, update times and how to report a problem

What OPChase is: a filter for eBay. We search eBay's live listings for English One Piece TCG cards graded PSA 10 and show only the ones that pass the checks below, linked to eBay. OPChase does not estimate, model or suggest prices: every price shown is the eBay seller's own price or the current eBay bid. For reference, card pages link to PriceCharting, where you can check sold prices yourself.

View all checks ▾Hide ▴

Same version only

A listing only appears for a card if it is the exact same spec: same card number and same version.

  • Manga ≠ Alternate Art / SEC (and Red Manga ≠ regular Manga, Gold ≠ Silver).
  • Online ≠ Offline Regionals.
  • Errata ≠ pre-errata.
  • English ≠ Japanese.
  • Foil ≠ non-Foil (e.g. P-043 Convention).
  • Winner ≠ participation (and Finalist, Top 64, etc. are their own specs).
  • Reprints (PRB "The Best") only count where the card itself is the PRB print. Lots, bundles, raw / "PSA 10 ready", proxies and customs are dropped.

Listing checks

  • Pulled from the eBay Browse API (read-only). All listings refresh about every 4 hours; auctions ending within 24 hours about every 30 minutes. Ended auctions are hidden in your browser.
  • Filters: item located in the US; price ≥ $100; graded-card condition; title says PSA 10; no raw, "ready", proxy, custom, lot, other grader or other grade words; no Japanese or other-language wording (English cards); the title must carry the card's exact number and pass its version rules (above); seller feedback ≥ 20 and ≥ 98% positive; listings ≥ $1,000 from sellers with feedback under 10 are dropped. Each version also has a fixed minimum price that we set by hand (set 2026-10-07, changed only by hand): a Buy It Now below it is dropped as a likely wrong version, reprint, proxy or raw card. Every version also has a fixed maximum price (see below), so absurd asks and mislisted pricier copies do not appear. PSA's own eBay store (PSA Vault) shortens long PSA labels in its titles and can drop the version words; for such listings we read the full PSA label in the listing and its item specifics before matching the version. These fixed values are filters only: they were set once by hand from our own judgment of each version's long-run price level, they are not recalculated from live eBay or sales data, and no price is shown, estimated or suggested from them.
  • Detail check: title-flagged rows (for example "SR" on a SEC card number, OP13 regular Manga where "red" may be missing, EB03 titled 2025) and Buy It Now listings close to the version's fixed minimum are opened via eBay's item API. Grader, grade, language, card number, rarity, year and description are checked, and mislabelled listings are removed. Rows that pass get the ✓ Checked badge. The PSA cert itself is not verified.
  • Badges: ✓ Checked = the item details were opened and matched; no badge = matched from the title and search data only. Either way, check the PSA label, cert and language on the listing before buying.
  • Problems reported by users are added to our QA rules (blocked listings / sellers, new mislabel patterns) so they are filtered automatically from then on.

How listings are arranged

  • Lowest Buy It Now: each card's lowest-priced verified Buy It Now (same version, incl. shipping), one per card; the cards whose lowest price changed most recently (a new cheaper listing, or the previous lowest sold or ended) come first.
  • Ending soon: each card's soonest-ending verified auction, one per card, ending soonest first. Each shows the current bid, the number of bids and the time left from eBay; bids can rise until the auction ends. We do not suggest bids and never call an auction a discount.
  • Tiers: Grail $5,000+ · Premium $1,000–4,999 · Core $100–999, by each card's lowest verified Buy It Now price on eBay right now (incl. shipping). Cards move between tiers as listings change; a card without a live Buy It Now listing is not in a tier but keeps its card page.
  • Inside each tier (and Japanese PSA 10) only two kinds of listing appear: each card's lowest verified Buy It Now and its soonest-ending verified auction. All shows the auctions (ending soonest first) followed by the lowest Buy It Now listings (lowest price first); Auctions and Buy Now show one kind. Every other listing of a card is on its card page.
  • Fixed maximum prices: every version has a hand-set maximum (set 2026-10-07, changed only by hand, roughly twice the version's long-run price level). A Buy It Now or a current bid above it is not shown; if that leaves a card without a Buy It Now, no Buy It Now is shown for it.
  • eBay buttons: "Buy Now $X" opens the lowest-priced verified Buy It Now (incl. shipping) and "Current bid $Y · N bids · time left" the soonest-ending verified auction of that exact version.
  • Search PSA 10 on eBay: when no listing qualifies, a neutral button opens an eBay search for the card number, version words and "PSA 10" (US sellers, CCG Individual Cards, excluding Japanese and other languages, other graders and lots). Those search results come straight from eBay and are not checked by OPChase.
  • Japanese PSA 10 is a separate, display-only section with Japanese language rules instead of English ones and its own fixed, hand-set minimum and maximum price per version. It is never mixed into the English tiers.

Photos, freshness and retention

  • Photos are the sellers' own eBay listing photos, hotlinked from eBay (i.ebayimg.com) and linked back to the listing, credited "Photo: eBay listing by <seller>". Tap one to open the full-size gallery and check the PSA label. A card without a live listing gets an OPChase card plate (typography only). No official card artwork is used.
  • Listings older than 6 hours are never shown (if a refresh fails, the listings disappear instead of going stale). Ended or removed listings are deleted from our data at the next refresh. Each listing section shows when its listings were last updated.
  • eBay listing content is shown in its own block; OPChase's own text (version guides, card details, links) sits outside it.

Sources

  • Listings, prices, bids, photos: eBay (Browse API). Card catalog: TCGplayer English catalog, Limitless TCG and the official card list. PSA label names (used to tell versions apart): PSA's own labels; for population numbers see PSA's population report (we do not copy them). Reference prices: we link to PriceCharting; we do not copy its numbers.
  • Links to eBay include eBay Partner Network affiliate tracking. OPChase may earn a commission from qualifying eBay purchases through links on this site. It does not change which listings we show or their order.

Report an issue

Every listing and card has a ⚑ Report link (plus one in the footer). It opens a prefilled GitHub issue on wonggum/optcg-index with the card id, version, listing link and item id, and a checklist: wrong version, JP not EN, not PSA 10, not a slab, fake or suspicious, other. A free GitHub account is needed.
