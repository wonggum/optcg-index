Methodology

View methodology ▾Hide methodology ▴

Universe

  • English-only PSA 10. Every card is the English print, graded PSA 10. Japanese, Chinese, Korean and other languages, other graders (BGS, CGC, SGC, TAG…) and other grades are excluded.
  • 100 constituents: manga rares, key SP / Wanted / Gold / Signature / alt arts, prize and promo cards, and current meta leaders. Cards under a $100 anchor are left out of the tiers.
  • Insufficient data: a card with fewer than 5 same-spec PSA 10 sales in the last 180 days shows "Insufficient data" instead of a price, is not placed in a tier and gets no listings (4 cards right now, listed below the tiers). It stays in the index calculation with its thin anchor.

Same-spec variant rules

A sale or listing only counts for a card if it is the exact same spec: same card number and same variant. In particular:

  • Manga ≠ AA / SEC (and Red Manga ≠ regular Manga, Gold ≠ Silver).
  • Online ≠ Offline Regionals.
  • Errata ≠ pre-errata.
  • EN ≠ JP.
  • Foil ≠ non-Foil (e.g. P-043 Convention).
  • Winner ≠ participation (and Finalist, Top 64, etc. are their own specs).
  • Reprints (PRB "The Best") only count where the constituent itself is the PRB print. Lots, bundles, raw / "PSA 10 ready", proxies and customs are dropped.

Market anchor

  • The anchor is the weighted average of the (up to) 5 most recent same-spec PSA 10 sales within 180 days, weights 5-4-3-2-1 (newest heaviest).
  • Sales come from PriceCharting's PSA 10 completed-sales feed (eBay sold items). Sales below 0.4× or above 2.5× the median of the 10 most recent kept sales are dropped as outliers. Two cards without a usable PriceCharting page use PSA's "similar items sold" data instead.
  • 30-day / 90-day levels: median of same-spec sales within ±15 days of that date (excluding the 5 anchor sales) when there are at least 2; otherwise PriceCharting's monthly PSA 10 value.

How the index is calculated

  • Price-weighted, fixed basket: level = 1,000 × Σ anchornow ÷ Σ level90 days ago, over the cards that have all three points (anchor, 30-day level, 90-day level). Today that is 60 cards. The 30-day-ago level uses the same basket.
  • Being price-weighted, the index is dominated by the most expensive grails. The equal-weight version is the geometric mean of (anchor ÷ 90-day level) × 1,000 over the same basket.
  • Headline level (chain-linked, divisor-adjusted). Level = Σ anchors of all members ÷ divisor. Each refresh, the level moves only by the price change of the cards that were members on both dates: levelt = levelt−1 × Σ anchort ÷ Σ anchort−1 (same cards). When cards enter or leave, the divisor is recalculated (divisor = Σ anchors of the new member set ÷ level), so the level does not jump. The series is seeded at 849.3 on 2026-09-29 (the published 65-card level, 90 days earlier = 1,000; divisor 297.68). On 2026-09-30, 35 new cards entered at their anchor prices and the divisor moved from 297.68 to 384.50 (99 priced members); the level that day (850.5) reflects only the price change of the 64 existing cards.
  • The 90-day / 30-day change figures use the fixed-basket formula above (cards with all three points), so they are always like-for-like.

Price tiers

  • Grail $5,000+ · Premium $1,000–4,999 · Core $100–999, by the card's anchor. Under $100 (or no anchor): excluded.

eBay listings

  • Pulled from the eBay Browse API (read-only). All listings refresh about every 4 hours (one search per tracked card plus one per card number). Auctions ending within 24 hours refresh about every 30 minutes. Ended auctions are hidden in your browser.
  • Filters: item located in the US; price ≥ $100; graded-card condition; title says PSA 10; no raw, "ready", proxy, custom, lot, other grader or other grade words; no Japanese or other-language wording; the title must carry the constituent's exact card number and pass its variant rules (above); seller feedback ≥ 20 and ≥ 98% positive. Buy It Now prices far under the spec's floor (below ~35% of anchor) are dropped as likely wrong variant or fake.
  • Buy Now: Buy It Now listings at or below the anchor (up to +10%), sorted by price incl. shipping vs anchor.
  • Auctions: sorted by end time, soonest first (not by current-bid discount, since the final price is usually much higher). Each shows the current bid, the anchor, and a "Good value up to" max bid = 90% of the anchor minus listed shipping, rounded down to $5 (the 10% margin covers price noise and the risk of a thin anchor; it does not include sales tax). Auctions whose current bid is already above that max are not shown.
  • Slots per tier: Grail 20, Premium 30, Core 50 in total, split half Buy Now and half auctions; slots one side cannot fill go to the other. At most 10 rows per card.
  • Detail check: Buy Now rows ≥ 30% below anchor and title-flagged rows (for example "SR" on a SEC card number, OP13 regular Manga where "red" may be missing, EB03 titled 2025) are opened via eBay's item API. Grader, grade, language, card number, rarity, year and description are checked, and mislabelled listings are removed. Rows that pass get the ✓ Checked badge. The PSA cert itself is not verified.
  • Badges: ✓ Checked = the item details were opened and matched; Auto = matched from the title and search data only. Either way, check the PSA label, cert and language on the listing before buying. A few constituents are not covered by the listings feed.
  • Every card and listing carries a Character field; the character chips filter all tiers at once (shareable as #c=Nami).
  • Photos are the sellers' own eBay listing photos, hotlinked from eBay (i.ebayimg.com) and linked back to the listing; tap one to enlarge and check the PSA label. Each card's representative photo comes from a matching listing (a detail-checked one when available) and is kept when the card has no current listings; otherwise a placeholder shows the card number. No official card artwork is used.
  • Links are plain eBay links (no affiliate tracking at present).

Report an issue

Every listing and card row has a ⚑ Report link (plus one in the footer). It opens a prefilled GitHub issue on wonggum/optcg-index with the card id, variant, listing link and item id, and a checklist: wrong variant, JP not EN, not PSA 10, not a slab, fake or suspicious, price anchor wrong, other. A free GitHub account is needed. Reports are reviewed, and confirmed problems are added to our QA rules (blocked listings / sellers, new mislabel patterns) so they are filtered automatically from then on.

按锚价分三档：Grail $5,000+、Premium $1,000–4,999、Core $100–999（<$100 不收录）。每档列出美国 eBay 上低于或接近锚价的 PSA 10 挂单（一口价与拍卖），按相对锚价的折扣排序。所有挂单均为按标题自动筛选，拍卖按结束时间排序，并给出“Good value up to”（锚价的 90% 减运费）作为出价上限。下单前请自行核实 PSA 标签与 cert。
