How we verify listings

What OPChase checks, where the data comes from, update times and how to report a problem

What OPChase is: a filter for eBay. We search eBay's live listings for English One Piece TCG cards graded PSA 10 and show only the ones that pass the checks below, linked to eBay. OPChase does not estimate, model or suggest prices: every price shown is the eBay seller's own price or the current eBay bid. For reference, card pages link to PriceCharting, where you can check sold prices yourself.

View all checks ▾Hide ▴

Same version only

A listing only appears for a card if it is the exact same spec: same card number and same version.

  • Manga ≠ Alternate Art / SEC (and Red Manga ≠ regular Manga, Gold ≠ Silver).
  • Online ≠ Offline Regionals.
  • Errata ≠ pre-errata.
  • English ≠ Japanese.
  • Foil ≠ non-Foil (e.g. P-043 Convention).
  • Winner ≠ participation (and Finalist, Top 64, etc. are their own specs).
  • Reprints (PRB "The Best") only count where the card itself is the PRB print. Lots, bundles, raw / "PSA 10 ready", proxies and customs are dropped.

Listing checks

  • Pulled from the eBay Browse API (read-only). All listings refresh about every 4 hours; auctions ending within 24 hours about every 30 minutes. Ended auctions are hidden in your browser.
  • Filters: item located in the US; price ≥ $100; graded-card condition; title says PSA 10; no raw, "ready", proxy, custom, lot, other grader or other grade words; no Japanese or other-language wording (English cards); the title must carry the card's exact number and pass its version rules (above); seller feedback ≥ 20 and ≥ 98% positive; listings ≥ $1,000 from sellers with feedback under 10 are dropped. Listings priced far outside the usual range for that version (a very low Buy It Now, or an auction already far above it) are also dropped as a likely wrong version, fake or shill listing; this is a sanity filter only and no price is shown or suggested from it.
  • Detail check: title-flagged rows (for example "SR" on a SEC card number, OP13 regular Manga where "red" may be missing, EB03 titled 2025) and unusual rows are opened via eBay's item API. Grader, grade, language, card number, rarity, year and description are checked, and mislabelled listings are removed. Rows that pass get the ✓ Checked badge. The PSA cert itself is not verified.
  • Badges: ✓ Checked = the item details were opened and matched; no badge = matched from the title and search data only. Either way, check the PSA label, cert and language on the listing before buying.
  • Problems reported by users are added to our QA rules (blocked listings / sellers, new mislabel patterns) so they are filtered automatically from then on.

How listings are arranged

  • Newly verified: the Buy It Now listings that most recently passed our checks, one per card, newest first.
  • Ending soon: verified auctions, one per card, ending soonest first. Each shows the current bid, the number of bids and the time left from eBay; bids can rise until the auction ends. We do not suggest bids and never call an auction a discount.
  • Tiers: Grail $5,000+ · Premium $1,000–4,999 · Core $100–999, by each card's lowest verified Buy It Now price on eBay right now (incl. shipping). Cards move between tiers as listings change; a card without a live Buy It Now listing is not in a tier but keeps its card page.
  • Buy Now tabs list the lowest prices first; Auctions tabs end soonest first; you can also sort by price. At most 10 listings per card per tier list.
  • eBay buttons: "Buy Now $X" opens the lowest-priced verified Buy It Now (incl. shipping) and "Current bid $Y · N bids · time left" the soonest-ending verified auction of that exact version.
  • Search PSA 10 on eBay: when no listing qualifies, a neutral button opens an eBay search for the card number, version words and "PSA 10" (US sellers, CCG Individual Cards, excluding Japanese and other languages, other graders and lots). Those search results come straight from eBay and are not checked by OPChase.
  • Japanese PSA 10 is a separate, display-only section with Japanese language rules instead of English ones. It is never mixed into the English tiers.

Photos, freshness and retention

  • Photos are the sellers' own eBay listing photos, hotlinked from eBay (i.ebayimg.com) and linked back to the listing, credited "Photo: eBay listing by <seller>". Tap one to open the full-size gallery and check the PSA label. A card without a live listing gets an OPChase card plate (typography only). No official card artwork is used.
  • Listings older than 6 hours are never shown (if a refresh fails, the listings disappear instead of going stale). Ended or removed listings are deleted from our data at the next refresh. Each listing section shows when its listings were last updated.
  • eBay listing content is shown in its own block; OPChase's own text (version guides, card details, links) sits outside it.
