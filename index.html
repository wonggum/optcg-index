About this data

Methodology, filters, update times and how to report a problem

Live eBay listings refresh several times a day (about every 4 hours, auctions ending within 24 hours about every 30 minutes), so listings can be newer than the index.

Universe

  • English-only PSA 10. Every card is the English print, graded PSA 10. Japanese, Chinese, Korean and other languages, other graders (BGS, CGC, SGC, TAG…) and other grades are excluded.
  • 100 constituents: manga rares, key SP / Wanted / Gold / Signature / alt arts, prize and promo cards, and current meta leaders. Cards under a $100 anchor are left out of the tiers.
  • Insufficient data: a card with fewer than 5 same-spec PSA 10 sales in the last 180 days shows "Insufficient data" instead of a price, is not placed in a tier and gets no listings (6 cards right now, listed below the tiers). It stays in the OPChase 100 so you can still open its page, but it is not counted in the Level until it reaches 5 qualifying sales (then it re-enters via the chain-link divisor, with no artificial Level jump).

Same-spec variant rules

A sale or listing only counts for a card if it is the exact same spec: same card number and same variant. In particular:

  • Manga ≠ AA / SEC (and Red Manga ≠ regular Manga, Gold ≠ Silver).
  • Online ≠ Offline Regionals.
  • Errata ≠ pre-errata.
  • EN ≠ JP.
  • Foil ≠ non-Foil (e.g. P-043 Convention).
  • Winner ≠ participation (and Finalist, Top 64, etc. are their own specs).
  • Reprints (PRB "The Best") only count where the constituent itself is the PRB print. Lots, bundles, raw / "PSA 10 ready", proxies and customs are dropped.

Market anchor

  • The anchor is the weighted average of the (up to) 5 most recent same-spec PSA 10 sales within 180 days, weights 5-4-3-2-1 (newest heaviest).
  • Sales come from PriceCharting's PSA 10 completed-sales feed (eBay sold items). Sales below 0.4× or above 2.5× the median of the 10 most recent kept sales are dropped as outliers. A single sale is also dropped when it is more than 40% away from the median of its 6 nearest sales in time and both neighbouring sales are more than 25% away from it (an isolated odd print); the newest and oldest sales are never dropped this way. Card pages show neutral notes: "Price trend: falling" when the median of the 3 latest sales is below 0.85× the 7 before them, and "Thin data" when fewer than 5 same-spec sales happened in 90 days. Two cards without a usable PriceCharting page use PSA's "similar items sold" data instead.
  • 30-day / 90-day levels: median of same-spec sales within ±15 days of that date (excluding the 5 anchor sales) when there are at least 2; otherwise PriceCharting's monthly PSA 10 value.

How the index is calculated

  • Price-weighted, fixed basket: level = 1,000 × Σ anchornow ÷ Σ level90 days ago, over the cards that have an OPChase Index value (≥ 5 comps) and all three points (anchor, 30-day level, 90-day level). Today that is 56 cards. The 30-day-ago level uses the same basket.
  • Being price-weighted, the index is dominated by the most expensive grails. The equal-weight version is the geometric mean of (anchor ÷ 90-day level) × 1,000 over the same basket.
  • Headline level (chain-linked, divisor-adjusted). Level members are constituents with an OPChase Index value (≥ 5 same-spec PSA 10 sales in 180 days). Cards marked Insufficient data are excluded from the Level and re-enter automatically once they reach 5 sales. Level = Σ anchors of Level members ÷ divisor. Each refresh, the level moves only by the price change of the cards that were members on both dates: levelt = levelt−1 × Σ anchort ÷ Σ anchort−1 (same cards). When cards enter or leave, the divisor is recalculated (divisor = Σ anchors of the new member set ÷ level), so the level does not jump. The series is seeded at 849.3 on 2026-09-29 (the published 65-card level, 90 days earlier = 1,000; divisor 297.68). On 2026-09-30, 35 new cards entered at their anchor prices and the divisor moved from 297.68 to 385.45 (99 priced members); the level that day (848.8) reflects only the price change of the 64 existing cards.
  • The Level chart (dashed backcast part) uses PriceCharting's own monthly PSA 10 estimates for the 54 constituents with 13 consecutive months of data, rebased so July 2026 = 1,000. It is an estimate of how the market moved before the Level started, not the Level itself; the solid line is the Level, built from our own sale anchors, and grows by one point each weekly update.
  • The 90-day / 30-day change figures use the fixed-basket formula above (cards with all three points), so they are always like-for-like.

Japanese PSA 10 (not in the OPChase Index)

  • A separate Japanese PSA 10 carousel sits under Core. These cards are not in the English OPChase 100, not in the Level, and not mixed into Grail / Premium / Core.
  • Each tile is tagged JP. Variants use PSA label wording (e.g. Manga Alternate Art, 3rd Anniversary Gold, Special Alternate Art). The set line shows the PSA JAPANESE set text.
  • Sale averages come from PriceCharting's eBay completed sales for the matching JP product page, filtered with JP must/exclude rules (require japanese|jp|jpn; exclude english|en; exclude the trap set "JAPANESE ENGLISH VERSION 2ND ANNIVERSARY SET"; Manga ≠ Red Manga ≠ AA ≠ SP, etc.). Domestic JP venues (Mercari, Yahoo Auctions JP, SNKRDUNK) are not included yet — eBay-only understates JP liquidity.
  • Live US eBay listings use the same 6-hour freshness, photo-credit, and safety filters as the English carousels, with JP language rules instead of EN. A future OPChase JP index will wait until coverage is sufficient.

Price tiers

  • Grail $5,000+ · Premium $1,000–4,999 · Core $100–999, by the card's OPChase Index (or, for gallery-only cards, by recent verified PSA 10 sales). Under $100 (or no usable sales): excluded from the carousels.
  • Tier carousels: every OPChase 100 card with an Index value appears in its tier. Cards with fewer than 5 comps stay visible in the matching tier with a Not in Index tag and a last-sales average (they are not Level members). A small set of non-Index English printings from the Card Database with verified same-spec PSA 10 sales can also appear the same way — never with an Index strip value.

eBay listings

  • Pulled from the eBay Browse API (read-only). All listings refresh about every 4 hours (one search per tracked card plus one per card number). Auctions ending within 24 hours refresh about every 30 minutes. Ended auctions are hidden in your browser.
  • Filters: item located in the US; price ≥ $100; graded-card condition; title says PSA 10; no raw, "ready", proxy, custom, lot, other grader or other grade words; no Japanese or other-language wording; the title must carry the constituent's exact card number and pass its variant rules (above); seller feedback ≥ 20 and ≥ 98% positive; auctions whose current bid/ask is above 1.5× the same-spec average are dropped; listings ≥ $1,000 from sellers with feedback under 10 are dropped. Buy It Now prices far under the spec's floor (below ~35% of anchor) are dropped as likely wrong variant or fake.
  • Buy Now: Buy It Now listings that passed the filters, in our featured order (you can also sort by price). The tables and the featured strip only list Buy It Now prices up to a cap set from the card's OPChase Index; we show the eBay price and our index side by side but no computed comparison between them.
  • Auctions: sorted by end time, soonest first. Each shows the current bid and time left from eBay; the final price is usually much higher than the current bid. Auctions whose current bid is already close to or above the card's OPChase Index are left out. We do not suggest bids.
  • Slots per tier: Grail 20, Premium 30, Core 50 in total, split half Buy Now and half auctions; slots one side cannot fill go to the other. At most 10 rows per card.
  • Detail check: Buy Now rows priced far under the anchor and title-flagged rows (for example "SR" on a SEC card number, OP13 regular Manga where "red" may be missing, EB03 titled 2025) are opened via eBay's item API. Grader, grade, language, card number, rarity, year and description are checked, and mislabelled listings are removed. Rows that pass get the ✓ Checked badge. The PSA cert itself is not verified.
  • Badges: ✓ Checked = the item details were opened and matched; Auto = matched from the title and search data only. Either way, check the PSA label, cert and language on the listing before buying. A few constituents are not covered by the listings feed.
  • Every card and listing carries a Character field; the character chips filter all tiers at once (shareable as #c=Nami).
  • Photos are the sellers' own eBay listing photos, hotlinked from eBay (i.ebayimg.com) and linked back to the listing; tap one to open the full-size gallery (zoom, every photo of the listing) and check the PSA label. Each photo is credited "Photo: eBay listing by <seller>" with a link to the listing. Each card's representative photo comes from a matching listing (a detail-checked one when available) and is removed within 24 hours after that listing is no longer returned by eBay; otherwise a placeholder shows the card number.
