FAQ

How many versions of OP07-015 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP07-015 Monkey.D.Dragon: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo, 1 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP07-015 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the OP07-015 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP07-015 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP07-015 version meets that today.

How many OP07-015 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-25) shows 1,478 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP07-015 Monkey.D.Dragon?

Limitless TCG credits Yosuke Adachi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
