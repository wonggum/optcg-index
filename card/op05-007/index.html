Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-007 Sabo first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 5 English printings across 3 products: 2 alternate art (parallel), 1 standard, 1 reprint, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 11 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Illustration Box Vol.5). Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 5 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 2,520 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Illustration Box Vol.5 (OP-PR) with 2,113. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 2 leaders listed on its card page, most often Ace OP03-001 (1 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-007 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP05-007 Sabo: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-007 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP05-007 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP05 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it.

What is a PSA 10 OP05-007 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP05-007 version meets that today.

How many OP05-007 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-15) shows 2,520 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-007 Sabo?

Limitless TCG credits tasaka for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
