Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP12-112 Baby 5 first appeared in English in Legacy of the Master (OP12), released Aug 22, 2025. Our database lists 6 English printings across 2 products: 4 promo, 1 standard, 1 winner / top prize. 5 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 5-; Promo · Tournament Pack 2026 Vol. 1; Winner / Top prize · Winner Pack 2026 Vol. 1; …). On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 6 leaders listed on its card page, most often Nami OP11-041 (138 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP12-112 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP12-112 Baby 5: 4 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP12-112 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP12-112 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP12-112 version meets that today.

How many OP12-112 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-07-02) shows 100 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP12-112 Baby 5?

Limitless TCG credits kicori for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
