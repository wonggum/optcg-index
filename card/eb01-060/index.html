Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

EB01-060 Did Someone Say...Kami? first appeared in English in Extra Booster: Memorial Collection (EB01), released May 3, 2024. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 17 months after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 2 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Enel OP05-098 (16 decklists).
