Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA label names, Limitless TCG)

OP01-005 Uta first appeared in English in Romance Dawn (OP01), released Dec 2, 2022. Our database lists 6 English printings across 3 products: 4 promo, 2 standard. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in One Piece Demo Deck Cards (OP-DD), released Dec 5, 2024, about 2 years after the first. 4 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Demo Deck 2023; Promo · Premium Card Collection -ONE PIECE FILM RED Edition-; Promo · Gift Collection 2023; …). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 2 leaders listed on its card page, most often Zoro OP01-001 (34 decklists).
