OP02-043 Roronoa Zoro first appeared in English in Paramount War Pre-Release Cards (OP02-PRE), released Mar 3, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP02-043_p1 (in -Uta-[ST-11]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in ST-11: Starter Deck 11 Uta (ST11), released Feb 2, 2024, 11 months after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 5 leaders listed on its card page, most often Kid ST02-001 (44 decklists).
