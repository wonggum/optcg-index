Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP08-076 It's to Die For first appeared in English in Two Legends Pre-Release Cards (OP08-PRE), released Sep 6, 2024, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 13 months after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 2 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Luffy OP11-040 (24 decklists).
