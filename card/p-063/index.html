Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

P-063 Jinbe first appeared in English in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, as the Reprint version. Our database lists 6 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 finalist / top cut, 1 reprint, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 foil (reprint set). 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Event Pack Vol. 4; Promo · CS 2024 Event Pack; Finalist / Top cut · CS 2024 Event Pack Finalist). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 189 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · CS 2024 Event Pack (OP-PR) with 73. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Bonney OP07-019 (1 decklists).
