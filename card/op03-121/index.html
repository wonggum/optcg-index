Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP03-121 Thunder Bolt first appeared in English in Pillars of Strength (OP03), released Jun 30, 2023. Our database lists 8 English printings across 5 products: 2 reprint, 2 foil (reprint set), 2 promo, 1 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP03-121_r2 (in -ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST- [PRB-01]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 16 months after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 2; Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 1-). FAQ

How many versions of OP03-121 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP03-121 Thunder Bolt: 2 Reprint, 2 Foil (reprint set), 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP03-121 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP03 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it.

What is a PSA 10 OP03-121 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP03-121 version meets that today.
