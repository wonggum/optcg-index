FAQ

How many versions of OP13-004 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP13-004 Sabo: 3 Promo, 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP13-004 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Who illustrated OP13-004 Sabo?

Limitless TCG credits He Ziwei for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
