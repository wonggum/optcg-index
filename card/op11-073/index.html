Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP11-073 Charlotte Linlin first appeared in English in A Fist of Divine Speed (OP11), released Jun 6, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 promo. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -6 assort vol.1-). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 270 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (OP11) with 137. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Katakuri OP11-062 (8 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP11-073 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP11-073 Charlotte Linlin: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP11-073 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP11-073 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP11-073 version meets that today.

How many OP11-073 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-04-28) shows 270 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP11-073 Charlotte Linlin?

Limitless TCG credits S-KINOKO for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
