Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP04-089 Bartolomeo first appeared in English in Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04), released Sep 22, 2023. Our database lists 6 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 full art, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 reprint, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 14 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 3). On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 9 leaders listed on its card page, most often Sakazuki OP05-041 (40 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP04-089 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP04-089 Bartolomeo: 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP04-089 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP04-089 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP04 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP04 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

What is a PSA 10 OP04-089 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP04-089 version meets that today.

How many OP04-089 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2024-11-13) shows 100 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP04-089 Bartolomeo?

Limitless TCG credits Koushi Rokushiro for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
