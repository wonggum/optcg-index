OP01-061 Kaido first appeared in English in Romance Dawn (OP01), released Dec 2, 2022. Our database lists 2 English printings across 1 product: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel). FAQ

How many versions of OP01-061 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP01-061 Kaido: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-061 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP01-061 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP01-061 version meets that today.
