Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST21-014 Monkey.D.Luffy first appeared in English in ST-21: Starter Deck 21 EX Gear 5 (ST21), released Mar 14, 2025. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal · Luffy Deck version in Learn Together Deck Set (LT01), released Oct 3, 2025, 7 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · 3rd Anniversary Treasure Campaign Pack). On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 8 leaders listed on its card page, most often Shanks OP09-001 (90 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of ST21-014 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of ST21-014 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST21-014 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 ST21-014 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST21-014 version meets that today.

How many ST21-014 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 2,563 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated ST21-014 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Hokuyuu for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
