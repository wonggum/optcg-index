FAQ

How many versions of P-084 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of P-084 Buggy: 1 Release Event, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Reprint, 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 P-084 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-084 version meets that today.

How many P-084 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 109 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-084 Buggy?

Limitless TCG credits Kyosuke Usuta for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
