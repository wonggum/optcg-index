P-084 Buggy first appeared in English in ST-25: Starter Deck 25 BLUE Buggy (ST25), released Jun 6, 2025, as the Reprint · Promo Reprint version. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 1 release event, 1 winner / top prize, 1 reprint, 1 SP. The most recent dated printing is the SP version in The World's Strongest Warriors (OP17), released Aug 28, 2026, 15 months after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Release Event · OP10 Release Event; Winner / Top prize · OP10 Release Event Winner). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Buggy OP09-042 (22 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of P-084 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of P-084 Buggy: 1 Release Event, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Reprint, 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Who illustrated P-084 Buggy?

Limitless TCG credits Kyosuke Usuta for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
