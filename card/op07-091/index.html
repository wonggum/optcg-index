In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

Our database lists 7 English printings across 2 products: 2 finalist / top cut, 2 promo, 2 winner / top prize, 1 standard. 6 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Finalist / Top cut · Offline Regional 2024 Vol. 3; Promo · Online Regional 2024 Vol. 3; Finalist / Top cut · Online Regional 2024 Vol. 3; …). FAQ

How many versions of OP07-091 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP07-091 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Promo, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP07-091 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP07-091 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP07-091 version meets that today.

How many OP07-091 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-22) shows 545 PSA 10 copies across the 7 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP07-091 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Nijihayashi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
