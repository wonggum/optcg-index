Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP03-008 Buggy first appeared in English in Pillars of Strength (OP03), released Jun 30, 2023. Our database lists 6 English printings across 5 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 SP, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 reprint, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, about 2.3 years after the first. New printings came out in 2023, 2024 and 2025. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 2). Printed rarities: UC (5), PR (1). 6 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1,594 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the SP (OP06) with 1,484. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 5 leaders listed on its card page, most often Zoro OP01-001 (203 decklists).
