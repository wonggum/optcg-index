FAQ

How many versions of OP01-025 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP01-025 Roronoa Zoro: 3 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-025 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Which OP01-025 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Promo · Treasure Cup from One Piece Promotion Cards has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($14,045, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP01-025 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 5,366 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.
