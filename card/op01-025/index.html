Gameplay

Keywords: Rush

How it plays

A red 3-cost Zoro with Rush, a simple and efficient early attacker. It was a staple of the early red era in OP01-001 Zoro and OP01-002 Law decks (Red Zoro held a 35.0% top-cut share in OP03, as of 2026-10-22), but it left Standard with the Block 1 rotation.

FAQ

How many versions of OP01-025 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP01-025 Roronoa Zoro: 3 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-025 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
