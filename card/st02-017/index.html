Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST02-017 Straw Sword first appeared in English in ST-02: Starter Deck 2 Worst Generation (Super Pre-Release Edition) (ST02-PRE), released Sep 30, 2022, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Revision Pack Cards (OP-RP), released Nov 26, 2024, about 2.2 years after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 10 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 3 leaders listed on its card page, most often Kid ST02-001 (23 decklists).
