Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP12-030 Dracule Mihawk first appeared in English in Legacy of the Master (OP12), released Aug 22, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 SP. The most recent dated printing is the SP version in The Azure Sea's Seven (OP14), released Jan 16, 2026, 5 months after the first. FAQ

How many versions of OP12-030 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP12-030 Dracule Mihawk: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP12-030 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP12-030 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP12-030 version meets that today.

How many OP12-030 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-25) shows 1,984 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP12-030 Dracule Mihawk?

Limitless TCG credits Matsumoto Akira for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
