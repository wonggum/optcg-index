Printing history

P-053 Nami first appeared in English in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, as the Foil (reprint set) · Jolly Roger Foil version. Our database lists 6 English printings across 3 products: 2 promo, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 full art, 1 reprint, 1 standard. The most recent dated printing is the Normal · Nami Deck version in Learn Together Deck Set (LT01), released Oct 3, 2025, 11 months after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Sealed Battle Kit Vol. 1; Promo · Premium Card Collection -Live Action Edition-). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 4 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Luffy OP11-040 (14 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of P-053 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of P-053 Nami: 2 Promo, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 P-053 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check.

Who illustrated P-053 Nami?

Limitless TCG credits nuisuke for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
