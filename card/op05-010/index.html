FAQ

How many versions of OP05-010 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP05-010 Nico Robin: 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP05-010 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 OP05-010 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP05-010 version meets that today.

How many OP05-010 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-06-18) shows 121 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-010 Nico Robin?

Limitless TCG credits Hashimoto Q for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
