FAQ

How many versions of ST04-010 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of ST04-010 Who's.Who: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST04-010 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.
