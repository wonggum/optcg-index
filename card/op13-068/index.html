Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP13-068 Douglas Bullet first appeared in English in Carrying On His Will: 3rd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP13-ANN), released Oct 31, 2025, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Carrying On His Will (OP13), released Nov 7, 2025, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 2 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Roger OP13-003 (6 decklists).
