Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP04-024 Sugar first appeared in English in Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04), released Sep 22, 2023. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 SP. The most recent dated printing is the SP version in Wings of the Captain (OP06), released Mar 15, 2024, 6 months after the first. Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1,283 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the SP (OP06) with 1,002. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 7 leaders listed on its card page, most often Doflamingo OP04-019 (45 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP04-024 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP04-024 Sugar: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP04-024 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP04-024 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP04-024 version meets that today.

How many OP04-024 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-17) shows 1,283 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP04-024 Sugar?

Limitless TCG credits Koushi Rokushiro for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
