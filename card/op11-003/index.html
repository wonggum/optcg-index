OP11-003 Usopp first appeared in English in A Fist of Divine Speed Release Event Cards (OP11-RE), released May 30, 2025, as the Release Event version. Our database lists 5 English printings across 5 products: 2 standard, 1 release event, 1 reprint, 1 promo. FAQ

How many versions of OP11-003 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP11-003 Usopp: 2 Normal, 1 Release Event, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Who illustrated OP11-003 Usopp?

Limitless TCG credits Aidle Co. Ltd. for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
