FAQ

How many versions of EB04-038 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of EB04-038 Rosinante & Law: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB04-038 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 EB04-038 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no EB04-038 version meets that today.

How many EB04-038 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 441 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB04-038 Rosinante & Law?

Limitless TCG credits Studio Vigor Co. Ltd for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
