FAQ

How many versions of P-055 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of P-055 Monkey.D.Luffy: 3 Promo, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 P-055 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 P-055 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-055 version meets that today.

How many P-055 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 35,954 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-055 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits NIJIMAARC for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
