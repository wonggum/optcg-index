Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

P-029 Bartolomeo first appeared in English in ST-16: Starter Deck 16 GREEN Uta (ST16), released Oct 25, 2024. Our database lists 7 English printings across 3 products: 2 promo, 1 finalist / top cut, 1 standard, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 full art, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 14 days after the first. 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Event Pack Vol. 1; Promo · CS 2023 Event Pack; Finalist / Top cut · CS 2023 Event Pack Finalist Ver.). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 7 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 168 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · CS 2023 Event Pack (OP-PR) with 58. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Kid ST02-001 (26 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of P-029 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of P-029 Bartolomeo: 2 Promo, 1 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 P-029 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check.

What is a PSA 10 P-029 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-029 version meets that today.

How many P-029 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-17) shows 168 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-029 Bartolomeo?

Limitless TCG credits TAPIOCA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
