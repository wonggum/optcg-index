FAQ

How many versions of P-112 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of P-112 Nami: 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-112 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Who illustrated P-112 Nami?

Limitless TCG credits BISAI for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
