Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP02-035 Trafalgar Law first appeared in English in Paramount War Pre-Release Cards (OP02-PRE), released Mar 3, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 9 English printings across 4 products: 2 promo, 2 finalist / top cut, 2 winner / top prize, 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP02-035_p2 (in -Uta-[ST-11]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in ST-11: Starter Deck 11 Uta (ST11), released Feb 2, 2024, 11 months after the first. 6 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Online Regional 2023 · Participant; Finalist / Top cut · Online Regional 2023; Winner / Top prize · Online Regional 2023; …). FAQ

How many versions of OP02-035 are there?

Our database lists 9 English printings of OP02-035 Trafalgar Law: 2 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP02-035 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.
