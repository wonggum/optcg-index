FAQ

How many versions of P-010 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of P-010 Kaido: 2 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-010 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 P-010 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-010 version meets that today.

How many P-010 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-03) shows 425 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-010 Kaido?

Limitless TCG credits BISAI for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
