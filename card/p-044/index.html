Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

P-044 Sabo first appeared in English in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, as the Foil (reprint set) · Pirate Foil version. Our database lists 5 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 finalist / top cut, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 reprint. 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Event Pack Vol. 4; Promo · CS 2024 Event Pack; Finalist / Top cut · CS 2024 Event Pack Finalist). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 5 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 244 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · CS 2024 Event Pack (OP-PR) with 153. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Betty OP05-002 (2 decklists).
