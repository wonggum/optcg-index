FAQ

How many versions of OP02-059 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP02-059 Boa Hancock: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Box Topper, 1 Pre-Release. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP02-059 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP02-059 version meets that today.

How many OP02-059 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-15) shows 2,858 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP02-059 Boa Hancock?

Limitless TCG credits nuisuke for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
