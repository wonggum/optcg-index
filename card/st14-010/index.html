FAQ

How many versions of ST14-010 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of ST14-010 Brook: 2 Reprint, 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 ST14-010 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST14-010 version meets that today.

How many ST14-010 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2024-12-18) shows 2 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated ST14-010 Brook?

Limitless TCG credits DAI-XT. for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
