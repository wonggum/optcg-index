FAQ

How many versions of OP06-110 are there?

Our database lists 11 English printings of OP06-110 Nekomamushi: 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Promo, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP06-110 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Is the PRB-01 OP06-110 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 OP06-110 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP06-110 version meets that today.

Who illustrated OP06-110 Nekomamushi?

Limitless TCG credits HISASHI HUJIWARA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
