FAQ

How many versions of P-135 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of P-135 Monkey.D.Luffy: 1 Release Event, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 P-135 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-135 version meets that today.

Who illustrated P-135 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Bashikou for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
