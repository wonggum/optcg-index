Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA label names, Limitless TCG)

OP05-106 Shura first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 5 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 2 promo, 1 winner / top prize. The most recent dated printing is the Normal · Luffy Deck version in Set Sail Deck Set (SD01), released Sep 18, 2026, about 2.8 years after the first. 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Tournament Pack Vol. 7; Winner / Top prize · Winner Pack Vol. 7; Promo · CS 2024 Celebration Pack). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 4 of 5 printings match a PSA label in our data. FAQ

How many versions of OP05-106 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP05-106 Shura: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP05-106 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Who illustrated OP05-106 Shura?

Limitless TCG credits Daiki Ihara for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
