FAQ

How many versions of OP10-109 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP10-109 Basil Hawkins: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP10-109 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 OP10-109 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP10-109 version meets that today.

How many OP10-109 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 88 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP10-109 Basil Hawkins?

Limitless TCG credits ASAKI KURODA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
