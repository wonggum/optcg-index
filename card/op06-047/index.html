FAQ

How many versions of OP06-047 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP06-047 Charlotte Pudding: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Reprint, 1 SP, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP06-047 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP06-047 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP06-047 version meets that today.

How many OP06-047 PSA 10s exist?
