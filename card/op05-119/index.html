Collector & player notes

Why collectors chase it

Gear 5 Luffy's first card. The OP05 Manga Alternate Art is one of the most valuable English cards in the game: OPChase tracks its PSA 10 in five figures, and OPChase's collector notes rank it top tier with the OP11 3rd Anniversary SP Gold/Silver GVBPC. The Alternate Art is a mid-tier PSA 10 with a large population (5,061 PSA 10 as of 2026-09-28) GR.

Traps

OP05-119 has PRB-01 (One Piece The Best) reprints of the base, Alternate Art and Manga. They are separate PSA specs filed under the PRB01 set line and trade far lower. Listings often just say 'OP05-119 Luffy PSA 10', so check the set line on the label KBGVB. Physical cues: PRB-01 reprints carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left, have no pen icon next to the illustrator name, and the PRB-01 Manga has no textured foil REDOAPI. The OP11 Gold and Silver SPs are two different specs KBGR.

Game role

A 10-cost Purple Character whose [On Play] DON!! −10 gives an extra turn LIM.

Seen in decklists (Limitless TCG)

Leaders of the recent Limitless TCG decklists that include this card: Luffy OP05-060 (29), Luffy ST10-002 (22), Luffy OP09-061 (6). Counts are the number of listed decklists, as shown on the card's Limitless page.

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-119 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Parallel (AA), 2 Manga, 1 Normal, 1 Reprint, 1 Wanted, 1 SP, 1 SP Gold, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-119 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP05-119 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

Which OP05-119 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Manga from Awakening of the New Era has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($17,453, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP05-119 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 19,121 PSA 10 copies across the 10 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits TAPIOCA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
