Gameplay

Ability timing: On Play, Activate: Main, Once Per Turn

How it plays

The Gear 5 Luffy: a purple 10-cost Character whose entry ability spends ten DON!! to put your other Characters on the bottom of the deck and take an extra turn, and which can also ramp DON!! once per turn. It is a signature card used only in dedicated builds around ST10-002, OP05-060 and OP09-061 Luffy, as its extra-turn cost is steep.

Keywords from the official English card list (Bandai); play note written by OPChase.

Collector & player notes

Versions to tell apart

Gear 5 Luffy's first card. In OP05 it comes as the base SEC, an Alternate Art (PSA: ALTERNATE ART) and a Manga (PSA: MANGA ALTERNATE ART); the OP11 3rd Anniversary SP comes in Gold and Silver. Each is a separate PSA spec, so match the set line and the variety line TCGGRKB.

Traps

OP05-119 has PRB-01 (One Piece The Best) reprints of the base, Alternate Art and Manga. They are separate PSA specs filed under the PRB01 set line. Listings often just say 'OP05-119 Luffy PSA 10', so check the set line on the label KBGVB. Physical cues: the PRB-01 reprints have no pen icon next to the illustrator name (the OP05 originals do), and TCGplayer notes the PRB-01 Manga was reprinted without the original textured foil REDOAPI. The faint bottom-left 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp is also on original OP05 prints, so it does not tell them apart. The OP11 Gold and Silver SPs are two different specs KBGR.

Game role

A 10-cost Purple Character whose [On Play] DON!! −10 gives an extra turn LIM.

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-119 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Parallel (AA), 2 Manga, 1 Normal, 1 Reprint, 1 Wanted, 1 SP, 1 SP Gold, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-119 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP05-119 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP05 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. TCGplayer notes that the PRB-01 Manga was reprinted without the original textured foil.
