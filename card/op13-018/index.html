OP13-018 Wapol first appeared in English in Carrying On His Will: 3rd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP13-ANN), released Oct 31, 2025, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament.
