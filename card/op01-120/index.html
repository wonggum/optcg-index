Collector & player notes

Versions

Shanks OP01-120 has an Alternate Art and a Manga in OP01, plus PRB-01 reprints (plain, Alternate Art and Manga) and a serial-numbered Championship 2023 prize. The PRB-01 versions are filed by PSA under the PRB01 set line, and the PRB-01 Manga lacks the original's textured foil TCGGRRED.

Seen in decklists (Limitless TCG)

Leaders of the recent Limitless TCG decklists that include this card: Luffy ST01-001 (23), Zoro OP01-001 (7), Luffy ST10-002 (4), Ace OP03-001 (1), Marco OP08-002 (1). Counts are the number of listed decklists, as shown on the card's Limitless page.

FAQ

How many versions of OP01-120 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP01-120 Shanks: 2 Parallel (AA), 2 Manga, 1 Normal, 1 Reprint, 1 Serial. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-120 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP01-120 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

Which OP01-120 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Manga · Parallel from Romance Dawn has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($5,088, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP01-120 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 5,257 PSA 10 copies across the 6 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP01-120 Shanks?

Limitless TCG credits Makitoshi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
