FAQ

How many versions of OP14-084 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP14-084 Ms. All Sunday: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP14-084 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP14-084 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP14-084 version meets that today.

How many OP14-084 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-25) shows 2,749 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP14-084 Ms. All Sunday?

Limitless TCG credits Daisuke Enoshima for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
