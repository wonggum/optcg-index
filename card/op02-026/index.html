FAQ

How many versions of OP02-026 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP02-026 Sanji: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP02-026 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

How many OP02-026 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-07-20) shows 715 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.
