FAQ

How many versions of OP01-039 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP01-039 Killer: 2 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Reprint, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP01-039 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP01-039 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP01-039 version meets that today.

How many OP01-039 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-06-04) shows 81 PSA 10 copies across the 7 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP01-039 Killer?

Limitless TCG credits Koushi Rokushiro for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
