Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

P-079 Lim first appeared in English in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, as the Foil (reprint set) · Pirate Foil version. Our database lists 5 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 finalist / top cut, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 standard. 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Event Pack Vol. 6; Promo · CS 25-26 Event Pack; Finalist / Top cut · CS 25-26 Event Pack Finalist Ver.). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 5 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 4 PSA 10 copies in total.
