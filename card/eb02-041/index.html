Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

EB02-041 Merry Go first appeared in English in Extra Booster: Anime 25th Collection (EB02), released May 9, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 standard. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 5-; Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 7). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 41 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 5- (OP-PR) with 20. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Luffy EB02-010 (202 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of EB02-041 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of EB02-041 Merry Go: 2 Promo, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How many EB02-041 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-17) shows 41 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB02-041 Merry Go?
