FAQ

How many versions of OP02-034 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP02-034 Tony Tony.Chopper: 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Release Event. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP02-034 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.
