FAQ

How many versions of OP02-004 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP02-004 Edward.Newgate: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 SP, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP02-004 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP02-004 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP02 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP02 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

What is a PSA 10 OP02-004 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP02-004 version meets that today.

How many OP02-004 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-15) shows 1,893 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP02-004 Edward.Newgate?

Limitless TCG credits tatsuya for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
