FAQ

How many versions of OP12-033 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP12-033 Helmeppo: 1 Normal, 1 Release Event. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP12-033 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP12-033 version meets that today.

Who illustrated OP12-033 Helmeppo?
