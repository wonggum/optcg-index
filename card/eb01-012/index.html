FAQ

How many versions of EB01-012 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of EB01-012 Cavendish: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB01-012 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 EB01-012 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no EB01-012 version meets that today.

How many EB01-012 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-15) shows 185 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB01-012 Cavendish?

Limitless TCG credits phima for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
