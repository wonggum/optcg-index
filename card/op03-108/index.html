Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP03-108 Charlotte Cracker first appeared in English in Pillars of Strength (OP03), released Jun 30, 2023. Our database lists 4 English printings across 2 products: 2 alternate art (parallel), 1 standard, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 16 months after the first. Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 4 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 213 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (OP03) with 131. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 8 leaders listed on its card page, most often Katakuri OP03-099 (147 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP03-108 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP03-108 Charlotte Cracker: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP03-108 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP03-108 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP03 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP03 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

What is a PSA 10 OP03-108 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP03-108 version meets that today.

How many OP03-108 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-19) shows 213 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP03-108 Charlotte Cracker?

Limitless TCG credits Misa Tsutsui for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
