Collector & player notes

Which version is which

Five English versions matter to collectors: base SEC, Alternate Art, Super Alt-Art (PSA: MANGA ALTERNATE ART), Red Super Alt-Art (PSA: RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART) and the Wanted Poster SP TCGGR. The Red version is OPChase's top-tier grail; listings that just say 'OP13-118 Manga' may be the non-red version GVBKB.

Prize version

A Treasure Cup Top 8 version also exists with a very small population; a single sale is not a reliable price KBGVB.

Seen in decklists (Limitless TCG)

Leaders of the recent Limitless TCG decklists that include this card: Luffy OP13-001 (17), Luffy EB02-010 (4), Zoro & Sanji ST12-001 (2). Counts are the number of listed decklists, as shown on the card's Limitless page.

FAQ

How many versions of OP13-118 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP13-118 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Super AA, 1 Red Manga, 1 Wanted. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP13-118 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is the difference between the OP13-118 Red Manga and the regular Manga?

The Red Super Alt-Art is a red-foil version found only in OP-13 (official product page). PSA files it as RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART; the non-red Super Alt-Art is MANGA ALTERNATE ART. They are different specs with different populations and prices.

Which OP13-118 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Red Manga (Red Super AA) from Carrying On His Will has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($30,178, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP13-118 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 11,332 PSA 10 copies across the 6 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP13-118 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Anderson for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
