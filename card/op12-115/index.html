Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP12-115 I Love You!! first appeared in English in Legacy of the Master Release Event Cards (OP12-RE), released Aug 15, 2025, as the Release Event version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 release event. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Legacy of the Master (OP12), released Aug 22, 2025, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 3 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Rosinante OP12-061 (74 decklists).
