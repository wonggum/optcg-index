Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP09-091 Vasco Shot first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World: 2nd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP09-ANN), released Dec 6, 2024, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in ST-27: Starter Deck 27 BLACK Marshall.D.Teach (ST27), released Jun 6, 2025, 6 months after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. None of the printings matches a PSA label in our data yet, so no PSA 10 population is shown. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Blackbeard OP09-081 (125 decklists).
