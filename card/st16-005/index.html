Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST16-005 Monkey.D.Luffy first appeared in English in ST-16: Starter Deck 16 GREEN Uta (ST16), released Oct 25, 2024. Our database lists 6 English printings across 3 products: 2 promo, 1 standard, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 reprint, 1 winner / top prize. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 11 months after the first. 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Beginners Deck Party [ST-23] - [ST-28] Participation Pack; Winner / Top prize · Beginners Deck Party [ST-23] - [ST-28] Winner Pack; Promo · Welcome Pack Vol. 2). On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Uta ST11-001 (5 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of ST16-005 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of ST16-005 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Reprint, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST16-005 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 ST16-005 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST16-005 version meets that today.

How many ST16-005 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-04) shows 151 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated ST16-005 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Yasushi Yamaguchi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
