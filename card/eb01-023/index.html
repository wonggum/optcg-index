Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

EB01-023 Edward Weevil first appeared in English in Extra Booster: Memorial Collection (EB01), released May 3, 2024. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 2 promo, 1 standard, 1 SP. The most recent dated printing is the SP version in The Azure Sea's Seven (OP14), released Jan 16, 2026, 20 months after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 4; Promo · Seven Warlords of the Sea Binder Set). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 4 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 869 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the SP (OP14) with 715. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Doflamingo OP01-060 (396 decklists).
