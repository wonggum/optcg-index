Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP04-114 Randolph first appeared in English in Kingdoms of Intrigue Pre-Release Cards (OP04-PRE), released Sep 15, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04), released Sep 22, 2023, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 2 leaders listed on its card page, most often Katakuri OP03-099 (94 decklists).
