FAQ

How many versions of OP09-076 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP09-076 Roronoa Zoro: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Reprint, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-076 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Which OP09-076 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Promo · Championship 25-26 Regionals Season 1 from One Piece Promotion Cards has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($8,573, from the last same-spec sales). How many OP09-076 PSA 10s exist?

Who illustrated OP09-076 Roronoa Zoro?
