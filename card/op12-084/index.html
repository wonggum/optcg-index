FAQ

How many versions of OP12-084 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP12-084 Emporio.Ivankov: 1 Normal, 1 Release Event, 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP12-084 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP12-084 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP12-084 version meets that today.

Who illustrated OP12-084 Emporio.Ivankov?

Limitless TCG credits HISASHI HUJIWARA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
