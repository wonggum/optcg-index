Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP09-106 Nico Olvia first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World: 2nd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP09-ANN), released Dec 6, 2024, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 8 English printings across 3 products: 2 promo, 2 finalist / top cut, 2 winner / top prize, 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Emperors in the New World (OP09), released Dec 13, 2024, 7 days after the first. 6 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Offline Regional Participation Pack 2025 Vol.1; Finalist / Top cut · Offline Regional Finalist Card Set 2025 Vol.1; Winner / Top prize · Online Regional Champion Card Set 2025 Vol.1; …). Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 8 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 92 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Finalist / Top cut · Offline Regional Finalist Card Set 2025 Vol.1 (OP-PR) with 74. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Robin OP09-062 (3 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP09-106 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP09-106 Nico Olvia: 2 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP09-106 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP09-106 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP09-106 version meets that today.

How many OP09-106 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-06-04) shows 92 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP09-106 Nico Olvia?
