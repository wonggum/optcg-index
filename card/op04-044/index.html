FAQ

How many versions of OP04-044 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP04-044 Kaido: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 SP, 1 Normal, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP04-044 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP04-044 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 OP04-044 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP04-044 version meets that today.

How many OP04-044 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 2,417 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP04-044 Kaido?

Limitless TCG credits Mitsuaki Matsumoto for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
