FAQ

How many versions of P-092 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of P-092 Koby: 2 Promo, 1 Release Event, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Finalist / Top cut. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-092 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.
