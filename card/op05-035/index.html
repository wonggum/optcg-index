FAQ

How many versions of OP05-035 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP05-035 Bellamy: 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP05-035 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP05-035 version meets that today.

How many OP05-035 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-07-20) shows 5 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-035 Bellamy?

Limitless TCG credits BISAI for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
