Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST21-010 Nico Robin first appeared in English in ST-21: Starter Deck 21 EX Gear 5 (ST21), released Mar 14, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 2 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel). The most recent dated printing is the Normal · Luffy Deck version in Learn Together Deck Set (LT01), released Oct 3, 2025, 7 months after the first. FAQ

How many versions of ST21-010 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of ST21-010 Nico Robin: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST21-010 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 ST21-010 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST21-010 version meets that today.

How many ST21-010 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-19) shows 612 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated ST21-010 Nico Robin?

Limitless TCG credits eiji kaneda for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
