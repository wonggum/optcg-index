Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP13-008 Emporio.Ivankov first appeared in English in Carrying On His Will: 3rd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP13-ANN), released Oct 31, 2025, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP13-008_r1 (in STARTER DECK -RED · BLACK Sabo- [ST-35]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in ST-35: Starter Deck 35 RED/BLACK Sabo (ST35), released Jul 31, 2026, 9 months after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. None of the printings matches a PSA label in our data yet, so no PSA 10 population is shown.
