FAQ

How many versions of OP08-110 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP08-110 Wyper: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP08-110 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the OP08-110 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Who illustrated OP08-110 Wyper?

Limitless TCG credits Hatori Kyoka for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
