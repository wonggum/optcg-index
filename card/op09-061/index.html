FAQ

How many versions of OP09-061 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP09-061 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-061 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Which OP09-061 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Promo · English Version 2nd Anniversary Set from One Piece Promotion Cards has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($841, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.
