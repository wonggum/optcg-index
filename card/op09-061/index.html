FAQ

How many versions of OP09-061 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP09-061 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-061 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
