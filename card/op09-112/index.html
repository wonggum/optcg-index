Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP09-112 Belo Betty first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World: 2nd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP09-ANN), released Dec 6, 2024, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Emperors in the New World (OP09), released Dec 13, 2024, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 8 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Anniversary Tournament (OP09-ANN) with 6. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Betty OP05-002 (1 decklists).
