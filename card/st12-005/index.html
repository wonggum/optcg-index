FAQ

How many versions of ST12-005 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of ST12-005 Perona: 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 ST12-005 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST12-005 version meets that today.

How many ST12-005 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-07-23) shows 6 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated ST12-005 Perona?

Limitless TCG credits Hatori Kyoka for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
