Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP08-067 Charlotte Pudding first appeared in English in Two Legends (OP08), released Sep 13, 2024. Our database lists 5 English printings across 3 products: 2 promo, 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 13 months after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Treasure Cup 2025; Promo · Store Treasure Cup 2025). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 5 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 338 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (OP08) with 319. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 2 leaders listed on its card page, most often Katakuri OP11-062 (11 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP08-067 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP08-067 Charlotte Pudding: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP08-067 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP08-067 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP08-067 version meets that today.

How many OP08-067 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-26) shows 338 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP08-067 Charlotte Pudding?

Limitless TCG credits Yuu Shimotsuki for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
