FAQ

How many versions of OP06-101 are there?

Our database lists 9 English printings of OP06-101 O-Nami: 4 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Finalist / Top cut. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP06-101 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the OP06-101 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP06-101 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP06-101 version meets that today.

How many OP06-101 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 4,825 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP06-101 O-Nami?

Limitless TCG credits Hagane Tsurugi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
