Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

PRB02-006 Roronoa Zoro first appeared in English in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025. Our database lists 5 English printings across 3 products: 2 promo, 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 SP. The most recent dated printing is the SP version in The Azure Sea's Seven (OP14), released Jan 16, 2026, 3 months after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Welcome Pack 2026 Vol.1; Promo · Round 1 Promo). FAQ

How many versions of PRB02-006 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of PRB02-006 Roronoa Zoro: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the PRB02-006 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 PRB02-006 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no PRB02-006 version meets that today.

Who illustrated PRB02-006 Roronoa Zoro?
