FAQ

How many versions of OP05-100 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP05-100 Enel: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-100 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Who illustrated OP05-100 Enel?

Limitless TCG credits Hokuyuu for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
