Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP02-117 Ice Age first appeared in English in Paramount War Pre-Release Cards (OP02-PRE), released Mar 3, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 7 English printings across 5 products: 2 reprint, 2 foil (reprint set), 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP02-117_r2 (in -ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST- [PRB-01]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 20 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 1-). FAQ

How many versions of OP02-117 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP02-117 Ice Age: 2 Reprint, 2 Foil (reprint set), 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP02-117 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 OP02-117 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP02-117 version meets that today.

How many OP02-117 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2025-06-26) shows 76 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.
