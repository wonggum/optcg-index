FAQ

How many versions of OP16-065 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP16-065 Sakazuki: 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP16-065 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Where can I find a verified PSA 10 OP16-065?
