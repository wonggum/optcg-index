FAQ

How many versions of OP05-082 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP05-082 Shirahoshi: 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-082 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP05-082 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 OP05-082 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP05-082 version meets that today.

How many OP05-082 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-31) shows 276 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-082 Shirahoshi?

Limitless TCG credits Hatori Kyoka for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
