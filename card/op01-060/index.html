OP01-060 Donquixote Doflamingo first appeared in English in Romance Dawn (OP01), released Dec 2, 2022. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 2 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel). FAQ

How many versions of OP01-060 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP01-060 Donquixote Doflamingo: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-060 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
