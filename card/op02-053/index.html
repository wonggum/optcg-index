FAQ

How many versions of OP02-053 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP02-053 Crocodile: 2 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP02-053 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Who illustrated OP02-053 Crocodile?

Limitless TCG credits Akihiro MIYANO for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
