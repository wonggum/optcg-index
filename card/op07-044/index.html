Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP07-044 Dracule Mihawk first appeared in English in 500 Years in the Future Pre-Release Cards (OP07-PRE), released Jun 21, 2024, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 4 English printings across 4 products: 2 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Learn Together Deck Set (LT01), released Oct 3, 2025, 15 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Seven Warlords of the Sea Binder Set). Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 200 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Seven Warlords of the Sea Binder Set (OP-PR) with 199.
