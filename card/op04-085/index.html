Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP04-085 Suleiman first appeared in English in Kingdoms of Intrigue Pre-Release Cards (OP04-PRE), released Sep 15, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04), released Sep 22, 2023, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 4 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Normal (OP04) with 2.
