Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP07-104 Nico Robin first appeared in English in 500 Years in the Future Pre-Release Cards (OP07-PRE), released Jun 21, 2024, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in 500 Years in the Future (OP07), released Jun 28, 2024, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 99 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Pre-Release (OP07-PRE) with 54.

FAQ

How many versions of OP07-104 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP07-104 Nico Robin: 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP07-104 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP07-104 version meets that today.

How many OP07-104 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-21) shows 99 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP07-104 Nico Robin?

Limitless TCG credits BISAI for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
