FAQ

How many versions of OP04-092 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP04-092 Rebecca: 3 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP04-092 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP04-092 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP04-092 version meets that today.

How many OP04-092 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-04-06) shows 262 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.
