FAQ

How many versions of OP09-085 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP09-085 Gecko Moria: 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP09-085 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP09-085 version meets that today.

How many OP09-085 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-02-11) shows 4 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP09-085 Gecko Moria?

Limitless TCG credits Aidle Co. Ltd. for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
