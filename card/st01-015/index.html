FAQ

How many versions of ST01-015 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of ST01-015 Gum-Gum Jet Pistol: 3 Normal, 2 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Pre-Release. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST01-015 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.
