Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP10-022 Trafalgar Law first appeared in English in Royal Blood (OP10), released Mar 21, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 promo. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Flame-Flame Fruit Coliseum). Every printing carries the L rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 527 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (OP10) with 510. On Limitless TCG, Trafalgar Law OP10-022 decks rank #13 with 1.02% of tracked points in the OP12 format.

FAQ

How many versions of OP10-022 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP10-022 Trafalgar Law: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP10-022 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP10-022 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP10-022 version meets that today.

How many OP10-022 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-14) shows 527 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP10-022 Trafalgar Law?

Limitless TCG credits Noriyuki Hirota for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
