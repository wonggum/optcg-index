FAQ

How many versions of OP07-113 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP07-113 Roronoa Zoro: 3 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP07-113 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP07-113 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP07-113 version meets that today.

How many OP07-113 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-11) shows 5,366 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP07-113 Roronoa Zoro?

Limitless TCG credits Koushi Rokushiro for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
