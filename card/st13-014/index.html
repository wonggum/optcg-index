FAQ

How many versions of ST13-014 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of ST13-014 Monkey.D.Luffy: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST13-014 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Who illustrated ST13-014 Monkey.D.Luffy?
