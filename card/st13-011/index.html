Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST13-011 Portgas.D.Ace first appeared in English in ST-13: Ultra Deck The Three Brothers (ST13), released Apr 19, 2024. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 SP. FAQ

How many versions of ST13-011 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of ST13-011 Portgas.D.Ace: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST13-011 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
