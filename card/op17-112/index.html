FAQ

How many versions of OP17-112 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP17-112 Charlotte Linlin: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP17-112 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 OP17-112 worth?
