Gameplay

Keywords: Rush · Ability timing: On Play

How it plays

A blue 6-cost Ace that gains Rush at 3 Life or less; on entry it gives a rested DON!! to your Leader and can return an opposing Character of cost 5 or less to hand, after which the opponent may play a Character of cost 4 or less from hand. It sees light play in OP13-002 Ace. Bandai announced that the Wanted Poster printing carries an incorrect effect and offered an exchange; all other printings use the correct text.

Which version is which

PSA's population report lists the Manga Alt Arts as RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART and MANGA ALTERNATE ART; label wording unconfirmed GR. The official site published an errata for OP13-119 on October 30, 2025 OFF.

FAQ

How many versions of OP13-119 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP13-119 Portgas.D.Ace: 3 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga Alt Art, 1 Red Manga Alt Art, 1 Wanted. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP13-119 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. PSA's population report lists it as ALTERNATE ART and the base card with no variety; that label wording is unconfirmed, so read the slab.

What is the difference between the OP13-119 Red Manga Alt Art and the regular Manga Alt Art?

The Red Manga Alt Art is a red-toned version found only in OP-13 (official product page); the non-red version is the regular Manga Alt Art (some sellers call it Super AA). They are different cards, so check the variety line on the slab. PSA's population report lists them as RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART and MANGA ALTERNATE ART; that label wording is unconfirmed.

Who illustrated OP13-119 Portgas.D.Ace?

Limitless TCG credits SENNSU for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
