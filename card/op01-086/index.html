OP01-086 Overheat first appeared in English in Romance Dawn (OP01), released Dec 2, 2022. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP01-086_r1 (in -Blue Donquixote Doflamingo- [ST-17]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in ST-17: Starter Deck 17 BLUE Donquixote Doflamingo (ST17), released Oct 25, 2024, 23 months after the first. Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 3 leaders listed on its card page, most often Nami OP03-040 (11 decklists).
