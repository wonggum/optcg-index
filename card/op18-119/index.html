FAQ

How many versions of OP18-119 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP18-119 Saint Shamrock: 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP18-119 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
