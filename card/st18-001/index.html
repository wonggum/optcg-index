FAQ

How many versions of ST18-001 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of ST18-001 Uso-Hachi: 1 Normal, 1 SP, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST18-001 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 ST18-001 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST18-001 version meets that today.

How many ST18-001 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-03-19) shows 1,392 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated ST18-001 Uso-Hachi?

Limitless TCG credits Yasushi Yamaguchi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
