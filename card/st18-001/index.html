FAQ

How many versions of ST18-001 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of ST18-001 Uso-Hachi: 1 Normal, 1 SP, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST18-001 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Who illustrated ST18-001 Uso-Hachi?

Limitless TCG credits Yasushi Yamaguchi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
