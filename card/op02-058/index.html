OP02-058 Buggy first appeared in English in Paramount War (OP02), released Mar 10, 2023. Our database lists 2 English printings across 1 product: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel). FAQ

How many versions of OP02-058 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP02-058 Buggy: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP02-058 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP02-058 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP02-058 version meets that today.

How many OP02-058 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2025-12-15) shows 265 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP02-058 Buggy?

Limitless TCG credits Denim2 for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
