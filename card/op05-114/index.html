Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-114 El Thor first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 6 English printings across 4 products: 2 foil (reprint set), 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 reprint, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Foil (reprint set) · Jolly Roger Foil version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 11 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 2-). Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 86 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 2- (OP-PR) with 81. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 5 leaders listed on its card page, most often Enel OP05-098 (36 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-114 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP05-114 El Thor: 2 Foil (reprint set), 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP05-114 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP05 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it.

What is a PSA 10 OP05-114 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP05-114 version meets that today.

How many OP05-114 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2025-11-11) shows 86 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.
