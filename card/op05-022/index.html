Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-022 Donquixote Rosinante first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 SP. The most recent dated printing is the SP version in Extra Booster: Anime 25th Collection (EB02), released May 9, 2025, 17 months after the first. FAQ

How many versions of OP05-022 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP05-022 Donquixote Rosinante: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-022 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
