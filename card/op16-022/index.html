FAQ

How many versions of OP16-022 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP16-022 Monkey.D.Luffy: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP16-022 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Which OP16-022 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Alternate Art (AA) from The Time of Battle has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($137, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP16-022 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-01) shows 99 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP16-022 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Paji for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
