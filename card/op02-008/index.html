FAQ

How many versions of OP02-008 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP02-008 Jozu: 3 Promo, 2 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP02-008 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP02-008 version meets that today.

How many OP02-008 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-27) shows 45 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP02-008 Jozu?

Limitless TCG credits Nijihayashi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
