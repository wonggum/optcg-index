Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP02-003 Atmos first appeared in English in Paramount War Pre-Release Cards (OP02-PRE), released Mar 3, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Paramount War (OP02), released Mar 10, 2023, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 2 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Normal (OP02) with 1. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 3 leaders listed on its card page, most often Whitebeard OP02-001 (227 decklists).
