FAQ

How many versions of ST01-007 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of ST01-007 Nami: 4 Promo, 2 Normal, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Treasure Rare. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST01-007 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Which ST01-007 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Winner / Top prize · 2025 New Year Event Winner from One Piece Promotion Cards has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($5,472, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many ST01-007 PSA 10s exist?
