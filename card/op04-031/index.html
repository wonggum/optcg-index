Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP04-031 Donquixote Doflamingo first appeared in English in Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04), released Sep 22, 2023. Our database lists 4 English printings across 2 products: 2 alternate art (parallel), 1 standard, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 14 months after the first. Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 4 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 558 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (OP04) with 340. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 10 leaders listed on its card page, most often Bonney OP07-019 (285 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP04-031 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP04-031 Donquixote Doflamingo: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP04-031 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP04-031 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP04 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP04 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

What is a PSA 10 OP04-031 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP04-031 version meets that today.

How many OP04-031 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-07-06) shows 558 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP04-031 Donquixote Doflamingo?

Limitless TCG credits DAI-XT. for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
