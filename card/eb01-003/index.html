FAQ

How many versions of EB01-003 are there?

Our database lists 11 English printings of EB01-003 Kid & Killer: 3 Promo, 2 Normal, 2 Winner / Top prize, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB01-003 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the EB01-003 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Which EB01-003 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Finalist / Top cut · Offline Regional Finalist Card Set 2025 Vol.2 from One Piece Promotion Cards has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($261, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many EB01-003 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2025-01-18) shows 1,661 PSA 10 copies across the 7 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB01-003 Kid & Killer?

Limitless TCG credits HISASHI HUJIWARA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
