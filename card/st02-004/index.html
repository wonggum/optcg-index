FAQ

How many versions of ST02-004 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of ST02-004 Capone"Gang"Bege: 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST02-004 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 ST02-004 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 ST02-004 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST02-004 version meets that today.
