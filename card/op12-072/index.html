Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP12-072 Zeff first appeared in English in Legacy of the Master Release Event Cards (OP12-RE), released Aug 15, 2025, as the Release Event version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 release event.
