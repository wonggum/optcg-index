FAQ

How many versions of ST15-005 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of ST15-005 Portgas.D.Ace: 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Who illustrated ST15-005 Portgas.D.Ace?
