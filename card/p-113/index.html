FAQ

How many versions of P-113 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of P-113 Jewelry Bonney: 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-113 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

How many P-113 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-19) shows 449 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-113 Jewelry Bonney?

Limitless TCG credits BISAI for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
