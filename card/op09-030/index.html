OP09-030 Trafalgar Law first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World: 2nd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP09-ANN), released Dec 6, 2024, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Emperors in the New World (OP09), released Dec 13, 2024, 7 days after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data.

FAQ

How many versions of OP09-030 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP09-030 Trafalgar Law: 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Who illustrated OP09-030 Trafalgar Law?

Limitless TCG credits Nijihayashi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
