OP09-011 Hongo first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World: 2nd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP09-ANN), released Dec 6, 2024, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 7 English printings across 4 products: 3 promo, 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 reprint, 1 winner / top prize. FAQ

How many versions of OP09-011 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP09-011 Hongo: 3 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament, 1 Reprint, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP09-011 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Who illustrated OP09-011 Hongo?

Limitless TCG credits Moopic for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
