Our database lists 7 English printings across 5 products: 2 standard, 2 foil (reprint set), 1 pre-release, 1 reprint, 1 promo. FAQ

How many versions of OP06-038 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP06-038 The Billion-fold World Trichiliocosm: 2 Normal, 2 Foil (reprint set), 1 Pre-Release, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP06-038 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check.
