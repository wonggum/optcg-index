Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP02-015 Makino first appeared in English in Paramount War Pre-Release Cards (OP02-PRE), released Mar 3, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 7 English printings across 5 products: 2 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 full art, 1 reprint, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in One Piece Demo Deck Cards (OP-DD), released Dec 5, 2024, 21 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Japanese 1st Anniversary Set). FAQ

How many versions of OP02-015 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP02-015 Makino: 2 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP02-015 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP02 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP02 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

What is a PSA 10 OP02-015 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP02-015 version meets that today.
