FAQ

How many versions of EB01-057 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of EB01-057 Shirahoshi: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB01-057 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Who illustrated EB01-057 Shirahoshi?

Limitless TCG credits Bashikou for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
