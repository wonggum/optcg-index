FAQ

How many versions of OP15-002 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP15-002 Lucy: 1 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP15-002 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 OP15-002 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP15-002 version meets that today.

How many OP15-002 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-22) shows 313 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP15-002 Lucy?

Limitless TCG credits He Ziwei for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
