FAQ

How many versions of ST07-007 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of ST07-007 Charlotte Brulee: 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 ST07-007 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 ST07-007 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST07-007 version meets that today.
