How many versions of OP12-021 are there?

What is a PSA 10 OP12-021 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP12-021 version meets that today.

Who illustrated OP12-021 Ipponmatsu?
