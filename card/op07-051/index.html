FAQ

How many versions of OP07-051 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP07-051 Boa Hancock: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 Manga, 1 SP, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP07-051 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Which OP07-051 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Manga · Parallel from 500 Years in the Future has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($2,813, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP07-051 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 7,549 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP07-051 Boa Hancock?

Limitless TCG credits Hagane Tsurugi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
