FAQ

How many versions of OP01-073 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP01-073 Donquixote Doflamingo: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-073 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP01-073 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP01-073 version meets that today.

How many OP01-073 PSA 10s exist?

Who illustrated OP01-073 Donquixote Doflamingo?
