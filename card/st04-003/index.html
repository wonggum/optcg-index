FAQ

How many versions of ST04-003 are there?

Our database lists 12 English printings of ST04-003 Kaido: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 2 Winner / Top prize, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Wanted, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST04-003 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the ST04-003 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Is the PRB-01 ST04-003 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check.
