Gameplay

Keywords: Rush · Ability timing: Activate: Main, Once Per Turn

How it plays

A red 6-cost Yamato that, once per turn with a Land of Wano or Portgas.D.Ace leader, gives a rested DON!! to the Leader and then gains Rush for the turn. Its leader condition keeps it niche, with light use almost entirely in OP13-002 Ace lists.

FAQ

How many versions of EB02-006 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of EB02-006 Yamato: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo, 1 Serial. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB02-006 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
