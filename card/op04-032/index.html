FAQ

How many versions of OP04-032 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP04-032 Baby 5: 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Reprint, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP04-032 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP04 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP04 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

What is a PSA 10 OP04-032 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP04-032 version meets that today.

How many OP04-032 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2024-11-08) shows 110 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP04-032 Baby 5?

Limitless TCG credits Akira Kano for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
