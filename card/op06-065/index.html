FAQ

How many versions of OP06-065 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP06-065 Vinsmoke Niji: 1 Normal, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP06-065 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP06-065 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check.

What is a PSA 10 OP06-065 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP06-065 version meets that today.

How many OP06-065 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2025-12-08) shows 26 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP06-065 Vinsmoke Niji?

Limitless TCG credits Moopic for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
