FAQ

How many versions of OP11-053 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP11-053 Tony Tony.Chopper: 1 Normal, 1 Release Event, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP11-053 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP11-053 version meets that today.

How many OP11-053 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-04-21) shows 116 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP11-053 Tony Tony.Chopper?

Limitless TCG credits Suzume Muraichi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
