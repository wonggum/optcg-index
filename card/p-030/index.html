FAQ

How many versions of P-030 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of P-030 Jinbe: 4 Promo, 1 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 P-030 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-030 version meets that today.

How many P-030 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-24) shows 327 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-030 Jinbe?

Limitless TCG credits SOWSOW for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
