Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

P-030 Jinbe first appeared in English in ST-17: Starter Deck 17 BLUE Donquixote Doflamingo (ST17), released Oct 25, 2024. Our database lists 6 English printings across 2 products: 4 promo, 1 finalist / top cut, 1 standard. 5 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Event Pack Vol. 1; Promo · CS 2023 Event Pack; Finalist / Top cut · CS 2023 Event Pack Finalist Ver.; …). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 6 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 469 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Premium Card Collection -BANDAI CARD GAMES Fest. 23-24 Edition- (OP-PR) with 149. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Doflamingo OP01-060 (34 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of P-030 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of P-030 Jinbe: 4 Promo, 1 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How many P-030 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-24) shows 469 PSA 10 copies across the 6 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-030 Jinbe?

Limitless TCG credits SOWSOW for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
