FAQ

How many versions of ST26-005 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of ST26-005 Monkey.D.Luffy: 1 Normal, 1 SP. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Which ST26-005 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the SP from Adventure on Kami's Island has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($988, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.
