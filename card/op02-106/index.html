FAQ

How many versions of OP02-106 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of OP02-106 Tsuru: 3 Promo, 2 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint, 1 Finalist / Top cut. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP02-106 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP02 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP02 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

Who illustrated OP02-106 Tsuru?

Limitless TCG credits Nijihayashi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
