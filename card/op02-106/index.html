FAQ

How many versions of OP02-106 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of OP02-106 Tsuru: 3 Promo, 2 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint, 1 Finalist / Top cut. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP02-106 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check.
