Gameplay

Keywords: Blocker · Ability timing: Activate: Main, Once Per Turn

How it plays

A black 6-cost Blocker Sabo that, once per turn, gives one of your Characters +2 cost until the end of the opponent's next turn and then gives a rested DON!! to your Leader. It sees very little play, mainly in OP13-004 Sabo.

FAQ

How many versions of OP13-120 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP13-120 Sabo: 2 Promo, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Super AA, 1 Red Manga, 1 Wanted, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP13-120 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is the difference between the OP13-120 Red Manga and the regular Manga?

The Red Super Alt-Art is a red-toned version found only in OP-13 (official product page). PSA files it as RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART; the non-red Super Alt-Art is MANGA ALTERNATE ART. They are different specs, so check the variety line.

Where can I find PSA 10 OP13-120 listings checked by OPChase?

Who illustrated OP13-120 Sabo?

Limitless TCG credits Hashimoto Q for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
