FAQ

How many versions of OP12-093 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP12-093 Morley: 3 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Release Event, 1 Reprint, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP12-093 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).
