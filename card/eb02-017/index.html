FAQ

How many versions of EB02-017 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of EB02-017 Nami: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB02-017 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Who illustrated EB02-017 Nami?

Limitless TCG credits Joji Yamada for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
