P-073 Sabo first appeared in English in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, as the Reprint version. Our database lists 4 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 reprint, 1 foil (reprint set). 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 3-; Promo · Tin Pack Set Vol. 2). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Betty OP05-002 (23 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of P-073 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of P-073 Sabo: 2 Promo, 1 Reprint, 1 Foil (reprint set). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Who illustrated P-073 Sabo?

Limitless TCG credits Hatori Kyoka for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
