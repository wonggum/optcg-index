Gameplay

How it plays

A purple 9-cost Luffy that can return DON!! on entry to draw a card and gain Rush. It is a common finisher in purple Luffy decks, mainly OP11-040 and OP05-060.

Collector & player notes

Versions

Luffy OP09-119 has a base SEC, an Alternate Art, a Manga (PSA: MANGA ALTERNATE ART) and an SP inserted in OP13 (PSA files it under the OP13 set line as #119 SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART) TCGGRKB.

Trap

The base SEC and the Manga are separate PSA specs, so a listing or sale of one says nothing about the price of the other. Always match the variety line GVB.

FAQ

How many versions of OP09-119 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP09-119 Monkey.D.Luffy: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga, 1 SP, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-119 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Where can I find PSA 10 OP09-119 listings checked by OPChase?

Who illustrated OP09-119 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Tacchan for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
