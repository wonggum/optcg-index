FAQ

How many versions of EB02-061 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of EB02-061 Monkey.D.Luffy: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga, 1 SP, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB02-061 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Which EB02-061 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Manga from Extra Booster: Anime 25th Collection has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($9,867, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many EB02-061 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 4,596 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB02-061 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Midori Matsuda for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
