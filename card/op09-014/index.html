Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP09-014 Limejuice first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World: 2nd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP09-ANN), released Dec 6, 2024, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 6 English printings across 4 products: 2 reprint, 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 foil (reprint set). The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 10 months after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 37 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 3 leaders listed on its card page, most often Shanks OP09-001 (111 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP09-014 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP09-014 Limejuice: 2 Reprint, 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Foil (reprint set). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-014 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).
