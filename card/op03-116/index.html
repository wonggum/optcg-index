FAQ

How many versions of OP03-116 are there?

Our database lists 13 English printings of OP03-116 Shirahoshi: 4 Promo, 2 Normal, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Reprint, 1 Release Event. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP03-116 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Is the PRB-01 OP03-116 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 OP03-116 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP03-116 version meets that today.

How many OP03-116 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2023-12-06) shows 300 PSA 10 copies across the 6 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP03-116 Shirahoshi?

Limitless TCG credits nuisuke for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
