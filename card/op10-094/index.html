Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP10-094 Ryuma first appeared in English in Royal Blood Release Event Cards (OP10-RE), released Mar 14, 2025, as the Release Event version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 release event.
