FAQ

How many versions of OP04-112 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP04-112 Yamato: 2 Parallel (AA), 2 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal, 1 Reprint, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP04-112 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the OP04-112 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Is the PRB-01 OP04-112 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP04 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP04 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).

How many OP04-112 PSA 10s exist?

Who illustrated OP04-112 Yamato?

Limitless TCG credits Kaito Shibano for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
