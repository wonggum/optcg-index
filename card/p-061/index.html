FAQ

How many versions of P-061 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of P-061 Monkey.D.Luffy: 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-061 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).
