FAQ

How many versions of ST13-004 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of ST13-004 Edward.Newgate: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST13-004 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 ST13-004 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST13-004 version meets that today.

How many ST13-004 PSA 10s exist?

Who illustrated ST13-004 Edward.Newgate?
