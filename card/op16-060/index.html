FAQ

How many versions of OP16-060 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP16-060 Sengoku: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP16-060 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Who illustrated OP16-060 Sengoku?

Limitless TCG credits nishinoshima for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
