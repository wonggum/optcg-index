FAQ

How many versions of ST03-008 are there?

Our database lists 19 English printings of ST03-008 Trafalgar Law: 5 Promo, 5 Finalist / Top cut, 3 Winner / Top prize, 2 Reprint, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST03-008 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Is the PRB-01 ST03-008 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check.
