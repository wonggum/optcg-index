FAQ

How many versions of ST03-008 are there?

Our database lists 19 English printings of ST03-008 Trafalgar Law: 5 Promo, 5 Finalist / Top cut, 3 Winner / Top prize, 2 Reprint, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST03-008 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Is the PRB-01 ST03-008 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

What is a PSA 10 ST03-008 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST03-008 version meets that today.

How many ST03-008 PSA 10s exist?
