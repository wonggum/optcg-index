Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP12-028 Kouzuki Hiyori first appeared in English in Legacy of the Master (OP12), released Aug 22, 2025. Our database lists 6 English printings across 4 products: 3 standard, 2 promo, 1 winner / top prize. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in ST-32: Starter Deck 32 GREEN Roronoa Zoro (ST32), released Jul 31, 2026, 11 months after the first. 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Tournament Pack 2026 Vol. 1; Winner / Top prize · Winner Pack 2026 Vol. 1; Promo · CS 26-27 Celebration Pack). On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Zoro OP12-020 (129 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP12-028 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP12-028 Kouzuki Hiyori: 3 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP12-028 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP12-028 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP12-028 version meets that today.

Who illustrated OP12-028 Kouzuki Hiyori?
