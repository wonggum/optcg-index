Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-085 Nefeltari Cobra first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 2 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Lucci OP07-079 (7 decklists).
