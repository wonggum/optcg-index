Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP08-062 Charlotte Katakuri first appeared in English in Two Legends Pre-Release Cards (OP08-PRE), released Sep 6, 2024, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 promo, 1 winner / top prize. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Two Legends (OP08), released Sep 13, 2024, 7 days after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Tournament Pack 2025 Vol. 3; Winner / Top prize · Winner Pack 2025 Vol. 3). On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 2 leaders listed on its card page, most often Katakuri OP11-062 (11 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP08-062 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP08-062 Charlotte Katakuri: 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP08-062 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP08-062 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP08-062 version meets that today.

How many OP08-062 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-05-29) shows 176 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP08-062 Charlotte Katakuri?

Limitless TCG credits BISAI for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
