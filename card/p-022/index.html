Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

P-022 Monkey.D.Luffy first appeared in English in One Piece Demo Deck Cards (OP-DD), released Dec 5, 2024. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 standard. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · One Piece Film Red; Promo · Premium Card Collection -ONE PIECE FILM RED Edition-). Every printing carries the PR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 2,068 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 5 leaders listed on its card page, most often Whitebeard OP02-001 (186 decklists).
