Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-077 Gamma Knife first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 22 months after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 5 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Normal (OP05) with 2. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 9 leaders listed on its card page, most often Enel OP15-058 (199 decklists).
