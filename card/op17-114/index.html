FAQ

How many versions of OP17-114 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP17-114 Sweet 3 Generals: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP17-114 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP17-114 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP17-114 version meets that today.

Who illustrated OP17-114 Sweet 3 Generals?

Limitless TCG credits Koushi Rokushiro for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
