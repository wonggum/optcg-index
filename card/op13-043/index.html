Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP13-043 Otama first appeared in English in Carrying On His Will (OP13), released Nov 7, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 standard. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Welcome Pack 2026 Vol.1; Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 6-). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 17 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Welcome Pack 2026 Vol.1 (OP-PR) with 13. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Ace OP13-002 (186 decklists).
