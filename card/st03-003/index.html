ST03-003 Crocodile first appeared in English in ST-03: Starter Deck 3 The Seven Warlords of The Sea (Super Pre-Release Edition) (ST03-PRE), released Sep 30, 2022, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 6 English printings across 4 products: 2 standard, 2 promo, 1 pre-release, 1 winner / top prize. Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data.

FAQ

How many versions of ST03-003 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of ST03-003 Crocodile: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST03-003 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).
