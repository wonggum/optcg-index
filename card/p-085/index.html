FAQ

How many versions of P-085 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of P-085 Jewelry Bonney: 3 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Reprint, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-085 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 P-085 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-085 version meets that today.

How many P-085 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-25) shows 344 PSA 10 copies across the 6 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-085 Jewelry Bonney?

Limitless TCG credits Naruse Uroko for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
