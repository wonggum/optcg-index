FAQ

How many versions of OP03-099 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP03-099 Charlotte Katakuri: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP03-099 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
