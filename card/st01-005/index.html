Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST01-005 Jinbe first appeared in English in ST-01: Starter Deck 1 Straw Hat Crew (Super Pre-Release Edition) (ST01-PRE), released Sep 30, 2022, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 6 English printings across 5 products: 3 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in One Piece Demo Deck Cards (OP-DD), released Dec 5, 2024, about 2.2 years after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Alternate Art (AA); Promo · Gift Collection 2023). Printed rarities: C (5), PR (1). 2 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 787 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Gift Collection 2023 (OP-PR) with 485.

FAQ

How many versions of ST01-005 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of ST01-005 Jinbe: 3 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST01-005 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
