FAQ

How many versions of OP07-021 are there?

Our database lists 11 English printings of OP07-021 Urouge: 2 Reprint, 2 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 SP, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP07-021 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the OP07-021 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP07-021 worth?

How many OP07-021 PSA 10s exist?

Who illustrated OP07-021 Urouge?
