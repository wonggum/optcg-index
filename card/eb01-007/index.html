FAQ

How many versions of EB01-007 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of EB01-007 Yamato: 1 Normal, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 EB01-007 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no EB01-007 version meets that today.

How many EB01-007 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-06-04) shows 1,003 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB01-007 Yamato?

Limitless TCG credits Koushi Rokushiro for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
