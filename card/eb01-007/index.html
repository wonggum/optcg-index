Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

EB01-007 Yamato first appeared in English in Extra Booster: Memorial Collection (EB01), released May 3, 2024. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 promo. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 4-). Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1,003 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 4- (OP-PR) with 1,001. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Oden EB01-001 (1 decklists).
