Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

Our database lists 4 English printings across 2 products: 2 promo, 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel). 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · English Version 3rd Anniversary Set; Promo · Sound Loader Ace and Sabo Edition 2026). FAQ

How many versions of OP13-002 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP13-002 Portgas.D.Ace: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP13-002 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP13-002 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP13-002 version meets that today.

How many OP13-002 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-15) shows 1,402 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP13-002 Portgas.D.Ace?

Limitless TCG credits Bahi JD for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
