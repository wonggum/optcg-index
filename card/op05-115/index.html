Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-115 Two-Hundred Million Volts Amaru first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 5 English printings across 2 products: 2 foil (reprint set), 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP05-115_r1 (in -ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST- [PRB-01]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 11 months after the first. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 7 leaders listed on its card page, most often Katakuri OP03-099 (144 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-115 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP05-115 Two-Hundred Million Volts Amaru: 2 Foil (reprint set), 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-115 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP05-115 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP05 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it.
