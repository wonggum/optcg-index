Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number printed on the card: OP05-113.
  • PSA labels show only the digits of the number (#113) and a shortened set name, so match the variety line too.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.

OP05-113 Yama first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. The most recent dated printing is the Normal · Luffy Deck version in Set Sail Deck Set (SD01), released Sep 18, 2026, about 2.8 years after the first. Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity.
