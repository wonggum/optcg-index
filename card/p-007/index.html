FAQ

How many versions of P-007 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of P-007 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-007 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Which P-007 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Promo · CS 2023 Celebration Pack from One Piece Promotion Cards has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($439, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many P-007 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-26) shows 1,104 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-007 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Misa Tsutsui for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
