Collector & player notes

Why collectors care

Eustass"Captain"Kid is one of the three OP05 Super Parallel (manga-art) cards OFFTCG. A PRB-01 reprint of the Manga exists without the original textured foil OAPIRED.

Seen in decklists (Limitless TCG)

Leaders of the recent Limitless TCG decklists that include this card: Luffy OP05-060 (174), Law ST10-001 (173), Katakuri OP11-062 (2), Foxy OP07-059 (1). Counts are the number of listed decklists, as shown on the card's Limitless page.

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-074 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of OP05-074 Eustass"Captain"Kid: 2 Parallel (AA), 2 Manga, 1 Normal, 1 SP, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-074 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP05-074 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

Which OP05-074 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Manga from Awakening of the New Era has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($1,169, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP05-074 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 3,203 PSA 10 copies across the 7 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-074 Eustass"Captain"Kid?

Limitless TCG credits tatsuya for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
