Our database lists 10 English printings across 6 products: 2 promo, 2 winner / top prize, 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 wanted poster, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 signature, 1 anniversary tournament. New printings came out in 2022, 2023 and 2025. 4 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Tournament Pack Vol. 5; Winner / Top prize · Winner Pack Vol. 5; Promo · CS 2024 Celebration Pack; …). Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 10 of 10 printings match a PSA label in our data. Collector & player notes

Versions in OP05

The OP05 booster includes a 1st Anniversary ST01-012 Luffy with an original Eiichiro Oda illustration OFF. It exists in two versions in this product: the 1st Anniversary parallel and a Gold-Stamped Signature version TCG. The two are separate PSA specs, so check the label for the signature version KB.

Traps

ST01-012 has 15+ PSA specs in English, including the original ST01 starter deck, the OP03 Wanted, Super Pre-Release, Tournament Pack and others. Always compare the set line and variety KBGVB. Note: a 2024 SNKRDUNK article claims the signature version was not released in English, but TCGplayer's English catalog and PSA's English population list it.

Seen in decklists (Limitless TCG)

Leaders of the recent Limitless TCG decklists that include this card: Whitebeard OP02-001 (414), Law OP01-002 (179), Zoro OP01-001 (131), Luffy ST01-001 (58), Shanks OP09-001 (13), Luffy ST10-002 (8). Counts are the number of listed decklists, as shown on the card's Limitless page.

FAQ

How many versions of ST01-012 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of ST01-012 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Promo, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Wanted, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Signature, 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST01-012 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the ST01-012 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).
