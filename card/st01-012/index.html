Collector & player notes

Why collectors chase it

The OP05 booster includes a 1st Anniversary ST01-012 Luffy with an original Eiichiro Oda illustration OFF. It exists in two versions in this product: the 1st Anniversary parallel and a Gold-Stamped Signature version TCG. In PSA 10 the Signature trades at roughly 80× the non-signature version, with a population about a quarter as large (1,458 vs 6,407 PSA 10) KBGRPC.

Traps

ST01-012 has 15+ PSA specs in English, including the original ST01 starter deck, the OP03 Wanted, Super Pre-Release, Tournament Pack and others. Always compare the set line and variety KBGVB. Note: a 2024 SNKRDUNK article claims the signature version was not released in English, but TCGplayer's English catalog and PSA's English population list it.

Seen in decklists (Limitless TCG)

Leaders of the recent Limitless TCG decklists that include this card: Whitebeard OP02-001 (414), Law OP01-002 (179), Zoro OP01-001 (131), Luffy ST01-001 (58), Shanks OP09-001 (13), Luffy ST10-002 (8). Counts are the number of listed decklists, as shown on the card's Limitless page.

FAQ

How many versions of ST01-012 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of ST01-012 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Promo, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Wanted, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Signature, 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST01-012 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the ST01-012 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Which ST01-012 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Signature · Gold-Stamped Signature from Awakening of the New Era has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($16,648, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many ST01-012 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-25) shows 17,766 PSA 10 copies across the 9 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.
