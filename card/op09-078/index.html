Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP09-078 Gum-Gum Giant first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World (OP09), released Dec 13, 2024. FAQ

How many versions of OP09-078 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP09-078 Gum-Gum Giant: 2 Reprint, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-078 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
