How many versions of OP06-052 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP06-052 Tokikake: 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP06-052 worth?
