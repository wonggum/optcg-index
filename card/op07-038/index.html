FAQ

How many versions of OP07-038 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP07-038 Boa Hancock: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP07-038 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
