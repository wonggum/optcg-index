FAQ

How many versions of OP13-033 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP13-033 Franky: 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP13-033 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP13-033 version meets that today.

How many OP13-033 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-24) shows 2 PSA 10 copies across the 1 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP13-033 Franky?
