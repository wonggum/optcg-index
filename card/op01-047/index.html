Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP01-047 Trafalgar Law first appeared in English in Romance Dawn (OP01), released Dec 2, 2022. Our database lists 5 English printings across 3 products: 2 alternate art (parallel), 1 standard, 1 SP, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint · Official list entry OP01-047_r1 (in -ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST- [PRB-01]); not in the TCGplayer catalog version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 23 months after the first. FAQ

How many versions of OP01-047 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP01-047 Trafalgar Law: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 SP, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-047 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP01-047 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP01 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP01 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources).
