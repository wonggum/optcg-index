Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP13-052 Boa Marigold first appeared in English in Carrying On His Will: 3rd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP13-ANN), released Oct 31, 2025, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament.
