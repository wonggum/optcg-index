FAQ

How many versions of ST02-010 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of ST02-010 Basil Hawkins: 2 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST02-010 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 ST02-010 worth?
