Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-118 Kaido first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 4 English printings across 2 products: 2 alternate art (parallel), 1 standard, 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 11 months after the first. Every printing carries the SEC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 4 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1,549 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Normal (OP05) with 680. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Sakazuki OP05-041 (107 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-118 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP05-118 Kaido: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-118 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP05-118 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP05 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it.

What is a PSA 10 OP05-118 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP05-118 version meets that today.

How many OP05-118 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-25) shows 1,549 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-118 Kaido?

Limitless TCG credits SENNSU for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
