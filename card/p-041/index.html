Open questions

Where our sources disagree or can't settle a detail, we say so instead of guessing.

  • OPChase links its P-041-1A-WIN index card to this Anniversary Tournament · 1st Anniversary Tournament printing by hand (likely): TCGplayer '1st Anniversary Tournament' (no Winner tag); PSA has only a WINNER variety for #041 in that event.
  • TCGplayer lists one '1st Anniversary Tournament' P-041 product with no Winner tag. PSA (via GemRate) has only a 1ST ANNIVERSARY TOURNAMENT-WINNER variety for #041, so OPChase treats that product as the Winner card. This is likely but not confirmed from a TCGplayer listing.

Collector & player notes

Winner vs participation

P-041 Luffy has Online and Offline Regionals 2024 Vol.2 versions in participant, Finalist and Winner forms, all separate PSA specs with very different populations (for example Online: 188 / 68 / 9 PSA 10) TCGGRKB. Winner versions usually trade at 3–10× participation KB.

Open question: 1st Anniversary Winner

TCGplayer lists one '1st Anniversary Tournament' P-041 product with no Winner tag. PSA (via GemRate) has only a 1ST ANNIVERSARY TOURNAMENT-WINNER variety for #041, so OPChase treats that product as the Winner card. This is likely but not confirmed from a TCGplayer listing TCGGR.

FAQ

How many versions of P-041 are there?

Our database lists 11 English printings of P-041 Monkey.D.Luffy: 5 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Anniversary Tournament, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-041 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Which P-041 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Anniversary Tournament · 1st Anniversary Tournament from One Piece Promotion Cards has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($2,093, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many P-041 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 3,492 PSA 10 copies across the 8 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-041 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits K Akagishi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
