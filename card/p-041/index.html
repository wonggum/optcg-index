Collector & player notes

Winner vs participation

P-041 Luffy has Online and Offline Regionals 2024 Vol.2 versions in participant, Finalist and Winner forms, all separate PSA specs, so match the variety line TCGGRKB.

1st Anniversary Winner (confirmed 2026-10-02)

TCGplayer lists one '1st Anniversary Tournament' P-041 product with no Winner tag in its name, but its product image shows the 1st Anniversary WINNER stamp, and PSA's population report has only a 1ST ANNIVERSARY TOURNAMENT-WINNER variety for #041. A PSA slab listed on eBay under the exact TCGplayer name shows that label on the WINNER-stamped card, so OPChase links the two TCGGR[EBAY].

FAQ

How many versions of P-041 are there?

Our database lists 11 English printings of P-041 Monkey.D.Luffy: 5 Promo, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Anniversary Tournament, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-041 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Where can I find PSA 10 P-041 listings checked by OPChase?

Who illustrated P-041 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits K Akagishi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
