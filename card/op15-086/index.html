FAQ

How many versions of OP15-086 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP15-086 Nami: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP15-086 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP15-086 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP15-086 version meets that today.

How many OP15-086 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-26) shows 2,200 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP15-086 Nami?

Limitless TCG credits SHIE NANAHARA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
