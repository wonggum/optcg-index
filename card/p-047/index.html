FAQ

How many versions of P-047 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of P-047 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 P-047 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-047 version meets that today.

How many P-047 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 1,065 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-047 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Studio Vigor Co. Ltd for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
