Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-076 When You're at Sea You Fight against Pirates!! first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 5 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 promo, 1 winner / top prize, 1 foil (reprint set). The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in ST-18: Starter Deck 18 PURPLE Monkey.D.Luffy (ST18), released Oct 25, 2024, 11 months after the first. 3 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · 2025 Pirates League Store Qualifier; Winner / Top prize · 2025 Pirates League Store Qualifier Winner; Foil (reprint set) · 2025 Pirates League Finals Textured Foil). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 5 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 68 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Normal (OP05) with 37. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 5 leaders listed on its card page, most often Luffy EB02-010 (199 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-076 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP05-076 When You're at Sea You Fight against Pirates!!: 2 Normal, 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Foil (reprint set). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP05-076 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.
