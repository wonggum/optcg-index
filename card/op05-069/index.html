Collector & player notes

Why collectors care

Trafalgar Law is one of the three OP05 Super Parallel (manga-art) cards, the 'three captains' OFFTCG. A PRB-01 reprint of the Manga exists without the original textured foil OAPIRED.

Seen in decklists (Limitless TCG)

Leaders of the recent Limitless TCG decklists that include this card: Rosinante OP12-061 (12), Law ST10-001 (2). Counts are the number of listed decklists, as shown on the card's Limitless page.

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-069 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP05-069 Trafalgar Law: 2 Manga, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-069 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP05-069 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP05 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. TCGplayer notes that the PRB-01 Manga was reprinted without the original textured foil.

Which OP05-069 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Manga from Awakening of the New Era has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($2,150, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP05-069 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 2,217 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-069 Trafalgar Law?

Limitless TCG credits Minato Sashima for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
