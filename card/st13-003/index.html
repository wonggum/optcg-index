Collector & player notes

Many versions

ST13-003 Luffy has the Ultra Deck base and parallel, a Leader Collection premium version, a BvB collab promo with a very large population and a 2nd Anniversary Tournament version. The Winner and participation copies of the 2nd Anniversary card are different PSA specs with very different prices TCGKBGRGVB.

2nd Anniversary Winner (confirmed 2026-10-02)

TCGplayer lists one '2nd Anniversary Tournament' ST13-003 product. PSA's population report has only a 2ND ANNIVERSARY TOURNAMENT-WINNER variety for #003 Luffy in that event, and a PSA-labelled slab photo on eBay shows the same stamped card as the TCGplayer image. A separate PSA label '#P 2ND ANNIVERSARY TOURNAMENT' is a different, event-only Leader card numbered P, not this one TCGGR[EBAY].

FAQ

How many versions of ST13-003 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of ST13-003 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Anniversary Tournament. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST13-003 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Where can I find PSA 10 ST13-003 listings checked by OPChase?

OPChase lists live eBay listings checked for the exact version, English language and PSA 10 grade for: Anniversary Tournament · 2nd Anniversary Tournament (OP-PR). Open a version's OPChase page to see them. OPChase does not estimate prices; for reference, each version links to PriceCharting.

Who illustrated ST13-003 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Hokuyuu for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
