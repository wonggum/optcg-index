OP05-093 Rob Lucci first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 SP, 1 promo. FAQ

How many versions of OP05-093 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP05-093 Rob Lucci: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 SP, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP05-093 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Who illustrated OP05-093 Rob Lucci?

Limitless TCG credits otton for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
