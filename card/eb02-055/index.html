Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

EB02-055 Jinbe first appeared in English in Extra Booster: Anime 25th Collection (EB02), released May 9, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 promo, 1 winner / top prize. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Tournament Pack 2025 Vol. 3; Winner / Top prize · Winner Pack 2025 Vol. 3). Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 166 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Winner / Top prize · Winner Pack 2025 Vol. 3 (OP-PR) with 164. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Arlong OP03-022 (1 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of EB02-055 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of EB02-055 Jinbe: 1 Normal, 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the EB02-055 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 EB02-055 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no EB02-055 version meets that today.

How many EB02-055 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-10) shows 166 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB02-055 Jinbe?

Limitless TCG credits Makoto Muroi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
