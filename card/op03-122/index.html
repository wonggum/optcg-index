FAQ

How many versions of OP03-122 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP03-122 Sogeking: 2 Manga, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP03-122 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Is the PRB-01 OP03-122 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP03 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it. PRB-01 copies of OP03 alternate-art and SEC cards also carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left that the originals lack (community sources). TCGplayer notes that the PRB-01 Manga was reprinted without the original textured foil.

Which OP03-122 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Manga from Premium Booster -The Best- has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($1,269, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.
