OP04-103 Kouzuki Hiyori first appeared in English in Kingdoms of Intrigue Pre-Release Cards (OP04-PRE), released Sep 15, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 2 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release.
