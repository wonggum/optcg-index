Diagram by OPChase from PSA label names in our data. Not a photo of a card or label.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number printed on the card: OP05-036.
  • PSA labels show only the digits of the number (#036) and a shortened set name, so match the variety line too.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.

Printing history

In release order, from our card database (Bandai official English card list, PSA label names)

OP05-036 Monet first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 22 months after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity.
