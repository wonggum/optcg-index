Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST11-004 New Genesis first appeared in English in ST-11: Starter Deck 11 Uta (ST11), released Feb 2, 2024. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in ST-16: Starter Deck 16 GREEN Uta (ST16), released Oct 25, 2024, 9 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Starter Deck 11: Uta Deck Battle). Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 30 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Normal (ST11) with 29. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Uta ST11-001 (41 decklists).
