FAQ

How many versions of P-052 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of P-052 Dracule Mihawk: 2 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 P-052 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-052 version meets that today.

How many P-052 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2025-03-12) shows 190 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated P-052 Dracule Mihawk?

Limitless TCG credits ASAKI KURODA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
