FAQ

How many versions of OP07-064 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP07-064 Sanji: 4 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP07-064 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP07-064 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP07-064 version meets that today.

How many OP07-064 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-17) shows 636 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP07-064 Sanji?
