Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP10-095 Roronoa Zoro first appeared in English in Royal Blood (OP10), released Mar 21, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 reprint. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in Premium Booster -The Best- Vol. 2 (PRB02), released Oct 3, 2025, 6 months after the first. Every printing carries the R rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 309 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (PRB02) with 281. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 2 leaders listed on its card page, most often Usopp OP10-042 (25 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP10-095 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP10-095 Roronoa Zoro: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Reprint. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP10-095 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP10-095 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP10-095 version meets that today.

How many OP10-095 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 309 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP10-095 Roronoa Zoro?

Limitless TCG credits Matsumoto Akira for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
