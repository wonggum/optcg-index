Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP04-087 Trafalgar Law first appeared in English in Kingdoms of Intrigue Pre-Release Cards (OP04-PRE), released Sep 15, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04), released Sep 22, 2023, 7 days after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Welcome Pack Vol. 1). Every printing carries the C rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 15 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Welcome Pack Vol. 1 (OP-PR) with 12.
