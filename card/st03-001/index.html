Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST03-001 Crocodile first appeared in English in ST-03: Starter Deck 3 The Seven Warlords of The Sea (Super Pre-Release Edition) (ST03-PRE), released Sep 30, 2022, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 4 English printings across 4 products: 2 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Revision Pack Cards (OP-RP), released Nov 26, 2024, about 2.2 years after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Leader Collection-). Every printing carries the L rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 883 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Pre-Release (ST03-PRE) with 464. On Limitless TCG, Crocodile ST03-001 decks rank #8 with 2.52% of tracked points in the OP04 format.
