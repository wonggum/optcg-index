FAQ

How many versions of OP09-093 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP09-093 Marshall.D.Teach: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga, 1 Wanted, 1 SP Silver, 1 SP Gold, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-093 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Which OP09-093 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the SP Gold from Legacy of the Master has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($3,162, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP09-093 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 6,150 PSA 10 copies across the 8 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP09-093 Marshall.D.Teach?

Limitless TCG credits ASAKI KURODA for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
