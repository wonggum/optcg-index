OP11-060 Let's Crash This Wedding!!! first appeared in English in A Fist of Divine Speed (OP11), released Jun 6, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal · Nami Deck version in Learn Together Deck Set (LT01), released Oct 3, 2025, 4 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 5-).
