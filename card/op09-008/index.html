Printing history

OP09-008 Building Snake first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World: 2nd Anniversary Tournament Cards (OP09-ANN), released Dec 6, 2024, as the Anniversary Tournament version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Emperors in the New World (OP09), released Dec 13, 2024, 7 days after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Premium Card Collection -Best Selection Vol. 4-). Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Shanks OP09-001 (36 decklists).
