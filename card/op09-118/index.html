FAQ

How many versions of OP09-118 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP09-118 Gol.D.Roger: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga, 1 Wanted. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-118 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
