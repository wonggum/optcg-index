FAQ

How many versions of EB04-023 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of EB04-023 Chaka & Pell: 1 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the EB04-023 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Who illustrated EB04-023 Chaka & Pell?

Limitless TCG credits Morechand for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
