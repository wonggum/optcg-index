FAQ

How many versions of OP12-063 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP12-063 Vinsmoke Reiju: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP12-063 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

How many OP12-063 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-25) shows 491 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP12-063 Vinsmoke Reiju?

Limitless TCG credits Yosuke Adachi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
