OP09-004 Shanks first appeared in English in Emperors in the New World (OP09), released Dec 13, 2024. Our database lists 9 English printings across 4 products: 2 promo, 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel), 1 manga, 1 wanted poster, 1 reprint, 1 sp silver, 1 sp gold. The most recent dated printing is the SP Gold version in Carrying On His Will (OP13), released Nov 7, 2025, 11 months after the first. 2 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · English Version 2nd Anniversary Set; Promo · Championship 25-26 Offline Regionals Season 2). Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 8 of 9 printings match a PSA label in our data. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Shanks OP09-001 (146 decklists).

Shanks OP09-004 has a Manga and a Wanted Poster in OP09, plus 3rd Anniversary SP Gold and Silver versions in OP13. Gold and Silver are separate PSA specs TCGGR.

FAQ

How many versions of OP09-004 are there?

Our database lists 9 English printings of OP09-004 Shanks: 2 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Manga, 1 Wanted, 1 Reprint, 1 SP Silver, 1 SP Gold. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP09-004 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Where can I find PSA 10 OP09-004 listings checked by OPChase?

OPChase lists live eBay listings checked for the exact version, English language and PSA 10 grade for: Manga (OP09); Wanted (OP09); SP Gold (OP13). Open a version's OPChase page to see them. OPChase does not estimate prices; for reference, each version links to PriceCharting.

Who illustrated OP09-004 Shanks?

Limitless TCG credits POKImari for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
