FAQ

How many versions of ST10-013 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of ST10-013 Eustass"Captain"Kid: 2 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal, 1 Winner / Top prize, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST10-013 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 ST10-013 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST10-013 version meets that today.

How many ST10-013 PSA 10s exist?
