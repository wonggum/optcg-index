FAQ

How many versions of P-103 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of P-103 Portgas.D.Ace: 3 Promo, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-103 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

Who illustrated P-103 Portgas.D.Ace?

Limitless TCG credits Ryuda for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
