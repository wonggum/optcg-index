FAQ

How many versions of ST02-007 are there?

Our database lists 9 English printings of ST02-007 Jewelry Bonney: 2 Normal, 2 Promo, 1 Pre-Release, 1 SP, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Reprint, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST02-007 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 ST02-007 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST02-007 version meets that today.

How many ST02-007 PSA 10s exist?
