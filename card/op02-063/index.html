Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP02-063 Mr.1 (Daz.Bonez) first appeared in English in Paramount War Pre-Release Cards (OP02-PRE), released Mar 3, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 8 English printings across 3 products: 2 finalist / top cut, 2 promo, 2 winner / top prize, 1 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Paramount War (OP02), released Mar 10, 2023, 7 days after the first. 6 printings have no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Finalist / Top cut · Offline Regional 2024 Vol. 3; Promo · Online Regional 2024 Vol. 3; Finalist / Top cut · Online Regional 2024 Vol. 3; …). FAQ

How many versions of OP02-063 are there?

Our database lists 8 English printings of OP02-063 Mr.1 (Daz.Bonez): 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Promo, 2 Winner / Top prize, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP02-063 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 OP02-063 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP02-063 version meets that today.

How many OP02-063 PSA 10s exist?

Who illustrated OP02-063 Mr.1 (Daz.Bonez)?
