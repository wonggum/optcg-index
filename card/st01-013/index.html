FAQ

How many versions of ST01-013 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of ST01-013 Roronoa Zoro: 4 Promo, 1 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the ST01-013 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.
