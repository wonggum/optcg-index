Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP05-073 Miss Doublefinger(Zala) first appeared in English in Awakening of the New Era (OP05), released Dec 8, 2023. Our database lists 6 English printings across 4 products: 1 standard, 1 anniversary tournament, 1 reprint, 1 foil (reprint set), 1 full art, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Full Art version in Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01), released Nov 8, 2024, 11 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 3). Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 5 of 6 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 50 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 3 (OP-PR) with 25. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Luffy OP05-060 (68 decklists).

FAQ

How many versions of OP05-073 are there?

Our database lists 6 English printings of OP05-073 Miss Doublefinger(Zala): 1 Normal, 1 Anniversary Tournament, 1 Reprint, 1 Foil (reprint set), 1 Full Art, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the PRB-01 OP05-073 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so the set line on the label is the reliable check. On the card, community sources say the original OP05 print has a pen icon next to the illustrator name and PRB-01 reprints lack it.

How many OP05-073 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-07-18) shows 50 PSA 10 copies across the 5 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP05-073 Miss Doublefinger(Zala)?

Limitless TCG credits Hashimoto Q for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
