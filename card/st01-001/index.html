FAQ

How many versions of ST01-001 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of ST01-001 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Serial. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Which ST01-001 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Pre-Release from ST-01: Starter Deck 1 Straw Hat Crew (Super Pre-Release Edition) has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($1,950, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.
