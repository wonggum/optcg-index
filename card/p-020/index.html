FAQ

How many versions of P-020 are there?

Our database lists 1 English printing of P-020 Helmeppo: 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 P-020 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no P-020 version meets that today.
