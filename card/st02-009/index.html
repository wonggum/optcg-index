Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

ST02-009 Trafalgar Law first appeared in English in ST-02: Starter Deck 2 Worst Generation (Super Pre-Release Edition) (ST02-PRE), released Sep 30, 2022, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in Revision Pack Cards (OP-RP), released Nov 26, 2024, about 2.2 years after the first. Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 432 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Pre-Release (ST02-PRE) with 405. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 4 leaders listed on its card page, most often Law OP01-002 (260 decklists).
