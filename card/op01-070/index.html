FAQ

How many versions of OP01-070 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP01-070 Dracule Mihawk: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal, 1 Reprint, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-070 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

Is the PRB-01 OP01-070 the same as the original?

No. One Piece The Best (PRB-01) reprints are separate printings: PSA files them under the PRB01 set line. Community sources say they carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name. PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil.

Which OP01-070 version is the most valuable?

Of the versions OPChase tracks with enough sales, the Alternate Art (AA) from Romance Dawn has the highest PSA 10 anchor ($346, from the last same-spec sales). Other versions show 'Price data insufficient' when there are fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales.

How many OP01-070 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-23) shows 836 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP01-070 Dracule Mihawk?

Limitless TCG credits Nijihayashi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
