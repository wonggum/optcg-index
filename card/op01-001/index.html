Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP01-001 Roronoa Zoro first appeared in English in Romance Dawn (OP01), released Dec 2, 2022. Our database lists 4 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 2 alternate art (parallel). The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in One Piece Demo Deck Cards (OP-DD), released Dec 5, 2024, about 2 years after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Alternate Art (AA)). Printed rarities: L (3), PR (1). 2 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1,688 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (OP01) with 1,557. On Limitless TCG, Roronoa Zoro OP01-001 decks rank #12 with 0.23% of tracked points in the OP13 format.

FAQ

How many versions of OP01-001 are there?

Our database lists 4 English printings of OP01-001 Roronoa Zoro: 2 Normal, 2 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-001 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.
