FAQ

How many versions of OP10-107 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of OP10-107 Jewelry Bonney: 2 Finalist / Top cut, 1 Normal, 1 Release Event, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP10-107 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP10-107 version meets that today.

How many OP10-107 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-05) shows 48 PSA 10 copies across the 4 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP10-107 Jewelry Bonney?

Limitless TCG credits kicori for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
