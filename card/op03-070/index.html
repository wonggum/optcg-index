Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP03-070 Monkey.D.Luffy first appeared in English in Pillars of Strength (OP03), released Jun 30, 2023. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Normal · Dash Pack version in Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04), released Sep 22, 2023, 3 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 2). Printed rarities: R (2), PR (1). 3 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 601 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · Judge Pack Vol. 2 (OP-PR) with 472. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Magellan OP02-071 (1 decklists).

How many versions of OP03-070 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP03-070 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Normal, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP03-070 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP03-070 version meets that today.
