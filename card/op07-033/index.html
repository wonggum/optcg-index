Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP07-033 Monkey.D.Luffy first appeared in English in 500 Years in the Future Pre-Release Cards (OP07-PRE), released Jun 21, 2024, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 4 English printings across 4 products: 1 standard, 1 pre-release, 1 reprint, 1 promo. The most recent dated printing is the Reprint version in ST-24: Starter Deck 24 GREEN Jewelry Bonney (ST24), released Jun 6, 2025, 11 months after the first. 1 printing has no release date in the TCGplayer catalog (Promo · 3rd Anniversary Treasure Campaign Pack). Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 3 of 4 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1,189 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Promo · 3rd Anniversary Treasure Campaign Pack (OP-PR) with 1,157. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 1 leader listed on its card page, most often Bonney OP07-019 (19 decklists).
