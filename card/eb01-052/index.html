Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

EB01-052 Viola first appeared in English in Extra Booster: Memorial Collection (EB01), released May 3, 2024. Our database lists 2 English printings across 1 product: 1 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel). Every printing carries the SR rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 2 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 214 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (EB01) with 174. FAQ

How many versions of EB01-052 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of EB01-052 Viola: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the EB01-052 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 EB01-052 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no EB01-052 version meets that today.

How many EB01-052 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-18) shows 214 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated EB01-052 Viola?

Limitless TCG credits otton for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
