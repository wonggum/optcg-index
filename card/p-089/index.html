FAQ

How many versions of P-089 are there?

Our database lists 7 English printings of P-089 Tony Tony.Chopper: 3 Promo, 2 Winner / Top prize, 2 Finalist / Top cut. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the P-089 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation). Winner versions usually trade at several times the participation price.

What is a PSA 10 P-089 worth?
