Collector & player notes

Super Leader Alternate Art on PSA (confirmed 2026-10-02)

The official card list shows three OP-17 entries for this Luffy leader (base, _p1, _p2). TCGplayer calls the two parallels Alternate Art and Super Leader Alternate Art. PSA-labelled slabs on eBay reading MANGA ALTERNATE ART show the gold/red manga-background Super Leader art, so PSA's Manga Alternate Art (the large pop) is the Super Leader version OFFGRTCG[EBAY].

Open question: plain Alternate Art on PSA

The plain Alternate Art is probably PSA's ALTERNATE ART spec (pop 1) by elimination, but no slab photo confirms it, so its PSA pop stays unassigned GRTCG.

FAQ

How many versions of OP17-079 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP17-079 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

How many OP17-079 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 135 PSA 10 copies across the 1 English version we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy?

Limitless TCG credits Bahi JD for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
