Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP11-062 Charlotte Katakuri first appeared in English in A Fist of Divine Speed (OP11), released Jun 6, 2025. Our database lists 3 English printings across 2 products: 2 standard, 1 alternate art (parallel). The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in ST-34: Starter Deck 34 PURPLE Charlotte Katakuri (ST34), released Jul 31, 2026, 14 months after the first. Every printing carries the L rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 2 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 520 PSA 10 copies in total, the most for the Alternate Art (AA) (OP11) with 514. FAQ

How many versions of OP11-062 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP11-062 Charlotte Katakuri: 2 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA). Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP11-062 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 OP11-062 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP11-062 version meets that today.

How many OP11-062 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-08-04) shows 520 PSA 10 copies across the 2 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP11-062 Charlotte Katakuri?

Limitless TCG credits Bahi JD for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
