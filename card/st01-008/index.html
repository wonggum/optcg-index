FAQ

How many versions of ST01-008 are there?

Our database lists 5 English printings of ST01-008 Nico Robin: 2 Normal, 1 Pre-Release, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the ST01-008 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety. English parallels from OP04 onward also show a star above the rarity (community source).

What is a PSA 10 ST01-008 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no ST01-008 version meets that today.

How many ST01-008 PSA 10s exist?
