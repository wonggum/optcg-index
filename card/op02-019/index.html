Printing history

In release order, from our card database (TCGplayer catalog and official card list, PSA population via GemRate, Limitless TCG)

OP02-019 Rakuyo first appeared in English in Paramount War Pre-Release Cards (OP02-PRE), released Mar 3, 2023, as the Pre-Release version. Our database lists 3 English printings across 3 products: 2 standard, 1 pre-release. The most recent dated printing is the Normal version in ST-15: Starter Deck 15 RED Edward.Newgate (ST15), released Oct 25, 2024, 20 months after the first. Every printing carries the UC rarity; the versions differ in artwork, finish or stamp, not rarity. 1 of 3 printings match a PSA label in our data; GemRate's PSA population mirror shows 1 PSA 10 copies in total. On Limitless TCG the card appears in decklists for 2 leaders listed on its card page, most often Whitebeard OP02-001 (7 decklists).
