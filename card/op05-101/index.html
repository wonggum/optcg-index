FAQ

How many versions of OP05-101 are there?

Our database lists 2 English printings of OP05-101 Ohm: 1 Normal, 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

What is a PSA 10 OP05-101 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP05-101 version meets that today.
