Collector & player notes

Two parallels, two PSA specs (settled 2026-10-02)

The official card list shows two parallel entries (_p1 and _p2, same rarity) besides the base card. TCGplayer names them Alternate Art and Parallel; PSA (via GemRate) has Alternate Art and Special Alternate Art. PSA-labelled slab photos on eBay for #080 and #083 show SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART on the TCGplayer Parallel art, so we link Parallel = Special Alternate Art and Alternate Art = Alternate Art for all five Five Elders cards OFFGRTCG[EBAY].

FAQ

How many versions of OP13-084 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP13-084 St. Shepherd Ju Peter: 2 Parallel (AA), 1 Normal. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP13-084 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

What is a PSA 10 OP13-084 worth?

Price data insufficient: OPChase only shows a PSA 10 price when there are at least 5 recent same-spec English PSA 10 sales, and no OP13-084 version meets that today.

How many OP13-084 PSA 10s exist?

GemRate's PSA population mirror (as of 2026-09-28) shows 2,001 PSA 10 copies across the 3 English versions we could match to a PSA spec. See the table for each version.

Who illustrated OP13-084 St. Shepherd Ju Peter?

Limitless TCG credits shosuke for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
