FAQ

How many versions of OP02-098 are there?

Our database lists 10 English printings of OP02-098 Koby: 4 Promo, 2 Normal, 2 Finalist / Top cut, 2 Winner / Top prize. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

Is the OP02-098 Winner card the same as the participation card?

No. Winner, Finalist and participation versions are separate cards and separate PSA specs (the PSA variety says WINNER / FINALIST / TOP prize; no suffix means participation).

Who illustrated OP02-098 Koby?

Limitless TCG credits kankurou for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
