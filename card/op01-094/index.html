FAQ

How many versions of OP01-094 are there?

Our database lists 3 English printings of OP01-094 Kaido: 1 Normal, 1 Parallel (AA), 1 Promo. Each one is a separate collectible (and a separate PSA spec when graded).

How do I tell the OP01-094 Alternate Art from the base card?

The Alternate Art (parallel) uses different artwork with a foil finish and keeps the same card number. On a PSA slab the variety line says ALTERNATE ART; the base card has no variety.

Where can I find PSA 10 OP01-094 listings checked by OPChase?

Who illustrated OP01-094 Kaido?

Limitless TCG credits Yosuke Adachi for the base printing. Parallel artworks can have different illustrators.
