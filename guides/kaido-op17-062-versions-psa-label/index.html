Kaido OP17-062: how to read the PSA label of the guest-artist version

On the English slab we checked, the guest-artist Kaido OP17-062 reads SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART; the Japanese slab we checked reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART. Read the first line, the variety and the code on the card.

By OPChase Research · Published 2026-10-26 · 4 min read

Short answer. To identify the guest-artist version of Kaido OP17-062 on an English PSA slab, check three things: the variety line (on the English slab we checked it reads SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART), the language tag at the end of the label's first line ("OP17 EN" for English), and the card number printed on the card (OP17-062; the label shows only "#062") PSA. The Japanese slab we checked carries a different variety, MANGA ALTERNATE ART. We did not find a public English MANGA PSA label, so we draw no conclusion about how an English manga-art Kaido is labelled.

The English slab we checked

For cert 175297710, the printed label and PSA's cert page read like this PSA:

Part Printed on the label Shown on the cert page (psacard.com/cert/175297710)
Year 2026 2026
Set line ONE PIECE OP17 EN ONE PIECE OP17-THE WORLD'S STRONGEST WARRIORS
Character KAIDO KAIDO
Variety SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART
Number #062 062
Grade GEM MT 10 GEM MT 10
  • The card in the slab is Kaido, with the code OP17-062 printed at the bottom right. The art is a close-up of Kaido's face with large Japanese characters across it. A small artist credit runs vertically along the card's right edge PSA.
  • The label shows "#062" only. The "OP17-" prefix is on the card, not on the label. Bandai's official English card list has Kaido as OP17-062 in BOOSTER PACK -THE WORLD'S STRONGEST WARRIORS- [OP-17] card list.
  • Do not stop at "Kaido". The same official list has other Kaido cards in OP-17 (OP17-042, the OP17-058 Leader, and OP17-063, which is also a purple SR), so check the number on the card as well as the name card list.
  • Our name for it. OPChase calls this the "guest-artist version" because the card carries a separate artist credit. That is our descriptive name. It is not PSA wording and it is not a Bandai version name.

The illustration is drawn from the label text on the English slab we checked. Orange tags mark "line 1", the "variety line" and the "#number". Below the label is the card code printed on the card itself; card artwork is not shown.

No eBay listings box for this one: we have not matched this PSA variety to one version in our listing data.

The Japanese slab we checked

Cert 178182518 is a Japanese slab. Its cert page shows a set name that starts with "JAPANESE", the subject KAIDO, and the variety MANGA ALTERNATE ART. The card in the slab has a black-and-white manga-panel background PSA.

  • This is a different PSA variety from the English slab's SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART.
  • It is a Japanese slab, so it does not tell you how an English slab of a manga-art Kaido is labelled.

What we could not check

  • English MANGA slab. We did not find a public English MANGA PSA label for OP17-062, so we do not say how PSA words it on an English slab, and we do not say which English variety belongs to which artwork beyond the slab above.
  • Other English versions. We did not check an English slab of the base card or of a plain ALTERNATE ART Kaido OP17-062. This guide says nothing about their labels.
  • Store catalog names. Stores and catalogs may call a version by their own names. Those names are not PSA wording; search by the words on the label.

Why the variety words alone are not enough

  • The same words can sit on a different card. "SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART" also appears on a Japanese OP05 #044 Kaido slab, labelled "2023 ONE PIECE OP05 JP / KAIDO / SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART / #044" (cert 105946891; the card itself is printed OP04-044). That is a different card from OP17-062 PSA.
  • So read the whole label. Compare the set line (OP17 versus OP05), the language tag (EN versus JP), the number and the variety, then check the card in the slab.
  • For the full list of fields on a label, see our guide How to read a One Piece PSA label.

How to check a slab you are looking at

  1. Read the first line of the label: year, "ONE PIECE", set code and language tag. "2026 ONE PIECE OP17 EN" is an English OP-17 card PSA.
  2. Read the name, the number and the variety line. Here: KAIDO, #062 and SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART.
  3. Look at the card in the slab. The code at its bottom right should read OP17-062.
  4. Look the cert number up at psacard.com/cert/<number>. Check that the year, set, character, number, variety and grade match the slab in your hand PSA.

Method and limits

  • How we checked:
    • Label wording comes from PSA's cert pages (psacard.com/cert/<number>), compared with the front photo of each slab on the same page. Cert 175297710 was read on 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10; certs 178182518 and 105946891 were read on 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-09.
    • Card numbers and names come from Bandai's official English card list card list, re-read on 2026-10-22.
    • All cited certs are publicly viewable. None belongs to OPChase.
  • What this guide is not: it is not a way to tell whether a slab is genuine, and it is not a grading guide. A matching label does not prove a slab or card is genuine; look the cert up on PSA's site. It does not cover grading counts or market values.
  • Known gaps:
    • One English slab of OP17-062 and one Japanese manga-art Kaido slab. That is all we checked.
    • We do not claim to list every version of this card or every way PSA words them.
    • PSA label formats change over time.

Where the facts come from

  • Card number and name: Bandai's official English card list, en.onepiece-cardgame.com/cardlist/ (OP-17 series page) card list.
  • Label fields and printed labels: PSA's cert pages, psacard.com/cert/<number> PSA. Certs cited: 105946891, 175297710, 178182518.
