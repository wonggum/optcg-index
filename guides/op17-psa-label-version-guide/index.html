OP-17 The World's Strongest Warriors: identify the version from the PSA label

How to tell which OP-17 version is in a PSA slab: line 1, the #number and the variety line, with the numbers that get confused.

By OPChase Research · Published 2026-10-27 · 6 min read

Short answer. An OP-17 PSA label tells you the version in three places: line 1 (2026 ONE PIECE OP17 EN), the #number (digits only) and the variety line. OP-17 is unusual because several of its chase cards carry numbers from other sets (EB04-061, OP13-028, ST31-004…), and three numbers (#062, #079, #118) have more than one special version. No English OP-17 variety string is cert-confirmed yet from a source we can cite, so every label wording below is marked unconfirmed.

What the OP-17 set line looks like

  • Printed label line 1 on English OP-17 slabs reads "2026 ONE PIECE OP17 EN" (unconfirmed: no English OP-17 cert from a source we can cite has been read yet).
  • The cert page shows the long set name "ONE PIECE OP17-THE WORLD'S STRONGEST WARRIORS". Both forms are normal.
  • Bandai's official OP-17 list has 169 entries, including SP cards and EB04-061 that keep their original numbers card list.

Store names such as "Super Alternate Art" or "Super Leader Alternate Art" are catalog names. The word "Super" is not a reliable guide to the PSA variety: on Kaido #062 the store's "Super Alternate Art" is the guest-art card, not the manga card, and its English PSA variety line is unconfirmed.

How to check any OP-17 slab

  1. Line 1: "2026 ONE PIECE OP17 EN" means an English card filed under the OP-17 set line. JP at the end means a Japanese card.
  2. Number: the label shows digits only (for example "#062"). The prefix (OP17-, EB04-, OP13-…) is printed on the card, bottom right. Read it through the slab.
  3. Variety line: no variety, ALTERNATE ART, MANGA ALTERNATE ART, SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART and other words are separate PSA entries. Read every word.
  4. Name: several OP-17 numbers belong to characters that appear more than once in the set. Match the name and the number together.
  5. Cert lookup: type the cert number into psacard.com/cert/ and check year, set, name, number and variety match the slab.

Method and limits

  • This is not an authenticity or grading guide. A matching label does not prove a slab is genuine.
  • It says nothing about values or grading counts.
  • PSA label formats change; we only describe the certs we cite.

Where the facts come from

  • Card numbers, names, rarities: Bandai official English card list, BOOSTER PACK -THE WORLD'S STRONGEST WARRIORS- [OP-17] (en.onepiece-cardgame.com, card list, OP-17 series), read 2026-10-27. 169 entries.
  • Label wording: PSA cert pages (psacard.com/cert/<number>) for the certs named on this page. Where no cert is named, the label wording is marked unconfirmed.
  • Version names used by stores: the TCGplayer catalog as held in the OPChase card database. Store names are not PSA wording.
