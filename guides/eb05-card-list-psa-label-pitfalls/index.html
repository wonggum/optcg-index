EB-05 Complete Card List & PSA Label Pitfalls Guide

Bandai's official card list now shows EB05-001 to EB05-061. Below is what that list contains, what it does not contain yet, and four label numbers that may end up shared by different cards.

By OPChase Research · Published 2026-10-26 · Updated 2026-10-28 · 6 min read

61 numbered cards; SP and alt-art Leaders pending

Short answer. EB-05 (Extra Booster ONE PIECE Heroines Edition vol.2; English release 2026-10-30, Japanese 2026-10-31) has 61 new-number cards, EB05-001 to EB05-061: 1 Leader, 9 SR, 1 SEC, 21 R and 29 C card list. The SP cards, the new-art Leaders that keep their old numbers, and the DON!! cards are not on the official list yet. Because those old-number cards are printed in EB-05, we expect a slab number such as #016 or #041 may belong to two or three different cards (see What we expect). Read the name line and the code on the card, not just the number.

Not on the official list yet

  • SP cards (8 reported): OP01-016 Nami, EB05-016 Nico Robin, EB05-006 Miss Buckingham Stussy, OP14-033 Perona, ST17-004 Boa Hancock, EB05-031 Vinsmoke Reiju, OP17-081 Gerd, OP17-109 Charlotte Pudding reported. The Nami and Robin SPs are reported to use visuals from the anime ONE PIECE HEROINES.
  • Returning Leaders with new art (reported): OP11-022 Shirahoshi, OP11-041 Nami, OP13-100 Jewelry Bonney, OP14-041 Boa Hancock, OP15-039 Rebecca, EB03-001 Vivi reported. These six plus EB05-010 make up the product page's "Leader 7".
  • Shirahoshi manga version (EB05-014): not officially confirmed. The SR base card is on the official list; the manga version is only reported reported.
  • DON!! cards: the product page lists 2; none are on the card list yet.

Two corrections to know about

  • Robin's Leader is EB05-010. A Bandai release-event page showed EB05-016 as the Leader. That is a misprint: on the official card list EB05-010 is the Robin Leader and EB05-016 is the Robin SR card list.
  • EB05-033 erratum. Bandai's notice of October 23, 2026 says the card text of EB05-033 Nico Robin reads "O-Robi" where it should read "Nico Robin", and that the corrected wording is to be treated as correct. The notice says this also applies to the alt-art cards and that corrected cards are not available in some regions notice.

Four label numbers shared by different cards

Each number below belongs to one new EB05 card on the official list and to one or more cards that are reported to be printed in EB-05 with an older number. Whether PSA will put them on the same label number is covered under What we expect.

#016: Robin SR, Robin SP and Nami SP

  • EB05-016 Nico Robin SR (official) card list.
  • EB05-016 Nico Robin SP (reported) reported.
  • OP01-016 Nami SP printed in EB-05 (reported) reported.

Two of the three are Nico Robin cards. Nami OP01-016 also has an earlier SP from OP-05, so "OP01-016 Nami SP" in a title can mean either one; check which set the listing says it came from.

#033: Perona SP and Robin R

  • OP14-033 Perona SP printed in EB-05 (reported) reported.
  • EB05-033 Nico Robin R (official; the card covered by the October 23 erratum) card list.

A regular R and an SP may share "#033". Check the name line first: PERONA and NICO ROBIN are different characters.

#039: Rebecca Leader and Pink Hornet

  • OP15-039 Rebecca Leader, new art (reported) reported.
  • EB05-039 Pink Hornet, an event card (official) card list.

Rebecca's OP15-039 Leader already has other versions, so check both the set line and the art.

#041: Nami Leader, Hancock Leader and Koala C

  • OP11-041 Nami Leader, new art (reported) reported.
  • OP14-041 Boa Hancock Leader, new art (reported) reported.
  • EB05-041 Koala C (official) card list.

Three characters on one number. The name line settles it.

What we expect

This section is our inference. No graded EB-05 cards have been seen yet.

  • When an older card was reprinted in a later set, PSA has filed it under the later set's line with the old number. One earlier example: the ST16-004 Shanks SP was labelled "OP11 #004".
  • If PSA does the same for EB-05, the old-number SPs and Leaders above would sit on "EB05" labels next to the new EB05 card with the same number. That is why we treat #016, #033, #039 and #041 as shared.
  • We do not know yet what variety words PSA will use for EB-05, or how it will type the EB05-033 name.

Check these 3 things before you buy

  1. The name line, not only "#number". On #016, #033, #039 and #041 the number alone does not tell you which card is in the slab.
  2. The code printed on the card. The label shows only the number; the full code (for example EB05-016 or OP01-016) is on the card's bottom right. It shows which set the card first came from.
  3. The cert page. Look the cert number up at psacard.com/cert/<number> and check that the year, set, name, number and variety match the slab and the listing title.

Method and limits

  • Official: the EB05-001 to 061 numbers, names and rarity counts, from Bandai's English and Japanese card lists, viewed 2026-10-26.
  • Reported: SP cards, new-art Leaders and the Shirahoshi manga version come from card-list trackers summarised by secondary sites. They are not on Bandai's list yet and may change.
  • Not known yet: how PSA will word EB-05 labels. No EB-05 slabs had been graded when this was written. The diagrams are generic mockups with placeholder variety text.
  • What this guide is not: it is not a way to tell whether a slab is genuine, and it covers no grading counts or market values.
