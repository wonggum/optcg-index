OP-13 Carrying On His Will: the official versions and the OP13-119 errata

What Bandai's official OP-13 list shows: the card numbers with several versions, the older-set SP cards in the set, Bandai's Super Alt-Art wording and the OP13-119 errata.

By OPChase Research · Published 2026-10-28 · Updated 2026-10-29 · 4 min read

Short answer. Bandai's official English card list for OP-13 has 175 entries under 129 card codes card list. The three Secret Rares, OP13-118 Monkey.D.Luffy, OP13-119 Portgas.D.Ace and OP13-120 Sabo, have five entries each. Five more codes have three entries, and nine cards from older sets are in the list under their original codes. Bandai changed the text of one OP13-119 version; the notice is quoted below.

The set at a glance

  • Release date: November 7, 2025. Contents: 133+1 card types product page.
  • Rarities listed by Bandai: Leader Cards, Commons, Uncommons, Rares, Super Rares, Secret Rares, Special Cards, Special 3rd Anniversary Cards product page.
  • Bandai names three special groups: a WANTED Edition ("4 newly designed WANTED Cards"), a Character Edition ("6 new Character cards are included with an exclusive design and a luxurious finish") and OP13-076 Divine Departure "as a gold foil event card" product page.

Super Alt-Art in Bandai's words

  • Super Alt-Art: "It's an Alt-Art card with a luxurious design." and "It is one of the Alt-Art cards, which has the entire card covered with designs from the "ONE PIECE" manga." Bandai illustrates it with OP13-118 Monkey.D.Luffy (Bandai OP-13 product page).
  • Bandai describes the "Red Super Alt-Art" as "An Alt-Art Card reminiscent of the theme of the Third Anniversay, "The Flame"." and "It's a new Red Super Alt-Art only for this booster pack." (Bandai OP-13 product page). Bandai illustrates it with OP13-119 Portgas.D.Ace.
  • Print run: not published.

OP13-119 Ace: the errata

Bandai's notice of October 30, 2025 covers OP13-119 Portgas.D.Ace (Wanted Poster Design) and says: "Only the Wanted Poster Version of this card is affected." (Bandai notice).

  • Before: "[On Play] If your Leader is multicolored, set up to 4 of your DON!! cards as active. Then, you cannot play Character cards with a base cost of 5 or more during this turn."
  • After (treated as correct): "[On Play] Give up to 1 rested DON!! card to your Leader. Then, you may return up to 1 of your opponent's Characters with a cost of 5 or less to the owner's hand. If you do, your opponent plays up to 1 Character card with a cost of 4 or less from their hand."

A separate Bandai notice corrects OP13-077 Go All the Way to the Top!!: "during this turn" becomes "during this battle" (Bandai notice).
