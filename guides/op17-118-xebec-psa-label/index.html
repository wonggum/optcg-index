OP17-118 Rocks.D.Xebec: base, Alternate Art and Pirate Crew on the PSA label

OP17-118 is a blue SEC Rocks.D.Xebec (cost 10) with three versions: base, parallel and a Pirate Crew super parallel.

By OPChase Research · Published 2026-10-27 · 4 min read

Short answer. OP17-118 is a blue SEC Rocks.D.Xebec (cost 10) with three versions: base, parallel and a Pirate Crew super parallel. Bandai's official OP-17 list has 3 entries for OP17-118 Rocks.D.Xebec card list. On an English slab, read line 1 (OP17 EN), the number (#118) and the variety line, then the code on the card.

What gets confused with it

  • The Xebec Leader OP17-039 is a different card (life value, no cost). It is not #118.
  • The two parallels: sellers often write "Alt Art Super Parallel" for the Pirate Crew card. The variety line, not the title, decides. Plain ALTERNATE ART and PIRATE CREW ALTERNATE ART would be separate PSA entries.
  • The Pirate Crew Luffy from the same treatment is card EB04-061 and reads #061, not #118.
