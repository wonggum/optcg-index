Luffy Promo Cards: Matching a Monkey.D.Luffy Promo to Its Official Entry

Bandai's Promotion card list (series 569901) has 39 entries named Monkey.D.Luffy, spread over 27 card numbers.

By OPChase Research · Published 2026-11-04 · 15 min read

Short answer. Bandai's Promotion card list (series 569901) has 39 entries named Monkey.D.Luffy, spread over 27 card numbers. Only one entry on that whole list names PSA in its Card Set(s): OP05-060 Version 2, "One Piece Card Game x PSA Exclusive Promo Card". Several Luffy Card Set(s) names on that list cover two entries with the same number and the same card text, so for a Luffy promo the number alone rarely settles which entry is in the slab.

This guide covers the Monkey.D.Luffy entries on the Promotion card list only. Other promo characters, and Luffy entries that appear only on other official lists, are outside this page; see "What we could not check" at the end.

The official list, by the numbers

  • Series 569901, "Promotion card": 375 entries over 241 card numbers.
  • Series 569801, "Other Product Card", is a separate official list with 182 entries. Some Luffy numbers have entries on both lists.
  • Monkey.D.Luffy on 569901: 39 entries, 27 numbers. 18 are (base) entries and 21 are Version entries.
  • Entries on 569901 with SP CARD or TR rarity: 0. Reprint entries on 569901: 0.
  • Entries on 569901 whose Card Set(s) names PSA: 1.

What counts as a version here

Only entries Bandai lists count. A number's (base) entry plus each numbered Version entry on any official list makes up its versions; a Reprint entry is a separate numbered entry on a later product's list. Words a listing adds, such as alternate art or manga, do not map to any of these entries, and we do not use them. When a listing describes a version that has no entry of its own on an official list, treat it as unknown until you match it to an entry below.

Key cards

Each key card below links to its card page on this site. The checks use product names, not version numbers, because listing titles rarely carry Bandai's Version labels.

OP05-060 Version 2 (Monkey.D.Luffy, Leader)

Official entry. OP05-060 Version 2 is a Purple Leader, rarity L, Life 5, and its Card Set(s) reads "One Piece Card Game x PSA Exclusive Promo Card". It is the only entry on the Promotion card list that names PSA.

Same number, other entries.

OP05-060 (base) L -AWAKENING OF THE NEW ERA-[OP05] BOOSTER PACK -AWAKENING OF THE NEW ERA- [OP-05] (569105)
OP05-060 Version 1 L -AWAKENING OF THE NEW ERA-[OP05] BOOSTER PACK -AWAKENING OF THE NEW ERA- [OP-05] (569105)
OP05-060 Version 3 L -Purple Monkey.D.Luffy- [ST-18] STARTER DECK -Purple Monkey.D.Luffy- [ST-18] (569018)
OP05-060 Version 4 L -Anime 25th Collection- [EB-02] EXTRA BOOSTER -Anime 25th Collection- [EB-02] (569202)

Why it matters. This Leader number has five entries across four products, each product on its own official list: the OP-05 booster list holds the (base) and Version 1, the ST-18 starter deck holds Version 3, the EB-02 Extra Booster holds Version 4, and the Promotion card list holds Version 2. Every one of the five is rarity L, Purple and Life 5, so rarity and colour give no help at all; only the product name tells them apart.

How to check the listing. The title must carry OP05-060 and words from "One Piece Card Game x PSA Exclusive Promo Card". A title with only "OP05-060 Luffy Leader" could be any of the five entries in this section. Hold the card through the slab against the product name, and ask on the listing which product the card came from when the title is silent. Card page on this site: OP05-060.

Why it matters. P-001 has seven official entries split across two lists, all rarity P. Version 1 and Version 2 share both the Card Set(s) text and the card text, so neither the list text nor the title product name can split them; the official list separates them only as two entries.

For the Super Pre-Release pair, ask on the listing which of the two entries it is and compare the card against both official entries before you buy. Card page on this site: P-001.

Why it matters. Listing traps

  1. Same event, two entries. P-001 Version 1 and 2 (Super Pre-Release), ST01-012 Version 5 and 6 (3rd Anniversary Event), P-110 (base) and Version 1 (4th Anniversary Event), and P-135 (base) and Version 1 (OP-16 Release Event) each share one Card Set(s) text on 569901. Check: ask which entry, and compare the card with both official entries.
  2. One Leader number, five entries across four products. OP05-060 has two entries from OP-05 and one each from ST-18, EB-02 and the PSA promo, all rarity L. Check: the title names the product, not just "Leader".
  3. One number, one version label, two lists. OP07-091 Version 1 appears on both 569901 and 569801 under different products. Check: Participation Pack or Finalist Card Set must be stated.
  4. "English Version" in a product name. ST10-006 Version 2's Card Set(s) is "English Version 1st Anniversary Set". Check: read the phrase as a product name and match it to that entry.
  5. SP CARD outside the promo list. No 569901 entry has SP CARD or TR rarity, yet ST01-012 Version 1 is an SP CARD on the OP-03 list. Check: an "SP" Luffy ST01-012 is the OP-03 entry, not a promo.

Where to check listings

Before you open a listing, match it to an entry on this site: the OP-PR set page covers the promo list as a whole, and each key card above links to its own card page. A listing for a Luffy promo can be any of the entries above; match it to one entry before you compare listings.
