Privacy

Last updated Oct 1, 2026

Accounts

OPChase has no user accounts and no log-in. We do not ask for your name, address or payment details.

Analytics

We do not run analytics right now. If we enable it, we will use GoatCounter, which is cookieless, does not track you across sites and only keeps aggregate statistics (page, referrer, browser type, country).

Waitlist email

If you join the OPChase Pro waitlist with your email address, we use it only to contact you about OPChase Pro. We do not sell or share it. Ask us (see Contact) and we will delete it. If you join via GitHub instead, your GitHub issue is public and handled under GitHub's own privacy statement.

eBay listing data

Listings (title, price, photo, time left, link) come from eBay's public active listings through the eBay API and are shown under eBay's API terms. Photos are loaded directly from eBay's servers. Listings are refreshed regularly; anything older than 6 hours is not shown, and ended or removed listings are deleted from our data. Each listing photo carries a small "Photo: eBay listing by <seller>" credit with the seller's public eBay username, linking to the listing; we do not show seller feedback or any other seller details. The OPChase Index is our own estimate, not an eBay price. Clicking "View on eBay" takes you to eBay, where eBay's privacy notice applies.

Affiliate links

Links to eBay are plain links today. If we join the eBay Partner Network, links to eBay may carry an affiliate tracking ID and OPChase may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; we will disclose this on every page.

Cookies & local storage

OPChase itself sets no cookies. If you switch between the light and dark theme, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage (key opc-theme) on your device only and is never sent to us.

Fonts

The Inter typeface is served from this site (self-hosted). No fonts are loaded from Google Fonts or any other third-party font service.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/wonggum/optcg-index/issues.

OPChase

OPChase is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Bandai, eBay, PSA or PriceCharting.
