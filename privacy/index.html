Privacy

Last updated Sep 30, 2026

Accounts

OPChase has no user accounts and no log-in. We do not ask for your name, address or payment details.

Analytics

We do not run analytics right now. If we enable it, we will use GoatCounter, which is cookieless, does not track you across sites and only keeps aggregate statistics (page, referrer, browser type, country).

Waitlist email

If you join the OPChase Pro waitlist with your email address, we use it only to contact you about OPChase Pro. We do not sell or share it. Ask us (see Contact) and we will delete it. If you join via GitHub instead, your GitHub issue is public and handled under GitHub's own privacy statement.

eBay listing data

Listings (title, price, photo, time left, link) come from eBay's public active listings through the eBay API and are shown under eBay's API terms. Photos are loaded directly from eBay's servers. Listings are refreshed regularly; anything older than 6 hours is not shown, and ended or removed listings are deleted from our data. We do not show seller names or seller feedback. The OPChase Index is our own estimate, not an eBay price. Clicking "View on eBay" takes you to eBay, where eBay's privacy notice applies.

Affiliate links

Links to eBay are plain links today. If we join the eBay Partner Network, links to eBay may carry an affiliate tracking ID and OPChase may earn a commission at no extra cost to you; we will disclose this on every page.

Cookies

OPChase itself sets no cookies.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/wonggum/optcg-index/issues.
