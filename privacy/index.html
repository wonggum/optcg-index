Privacy

Last updated Oct 29, 2026

Accounts

OPChase has no user accounts and no log-in. We do not ask for your name, address or payment details.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits to OPChase. The counting script is loaded from gc.zgo.at and reports the visit to opchase.goatcounter.com. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser (apart from the opt-out marker described below, if you use it), does not follow you across sites and does not build a profile of you.

What it records, as hourly or daily totals: the page address and page title, the referring site if there is one, your browser name and version, your operating system, your country (worked out from your IP address), your language and your screen width. These are kept as separate counts (for example "4 visits to this page used Firefox today") that cannot be linked to each other or to a person. GoatCounter does not store your IP address, your full browser identification string (User-Agent) or any visitor ID. To tell a new visit from a repeat one, it keeps your IP address and browser string together with a random ID in its server memory only, for up to 8 hours; this is never written to its database or disk. We have not switched on GoatCounter's optional per-pageview log.

We also count clicks on links to eBay as anonymous events. The event name holds only the page type, the link position and the card, for example ebay/home/bin/OP05-119-MANGA. It does not include the eBay item number, the price or anything you typed, and it is counted in the same way as a page view.

GoatCounter does not share this data with third parties. It is stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany, and GoatCounter is run from Ireland. The script file itself is delivered through a content delivery network, which, like any web server, sees your IP address when it sends the file. GoatCounter keeps the statistics until we delete them; deleted data can stay in its backups for up to 30 days. We use the statistics only to see which pages and cards are useful and to fix problems. We do not sell them and we do not combine them with any other data.

Opting out of analytics. To stop being counted in your current browser, add #toggle-goatcounter to the end of any OPChase page address and reload the page. GoatCounter shows a short notice and remembers the choice in that browser's local storage (key skipgc); repeat it to undo. You can also block gc.zgo.at and goatcounter.com with a content blocker, or turn off JavaScript; the site works without analytics.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. GoatCounter's counter ignores the Do Not Track setting by design, because it does not track people across sites. GoatCounter itself does not act on these signals; our page checks for them before counting. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the counting script still loads from gc.zgo.at, but no page view and no eBay click event is sent. If your browser sends neither signal and you do not want to be counted, use the opt-out above.

Changes. We will not change what analytics collects without updating this page first. Earlier versions of this page are in the public history of the site's repository (github.com/wonggum/optcg-index). OPChase has no accounts or mailing list, so there is no one to notify by email.

Listing alert emails

Free email alerts for new eBay listings are coming soon; sign-up is not open yet. Once they are switched on: if you set an alert, we store your email address, the card and the alert you chose (for example a new checked Buy It Now listing at or below a price you pick), and when you signed up and confirmed. We use this only to send the confirmation email and the alert emails. It will be stored in a database on Cloudflare (Workers and D1) and emails will be sent through Resend; both process it for us only. To limit abuse we keep a salted hash of your IP address and of your email address, which changes daily and is deleted after 2 days; we never store the IP address itself. Every email has a one-click link to cancel, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Contact). We do not sell or share your address, add it to other lists, or track opens or clicks in these emails.

Waitlist

OPChase no longer runs a waitlist. If you joined the earlier OPChase Pro waitlist on GitHub, that issue is public and handled under GitHub's own privacy statement; ask us (see Contact) and we will close it. We never collected waitlist email addresses.

eBay listing data

Listings (title, price, photo, time left, link) come from eBay's public active listings through the eBay API and are shown under eBay's API terms. Photos are loaded directly from eBay's servers. Listings are refreshed regularly; anything older than 6 hours is not shown, and ended or removed listings are deleted from our data. Each listing photo carries a small "Photo: eBay listing by <seller>" credit with the seller's public eBay username, linking to the listing; we do not show seller feedback or any other seller details. OPChase does not estimate or suggest prices; prices shown are the sellers' own eBay prices or current bids. Clicking "View on eBay" takes you to eBay, where eBay's privacy notice applies.

Affiliate links

OPChase is a member of the eBay Partner Network, eBay's affiliate program. Links to eBay on this site carry an affiliate tracking ID (our campaign ID plus a placement tag naming the page, link position and card, for example home_bin__OP05x119xMANGA; no personal data), and eBay may set its own cookies when you click through, under eBay's privacy notice. OPChase may earn a commission from qualifying eBay purchases through links on this site. It costs you nothing extra and does not change which listings we show or their order. If you do not want eBay to use cookies to measure or tailor ads after you click through, you can use "Your Privacy Choices" in the footer of ebay.com, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, or block third-party cookies in your browser settings. You can also skip our eBay links and go to eBay directly; the listings and photos on OPChase are the same either way, and we are not told who you are.

Hosting

OPChase is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account (see GitHub's Pages documentation and privacy statement). We do not have access to these logs.

Cookies & local storage

OPChase itself sets no cookies and GoatCounter sets none. eBay may set its own cookies when you click a link to eBay (see Affiliate links). The only things stored in your browser's local storage are your light/dark theme choice (key opc-theme) and, if you use the analytics opt-out above, GoatCounter's marker (key skipgc). Both stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Fonts

The Inter typeface is served from this site (self-hosted). No fonts are loaded from Google Fonts or any other third-party font service.
