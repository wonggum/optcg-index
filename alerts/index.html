FAQ

When will listing alerts launch?

Soon. The email service is being set up. This page will say so when you can sign up.

What will an alert tell me?

That an eBay listing passed our checks for the card you picked (exact version, English, PSA 10, US seller): either a new lowest checked Buy It Now for that card, or a checked auction ending within the number of hours you choose (1, 3, 6, 12 or 24). The email links to the card's OPChase page, where the listing is shown with eBay's own price or current bid.

How quickly do alerts arrive?

Listings are checked several times a day, so an alert can arrive some time after a listing appears. Alerts are not instant.

Does OPChase suggest a price?

No. Alerts are based only on new checked listings and auction end times. OPChase does not estimate, model or suggest prices.

Is this free?

Yes. Listing alerts are free.

How do I stop alerts?

Every email will have a one-click link to cancel that alert or unsubscribe from everything.
