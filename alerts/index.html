How alerts work

  • The OPChase Index updates once a week (Mondays). Alerts are checked right after each update, so they are not real-time.
  • Your level must be below the current index for a "falls below" alert, or above it for a "rises above" alert.
  • Alerts use only the OPChase Index. They never look at eBay listings, and emails contain no listing prices.
  • Each alert sends one email and then ends. You can set it again from the link in that email.
  • The OPChase Index is an estimate, not an eBay price or a quote, and an alert is not financial advice.

FAQ

How often are alerts checked?

Once a week. The OPChase Index updates on Mondays from recent same-spec English PSA 10 sold prices, and alerts are checked right after each update. Alerts are not real-time.

What data do alerts use?

Only the OPChase Index, our own estimate. Alerts never look at eBay listings, and alert emails contain no listing prices or listings. Each email links to the card's OPChase page and to a plain eBay search.

Which cards can I set an alert for?

The 89 tracked cards that have an OPChase Index value right now. Cards marked Insufficient data (fewer than 5 recent same-spec sales) cannot have alerts.

How many emails will I get?

One confirmation email, then one email when your level is crossed; after that the alert ends. If several of your alerts are crossed in the same week, they come in one email. Up to 10 alerts per email address.

How do I stop alerts?

Every email has a one-click link to cancel that alert or unsubscribe from everything. Your address is deleted once you have no alerts left.

Is this free?

Yes. OPChase Index price alerts are free for everyone and are not part of OPChase Pro.

Is an alert a buy or sell signal?

No. The OPChase Index is an estimate from public sales data, updated weekly. An alert only tells you that our estimate crossed your level. It is not financial advice.
