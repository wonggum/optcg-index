Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Law's manga card from OP-10 Royal Blood (Mar 2025), the most-graded Law spec in both English and Japanese per our notes.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP10-119-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • The Law SP exists only in PRB-02 and is filed as PRB02 #119 SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART.
  • PRB02 #119 can also be the new Sanji manga or a base reprint. Read the name and variety lines.
  • The Japanese label year is 2024; the English one is 2025.

How it plays

A yellow 7-cost Law that adds a Supernovas Character from hand to the top of your Life and attaches DON!! to the Leader on entry. It sees light play in OP10-099 Kid and OP10-022 Law.
