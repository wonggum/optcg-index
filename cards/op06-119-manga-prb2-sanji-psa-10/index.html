Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP06-119-MANGA-PRB2).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • This manga is new PRB-02 art, not a reprint of an OP-06 card.
  • PRB02 #119 can also be the Law OP10-119 SP or a base reprint. Read the name and variety lines.
  • The OP06-119 SP printed in OP-11 is filed as OP11 #119.

How it plays

A blue 9-cost Sanji that reveals the top card on entry and plays a non-Sanji Character from it for free. It is a core card of OP11-040 Blue/Purple Luffy, which led OP11 and OP12 with top-cut shares of 21.1% and 19.4% (as of 2026-10-22).
