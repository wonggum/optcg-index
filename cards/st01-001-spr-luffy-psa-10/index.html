Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • The Super Pre-Release leader from the English launch event (Sept 30 to Oct 6, 2022), with exclusive art and a first-edition gold stamp.
  • Our tier notes list ST01-001 Super Pre-Release among the high-end, scarce cards.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (ST01-001-SPR).
  • PSA label variety line reads SUPER PRE-RELEASE.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • ST01 #001 also exists as Base, ERRATA and Revision Pack. ERRATA is the corrected reprint and a different spec.
  • PROMOS #001 SUPER PRE-RELEASE is the P-001 Luffy promo, not this leader.
  • A third-party cert tool once mis-identified an ST01-001 ERRATA as another card. Read the label or PSA's own cert page.

How it plays

The first red Luffy Leader, which attaches an extra rested DON!! to the Leader or a Character each turn to speed up attacks. It held top-cut shares of 15.7% in OP01 and 7.3% in OP02 (as of 2026-10-22) and is no longer Standard-legal after the Block 1 rotation.
