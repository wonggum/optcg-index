Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP01-120-AA).
  • PSA label variety line reads ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • PRB-01 reprinted the AA (PRB01 #120). The reprint has the bottom-left ONE PIECE CARD GAME logo and no pen icon next to the illustrator name.
  • OP01 #120 MANGA ALTERNATE ART and the 2023 PROMOS #120 serialized prize are different cards.

How it plays

A red 9-cost Shanks with Rush that stops low-power Blockers from blocking when it attacks, an early red finisher. Its high cost kept tournament use light, mostly in ST01-001 Luffy and OP01-001 Zoro aggro.
