Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Law's manga card from OP-05 Awakening of the New Era (Dec 2023).
  • A Japanese comic-parallel ranking cited in our notes puts it seventh among manga cards.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP05-069-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • The PRB-01 manga reprint (PRB01 #069) reportedly shows no visible text change; check the foil texture and pen icon instead.
  • PRB01 #069 also covers Vinsmoke Reiju. Read the name line.

How it plays

A purple 3-cost Law that searches for Heart Pirates cards on attack when the opponent has more DON!! than you. It sees only light play, mainly in OP12-061 Rosinante.
