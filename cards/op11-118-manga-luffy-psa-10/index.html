Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Luffy's manga card from OP-11 A Fist of Divine Speed (Jun 2025).
  • Our tier notes list OP11-118 Manga among the high-end, scarce cards.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP11-118-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • LEARN TOGETHER DECK SET #118 reuses the OP11-118 art; it is a different spec.
  • OP11 #118 Base and ALTERNATE ART are the other pack versions.
  • OP-11 came out in 2025 in both languages; check the label for English.

How it plays

A blue 8-cost Luffy with Rush that can discard on attack to bounce a low-cost Character and attach a rested DON!!. It belongs to OP11-040 Blue/Purple Luffy and declined along with that leader after the Banned Pair restriction took effect on 2025-08-30.
