Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • The ST26-005 Luffy SP printed in OP15-EB04 (Apr 2026).
  • Our tier notes rate the English ST26-005 SP in the mid-tier group.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (ST26-005-SP-EN).
  • PSA label variety line reads SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • PSA keeps the original number under the newer set, so look for #005 with the OP15 set line, not ST26.
  • The Japanese SP is filed as JAPANESE OP15 #005 SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART, a separate spec.

How it plays

A purple 6-cost Luffy that spends DON!! to raise a multicolor Straw Hat leader's base power when the opponent has 5+ DON!!. It is played almost only in OP09-061 Purple/Black Luffy.
