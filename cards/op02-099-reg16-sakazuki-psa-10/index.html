Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP02-099-REG16).
  • PSA label variety line reads OFFLINE REGIONAL-TOP 16.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP02 #099 Base/AA, the OP04 #099 SP and the PRB-01 copies are different cards.
  • The Japanese CS2023 Top 32 prize is filed as ... #099 CHAMPIONSHIP 2023-TOP PRIZE.

How it plays

A black 6-cost Sakazuki that discards a card on entry to K.O. an opposing mid-cost Character, a general-purpose black removal piece. It was played across black decks such as OP06-080 Moria, OP02-093 Smoker and OP03-076 Lucci, and is no longer Standard-legal after the Block 1 rotation.
