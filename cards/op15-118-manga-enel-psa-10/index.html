Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Enel's manga card from OP15-EB04 (Apr 2026), the top-tier Enel treatment in our notes.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP15-118-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP15-EB04 #118 also has Enel Base and ALTERNATE ART; only MANGA ALTERNATE ART is this card.
  • The English set line is OP15-EB04; the Japanese printing uses OP15.

How it plays

A purple 6-cost Enel with the same protection and +2000 while you have 6 or fewer DON!!, plus an entry ability that returns a DON!! to look at the top 5 cards, take one, then discard one. It is a core card of OP15-058 Enel, which held top-cut shares of 44.5% in OP15 and 22.7% in OP16 (as of 2026-10-22).
