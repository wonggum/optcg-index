Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (ST01-010-1A).
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • ST01 #010 Franky also exists as SUPER PRE-RELEASE and DEMO DECK versions under the ST01 line.
  • The set version is filed under ENGLISH VERSION 1ST ANNIVERSARY SET, not ST01.

How it plays

A red 4-cost Franky with 6000 power and no effect, a vanilla stat body. It was a common inclusion in early OP02-001 Whitebeard decks and is no longer Standard-legal after the Block 1 rotation.
