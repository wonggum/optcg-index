Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • The Luffy leader from the English Version 2nd Anniversary Set, a Premium Bandai boxed set delivered June 2025.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP09-061-2A).
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP09 #061 Base and ALTERNATE ART and the ST-26 deck reprint are different cards with different set lines.
  • A Japanese-printed copy of the same set is filed by PSA as a separate set.
  • Treasure Cup listings of a Jumbo OP09-061 are suspect: Bandai corrected that prize to an OP-11 uncut sheet.

How it plays

A Purple/Black Luffy Leader that, with a DON!! attached, raises all your Characters' cost by 1 and, once per turn on your turn, adds one active and one rested DON!! when two or more DON!! are returned. It reached a 5.6% top-cut share in OP11 (as of 2026-10-22), and Limitless lists no first-place finish for it.
