Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Rayleigh's manga card from OP-08 Two Legends (Sep 2024), the top-tier Rayleigh treatment in our notes.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP08-118-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP08 #118 also has Rayleigh Base and ALTERNATE ART.
  • The PRB-02 copy is a base-art reprint (copyright line ends in EN), not a manga.

How it plays

A red 8-cost Rayleigh that weakens two opposing Characters and K.O.s a low-power one on entry, a large red board-clearing card. It is mainly played in OP09-001 Shanks, which held a 6.9% top-cut share in OP09 (as of 2026-10-22), and in OP08-002 Marco.
