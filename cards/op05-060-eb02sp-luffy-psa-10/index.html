Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP05-060-EB02SP).
  • PSA label variety line reads SPECIAL.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • EB02 #060 SPECIAL is a different card from OP05 #060 ALTERNATE ART; 'OP05-060 SP' titles can mean either.
  • The PSA Magazine Exclusive and Sound Loader #060 promos and the ST-18 deck copy are other #060 Luffys.

How it plays

A purple Luffy Leader that, once per turn, can move a Life card to hand to gain an extra active DON!! if you have 0 or 3+ DON!! on the field, a representative leader of purple's DON!!-return style. It held a 12.8% top-cut share in OP05 and 9.0% in OP09 (as of 2026-10-22), and Limitless lists it first at the Latin America and Oceania Championship Finals on 2024-01-27.
