What drives collector demand

  • Kuzan's manga card from OP-16 The Time of Battle (Jun 2026).

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP16-063-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP16 #063 also has a Kuzan ALTERNATE ART; only MANGA ALTERNATE ART is this card.
  • A Japanese OP16 printing of the same manga card exists under a separate Japanese set line.

How it plays

A purple 7-cost Kuzan that adds DON!! on entry and can spend DON!! to stop an opposing Character from using Blocker that turn. It has very little tournament play so far, appearing only in a handful of OP16-060 Sengoku lists.
