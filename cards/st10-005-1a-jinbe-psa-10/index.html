Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (ST10-005-1A).
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • #005 Jinbe appears under many set lines (ST01 Super Pre-Release, ST21 and ST29 AAs, PRB-02 Sparkle Foil, Gift Collection 2023 and more). Look for ENGLISH VERSION 1ST ANNIVERSARY SET.
  • ST10-005 also has a PRB-02 reprint and a PRB-02 Pirate Foil.

How it plays

A red 2-cost Jinbe that weakens an opposing Character when attacking with DON!! attached, a low-cost red pressure piece. It sees light play in OP08-002 Marco, OP02-001 Whitebeard and ST10-002 Luffy.
