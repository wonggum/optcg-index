Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Sabo's Red Super Alternate Art from OP-13 Carrying On His Will, the 3rd-anniversary set (Nov 2025).
  • Our character notes list it as the top-tier treatment for Sabo, alongside the OP04-083 manga.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP13-120-REDMANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP13 #120 also exists as Base, ALTERNATE ART, MANGA ALTERNATE ART (the non-red Super AA) and WANTED ALTERNATE ART. Only the variety line RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART is this card.
  • Store Treasure Cup 2025 and the English Version 3rd Anniversary Set also carry a #120 Sabo under different label lines.

How it plays

A black 6-cost Blocker Sabo that, once per turn, gives one of your Characters +2 cost until the end of the opponent's next turn and then gives a rested DON!! to your Leader. It sees very little play, mainly in OP13-004 Sabo.
