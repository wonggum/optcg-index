Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Luffy Manga Alternate Art from EB-02 Anime 25th Collection (May 2025).
  • The card is the main finisher of multicolor Luffy decks, per this page's How it plays section.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (EB02-061-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • PRB02 #061 SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART is a new SP art, a different card; PRB02 #061 Base is a base-art reprint. Neither is the EB-02 manga.
  • EB02 #061 also exists as Base and Alternate Art.

How it plays

A purple 6-cost Luffy that gains Rush with a multicolor leader when the opponent has 5+ DON!!, and once per turn can return DON!! on attack to ready itself and take a Life card into hand. It is the main finisher of multicolor Luffy decks, above all EB02-010 Green/Purple Luffy (top-cut share 16.2% in OP11, as of 2026-10-22), and also OP09-061 Luffy.
