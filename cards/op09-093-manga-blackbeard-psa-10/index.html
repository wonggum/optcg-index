Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Teach's manga card from OP-09 Emperors in the New World (Dec 2024).

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP09-093-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP09 #093 SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART is Rob Lucci, not Teach.
  • OP09 #093 WANTED ALTERNATE ART and the OP12 #093 Gold/Silver SPs are different Teach cards.
  • A PRB-02 base reprint of OP09-093 is filed under the PRB02 set line.

How it plays

A black 10-cost Blocker Teach that, under a Blackbeard Pirates leader on the turn it is played, can negate the opponent's Leader effect and one opposing Character's effect, and that Character also cannot attack until the end of the opponent's next turn. It is the ace of OP09-081 Blackbeard (14.8% top-cut share in OP10, as of 2026-10-22) and also appears in OP16-080 Blackbeard.
