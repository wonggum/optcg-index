Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Sakazuki's manga card from OP-16 The Time of Battle (Jun 2026).

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP16-065-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP16 #065 also has Sakazuki Base and ALTERNATE ART; only MANGA ALTERNATE ART is this card.
  • A Japanese OP16 printing of the same manga card exists under a separate Japanese set line.

How it plays

A purple 7-cost Sakazuki that spends DON!! on entry to give an opposing Character a large power reduction through the opponent's turn, and can ramp DON!! under a Navy leader. It sees moderate play, mostly in OP12-061 Rosinante (9.9% top-cut share in OP16, as of 2026-10-22).
