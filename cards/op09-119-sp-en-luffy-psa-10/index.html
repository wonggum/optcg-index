Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • The OP09-119 Luffy SP printed in OP-13 (Nov 2025).
  • Our tier notes rate the English OP09-119 SP in the mid-tier group.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP09-119-SP-EN).
  • PSA label variety line reads SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP13 #119 is otherwise Ace (Base, AA, Manga, Red Manga, Wanted). Only the Luffy SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART is this card.
  • JAPANESE OP13 #119 SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART and the Japanese 3rd Anniversary Set #119 are different specs.

How it plays

A purple 9-cost Luffy that can return DON!! on entry to draw a card and gain Rush. It is a common finisher in purple Luffy decks, mainly OP11-040 and OP05-060.
