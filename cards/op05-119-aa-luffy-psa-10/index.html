Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • The Gear 5 Luffy alternate art from OP-05 (Dec 2023).
  • Our tier notes rate the OP05-119 AA in the mid-tier group.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP05-119-AA).
  • PSA label variety line reads ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • PRB-01 reprinted the AA (PRB01 #119); the reprint has no pen icon next to the illustrator name. The bottom-left stamp is not a reliable tell for this card.
  • OP05 #119 MANGA ALTERNATE ART, the OP09 #119 Wanted, the OP11 #119 Gold/Silver SPs and the English Version 2nd Anniversary Set #119 are different cards.
  • CGC has issued a counterfeit alert for this card family.

How it plays

The Gear 5 Luffy: a purple 10-cost Character whose entry ability spends ten DON!! to put your other Characters on the bottom of the deck and take an extra turn, and which can also ramp DON!! once per turn. It is a signature card used only in dedicated builds around ST10-002, OP05-060 and OP09-061 Luffy, as its extra-turn cost is steep.
