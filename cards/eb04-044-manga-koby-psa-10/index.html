Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Koby's manga card, printed in OP15-EB04 in English (Apr 2026), the top-tier Koby treatment in our notes.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (EB04-044-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • In Japanese it is an EB-04 card; in English the set line is OP15-EB04.
  • OP15-EB04 #044 also has Koby Base and ALTERNATE ART and a Koala RELEASE EVENT card. Read the name line.

How it plays

A black 6-cost Koby that, under a Navy leader, can discard a card once per turn instead of leaving the field, and draws once per turn on your turn when an opposing Character is K.O.'d. It sees very little competitive play, mainly in OP11-001 Koby, and Navy decks have not been a top-five archetype.
