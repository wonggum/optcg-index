Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP03-114-CS24).
  • PSA label variety line reads OFFICIAL EVENT TOP PRIZE.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • OP03 #114 Base/AA and the SP printed in OP-06 (OP06 #114) are different cards.
  • The Japanese twin is filed as JAPANESE PROMOS #114 CHAMPIONSHIP 2024-TOP PRIZE.

How it plays

A yellow 10-cost Big Mom that, under a Big Mom Pirates leader, adds the top card of your deck to your Life and trashes the opponent's top Life card on entry, a late-game card for Life-based yellow. It was common in OP03-099 Katakuri, which reached top-cut shares of 9.8%–11.2% in OP03–OP04 (as of 2026-10-22); it is no longer Standard-legal after Block 1 rotated.
