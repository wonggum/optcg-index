Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP03-122-MANGA-PRB1).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • This is the PRB-01 reprint, filed as PRB01 #122 with the character name Sogeking. The OP-03 original is OP03 #122.
  • PRB-01 copies carry the bottom-left ONE PIECE CARD GAME logo; OP01 to OP04 originals do not.

How it plays

A blue 7-cost Sogeking (also treated as Usopp) that returns a Character of cost 6 or less to its owner's hand on entry, then draws 2 and discards 2. It was used in blue decks such as ST03-001 Crocodile, OP04-040 Queen and OP03-040 Nami.
