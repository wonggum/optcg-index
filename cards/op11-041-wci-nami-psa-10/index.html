Collector guide

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP11-041-WCI).
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • The Whole Cake Island playmat-set promo is filed as PROMOS #041 OFFICIAL PLAYMAT & CARD SET -WHOLE CAKE ISLAND ARC-; OP11 #041 Base/AA and the Learn Together Deck Set #041 are different.

How it plays

A Blue/Yellow Nami Leader that, once per turn on your turn, draws when a card is removed from either player's Life (if you hold 7 or fewer cards), and with DON!! attached can discard on the opponent's attack to gain +2000. On Limitless it held top-cut shares of 20.7% in OP15 and 23.5% in OP16 (as of 2026-10-22).
