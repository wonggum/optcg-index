Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Kid's OP-05 manga card (Dec 2023); a Japanese comic-parallel ranking cited in our notes puts it eighth.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP05-074-MANGA).
  • PSA label variety line reads MANGA ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • The PRB-01 manga reprint is filed as PRB01 #074. It has no pen icon next to the illustrator name and lacks the original's textured foil.
  • The OP05-074 SP printed in OP-07 is filed as OP07 #074.

How it plays

A purple 5-cost Blocker Kid that, once per turn, replaces a DON!! when your DON!! gets returned, smoothing DON!!-return strategies. It is an engine piece in OP05-060 Purple Luffy and ST10-001 Law.
