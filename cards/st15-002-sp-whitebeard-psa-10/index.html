Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (ST15-002-SP).
  • PSA label variety line reads SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • The SP is printed in OP-10, so the label reads OP10 #002, not ST15.
  • PRB02 #002 can be a Newgate AA, the Newgate base reprint, or Law cards. Read the name and variety lines.

How it plays

A red 7-cost Edward.Newgate that attaches DON!! on entry and can rest itself to K.O. a mid-power Character. It sees moderate play in OP09-001 Shanks and OP08-002 Marco.
