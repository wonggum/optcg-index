Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • Serial-numbered Yamato, the Extra Grand Battle finalist prize running all year.

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (EB02-006-SERIAL).
  • PSA label variety line reads OFFICIAL EVENT SERIALIZED TOP PRIZE.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • EB02 #006 Base and ALTERNATE ART are the pack versions from EB-02, not the serialized prize.
  • A Premium Card Collection promo of #006 Yamato also exists under a PROMOS line.
  • For serialized prizes the number is printed on the card; check it matches the photos.

How it plays

A red 6-cost Yamato that, once per turn with a Land of Wano or Portgas.D.Ace leader, gives a rested DON!! to the Leader and then gains Rush for the turn. Its leader condition keeps it niche, with light use almost entirely in OP13-002 Ace lists.
