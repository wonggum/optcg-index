Collector guide

Version ID

Diagram by OPChase from the facts on this page. Not a photo of the card.

What drives collector demand

  • The OP07-109 Luffy Treasure Rare printed in OP-08 Two Legends (Sep 2024).

Before you buy: checklist

  • Card number and version match this page (OP07-109-TR).
  • PSA label variety line reads TREASURE RARE.
  • English printing.
  • Grade 10 on the label; look up the cert on PSA's own site yourself.
  • Photos show the actual slab front and back.
  • Other #109 Luffys include OP07 Base/AA, the Championship 2024 serialized card, Championship 25-26 Top Player Pack cards, Illustration Box Vol.3 and a PRB-02 reprint.
  • OP08 #109 Base is Mont Blanc Noland. Read the name line.
  • There was no Japanese TR before OP-16, so a 'JP Treasure Rare OP07-109' listing is an English TR or mis-titled.

How it plays

A yellow 5-cost Luffy that can trash itself when you are at 2 Life or less to K.O. an opposing Character of cost 4 or less and draw a card; its Trigger performs the same K.O. without the draw. It is a core part of ST13-003 Black/Yellow Luffy, which led OP08 with a 28.6% top-cut share (as of 2026-10-22), and also appears in OP05-098 Enel.
