Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2023-09-22 TCG. Our database has 155 card printings of 130 card numbers in it (125 Normal, 24 Parallel (AA), 5 SP, 3 DON!!, 1 Manga), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP04-PRE TCG. The most-graded card is OP01-078 Boa Hancock (SP) with 2,700 PSA 10 copies GR.

FAQ

When was OP04 Kingdoms of Intrigue released in English?

Kingdoms of Intrigue (OP04) was released in English on 2023-09-22 (TCGplayer group publishedOn). The Japanese version came out on 2023-05-27.

How many cards are in OP04?

130 card numbers in the tcgplayer catalog. Our database has 155 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP04?
