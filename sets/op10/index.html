Royal Blood (OP10) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2025-03-21 TCG. Our database has 151 card printings of 126 card numbers in it (119 Normal, 24 Parallel (AA), 6 SP, 1 Treasure Rare, 1 Manga), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP10-RE TCG. The most-graded card is OP10-119 Trafalgar Law (Manga) with 2,284 PSA 10 copies GR.
