FAQ

When was OP17 The World's Strongest Warriors released in English?

Pre-Release August 21, 2026; Release Date August 28, 2026 (Bandai product page).

How many cards are in OP17?

The official OP-17 card list has 169 entries, with card numbers OP17-001 to OP17-119, and the product page lists 131+4 types in total.

Why does a Pirate Crew Luffy slab say #061, not an OP17 number?

The card is EB04-061, listed in Bandai's official OP-17 card list with that code. PSA prints only the digits, so the label shows #061.

Why do some OP-17 slabs show numbers like #028 or #004?

SP cards from older sets (for example OP13-028 Shanks, ST31-004 Luffy) are printed in OP-17 with their original codes. The label keeps the old number under the OP-17 set line.
