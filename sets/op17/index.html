The World's Strongest Warriors (OP17) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2026-08-28 TCG. Our database has 169 card printings of 130 card numbers in it (125 Normal, 26 Parallel (AA), 11 SP, 7 DON!!, 4 Manga), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP17-RE TCG. The most-graded card is OP17-079 Monkey.D.Luffy (Alternate Art (AA) · Super Leader Alternate Art) with 135 PSA 10 copies GR.

When was OP17 The World's Strongest Warriors released in English?
