ST-21: Starter Deck 21 EX Gear 5 (ST-21) is an English One Piece Card Game deck first listed on 2025-03-14 TCG. Our database has 32 card printings of 17 card numbers in it (17 Normal, 15 Parallel (AA), 1 DON!!) TCG. The most-graded card is ST21-001 Monkey.D.Luffy (Normal) with 2,792 PSA 10 copies GR.
