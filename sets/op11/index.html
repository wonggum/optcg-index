A Fist of Divine Speed (OP11) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2025-06-06 TCG. Our database has 156 card printings of 127 card numbers in it (119 Normal, 27 Parallel (AA), 7 SP, 3 DON!!, 1 SP Gold), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP11-RE TCG. The most-graded card is OP11-080 Gear Two (Alternate Art (AA)) with 5,560 PSA 10 copies GR.

FAQ

When was OP11 A Fist of Divine Speed released in English?

A Fist of Divine Speed (OP11) was released in English on 2025-06-06 (TCGplayer group publishedOn). The Japanese version came out on 2025-03-01.

How many cards are in OP11?

127 card numbers in the tcgplayer catalog. Our database has 156 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP11?

By OPChase PSA 10 anchors: OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy SP Gold ($17,027); OP11-118 Monkey.D.Luffy Manga ($2,005); OP11-041 Nami Alternate Art (AA) ($118).
