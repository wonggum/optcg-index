FAQ

When was OP11 A Fist of Divine Speed released in English?

A Fist of Divine Speed (OP11) was released in English on 2025-06-06. The Japanese version came out on 2025-03-01.

How many cards are in OP11?

127 card numbers in our card database. Our database has 156 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP11?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy SP Gold; OP11-118 Monkey.D.Luffy Manga; OP11-041 Nami Alternate Art (AA).
