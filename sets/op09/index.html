Emperors in the New World (OP09, OP-09) is an English One Piece Card Game booster with an English release date of December 13, 2024 and 159 cards in its full card list, counting each parallel, manga and SP version separately. Start with the top chase cards below, or jump to the full card list.

Emperors in the New World (OP-09) celebrated the English game's 2nd anniversary on December 13, 2024. It brought back alt-art Leaders and put all four New Four Emperors (Shanks, Buggy, Marshall.D.Teach and Luffy) on Super Alt-Art (manga) cards, plus Gol.D.Roger's Gold Manga, Wanted Poster SPs and the OP05-119 Luffy Wanted. The official page lists 130+1 card types including 10 Special Cards and 1 Treasure Rare.

FAQ

When was OP09 Emperors in the New World released in English?

Emperors in the New World (OP09) was released in English on 2024-12-13 (official product page). The Japanese version came out on 2024-08-31.

How many cards are in OP09?

130+1 card types per the official product page. Our database has 159 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP09?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP09-118 Gol.D.Roger Manga; OP09-119 Monkey.D.Luffy Manga; OP09-093 Marshall.D.Teach Manga; OP09-051 Buggy Manga; OP09-004 Shanks Manga; OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy Wanted; OP09-004 Shanks Wanted; OP09-093 Marshall.D.Teach Wanted.

What's new in OP09?

2nd anniversary of the English launch; first new Alt-art Leader cards since OP-05. The New Four Emperors are all available as Super Alt-Art cards (four Super Alt-Arts, a first). Gol.D.Roger appears. Lim and Nico Robin debut as Leader cards. Launch event with exclusive prizes for the 2nd Anniversary.

Collector notes

Treasure Rare

The official EN card list (OP-09 page, read 2026-10-01) shows the one Treasure Rare as ST18-004_p1 Zoro-Juurou, rarity TR. It is in our card database as 'Zoro-Juurou (ST18-004)' in the OP09 group with rarity TR OFF.

Special cards

The official page lists 10 Special Cards. Our database has 6 SP and 4 Wanted Poster cards in the booster (OP05-119, OP09-004, OP09-051, OP09-093), which adds up to 10 OFF.

Four Emperors Manga cards

The New Four Emperors (Shanks OP09-004, Buggy OP09-051, Marshall.D.Teach OP09-093, Luffy OP09-119) all have Super Alt-Art (manga) versions OFF. PSA files them as MANGA ALTERNATE ART; Gol.D.Roger OP09-118 is filed as GOLD MANGA ALTERNATE ART GR.
