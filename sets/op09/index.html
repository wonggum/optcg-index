Emperors in the New World (OP-09) celebrated the English game's 2nd anniversary on December 13, 2024 OFF. It brought back alt-art Leaders and put all four New Four Emperors (Shanks, Buggy, Marshall.D.Teach and Luffy) on Super Alt-Art (manga) cards, plus Gol.D.Roger's Gold Manga, Wanted Poster SPs and the OP05-119 Luffy Wanted OFFTCGGR. The official page lists 130+1 card types including 10 Special Cards and 1 Treasure Rare OFF.

Collector notes

Treasure Rare

The official EN card list (OP-09 page, read 2026-10-01) shows the one Treasure Rare as ST18-004_p1 Zoro-Juurou, rarity TR. It is in our database as TCGplayer product 600658 'Zoro-Juurou (ST18-004)' in the OP09 group with rarity TR; TCGplayer's product name just doesn't say Treasure Rare OFFTCG.

Special cards

The official page lists 10 Special Cards. Our database has 6 SP and 4 Wanted Poster cards in the booster (OP05-119, OP09-004, OP09-051, OP09-093), which adds up to 10 OFFTCG.

Four Emperors Manga cards

The New Four Emperors (Shanks OP09-004, Buggy OP09-051, Marshall.D.Teach OP09-093, Luffy OP09-119) all have Super Alt-Art (manga) versions OFFTCG. PSA files them as MANGA ALTERNATE ART; Gol.D.Roger OP09-118 is filed as GOLD MANGA ALTERNATE ART GR.

When was OP09 Emperors in the New World released in English?

Emperors in the New World (OP09) was released in English on 2024-12-13 (official product page; TCGplayer group publishedOn). The Japanese version came out on 2024-08-31.

How many cards are in OP09?

130+1 card types per the official product page. Our database has 159 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.
