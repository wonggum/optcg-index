FAQ

When was EB05 Extra Booster: One Piece Heroine's Edition Vol. 2 released in English?

Extra Booster: One Piece Heroine's Edition Vol. 2 (EB-05) was released in English on 2026-10-30.

How many cards are in EB05?

61 card numbers (1 Leader, 9 SR, 1 SEC, 21 R and 29 C) per the official English card list. The official list shows 96 entries (61 base, 35 parallel); our card database lists all 96.

Why does the EB05 card list show 96 entries but only 61 card numbers?

The official English card list shows 96 entries: 61 base entries, one per card number EB05-001 to EB05-061, and 35 parallel entries — 24 for EB05 card numbers and 11 for card numbers from other sets (EB03-001 Nefeltari Vivi, OP01-016 Nami (SP CARD), OP11-022 Shirahoshi, OP11-041 Nami, OP13-100 Jewelry Bonney, OP14-033 Perona, OP14-041 Boa Hancock, OP15-039 Rebecca, OP17-081 Gerd, OP17-109 Charlotte Pudding and ST17-004 Boa Hancock). Our card database lists all 96 as printings.
