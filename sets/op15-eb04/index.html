FAQ

When was OP15-EB04 Adventure on Kami's Island released in English?

Adventure on Kami's Island (OP15-EB04) was released in English on 2026-04-03. The Japanese version came out on 2026-02-28 (OP-15) / 2026-01-31 (EB-04).

How many cards are in OP15-EB04?

158 card numbers in our card database. Our database has 203 card printings in the English booster, plus 90 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP15-EB04?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP15-118 Enel Manga; ST26-005 Monkey.D.Luffy SP; EB04-044 Koby Manga.

Collector notes

EB-04 split

In English, the Japanese EB-04 (EGGHEAD CRISIS) cards were split across OP14 and OP15-EB04; there is no stand-alone English EB-04 KB.
