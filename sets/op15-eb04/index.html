Adventure on Kami's Island (OP15-EB04) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2026-04-03 TCG. Our database has 203 card printings of 158 card numbers in it (156 Normal, 35 Parallel (AA), 9 SP, 3 DON!!, 2 Manga), plus 90 event-stamped cards from OP15-RE TCG. The most-graded card is OP15-086 Nami (Alternate Art (AA)) with 2,061 PSA 10 copies GR.
