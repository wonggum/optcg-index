The Time of Battle (OP16) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2026-06-12 TCG. Our database has 155 card printings of 125 card numbers in it (119 Normal, 25 Parallel (AA), 6 SP, 3 Manga, 3 DON!!), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP16-RE TCG. The most-graded card is OP14-084 Ms. All Sunday (SP) with 1,026 PSA 10 copies GR.

When was OP16 The Time of Battle released in English?

How many cards are in OP16?
