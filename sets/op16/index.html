FAQ

When was OP16 The Time of Battle released in English?

The Time of Battle (OP16) was released in English on 2026-06-12. The Japanese version came out on 2026-05-30.

How many cards are in OP16?

125 card numbers in our card database. Our database has 155 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.
