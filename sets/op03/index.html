Pillars of Strength (OP03) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2023-06-30 TCG. Our database has 154 card printings of 127 card numbers in it (123 Normal, 26 Parallel (AA), 4 Wanted, 1 Manga, 1 DON!!), plus 77 event-stamped cards from OP03-PRE TCG. The most-graded card is ST01-012 Monkey.D.Luffy (Wanted) with 1,515 PSA 10 copies GR.
