Extra Booster: One Piece Heroines Edition (EB-03-04) is an English One Piece Card Game Extra Booster first listed on 2026-02-20 TCG. Our database has 94 card printings of 66 card numbers in it (66 Normal, 18 Parallel (AA), 9 SP, 5 Gold DON!!, 5 DON!!) TCG. The most-graded card is EB03-001 Nefeltari Vivi (Alternate Art (AA)) with 1,282 PSA 10 copies GR.
