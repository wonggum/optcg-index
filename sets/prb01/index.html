ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST (PRB-01) is a Premium Booster released November 8, 2024 OFF. It reprints popular cards from earlier sets, many with new full-art or alternate-art designs, adds a new Sanji Leader and 30 new DON!! cards (also in gold) OFF. Every PRB-01 printing is a separate PSA spec from the original, so 'OP05-119 Luffy PSA 10' can mean a much cheaper PRB-01 reprint KBGR.

Collector notes

Reprints are separate cards

PRB-01 (ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST) reprints popular cards from earlier sets. PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so they are separate specs from the originals and usually trade much lower KBGR. Community sources say PRB-01 reprints carry a 'ONE PIECE CARD GAME' stamp at the bottom left and lack the pen icon next to the illustrator name, and PRB-01 Manga reprints have no textured foil REDOAPI.

Versions in this product

TCGplayer lists most numbers here in several finishes: plain reprint, foil reprint (Jolly Roger foil), Alternate Art, Full Art and Manga. Each is its own product and, when graded, its own PSA spec TCGGR.
