Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB01, PRB-01) is an English One Piece Card Game Premium Booster with an English release date of November 8, 2024 and 319 cards in its full card list, counting each parallel, manga and SP version separately. ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST (PRB-01) is a Premium Booster released November 8, 2024. It reprints popular cards from earlier sets, many with new full-art or alternate-art designs, adds a new Sanji Leader and 30 new DON!! cards (also in gold) (Source: official product page). Every PRB-01 printing is a separate PSA spec from the original, so 'OP05-119 Luffy PSA 10' can mean a PRB-01 reprint; check the set line.

FAQ

When was PRB01 Premium Booster -The Best- released in English?

Premium Booster -The Best- (PRB-01) was released in English on 2024-11-08 (official product page). The Japanese version came out on 2024-07-27.

How many cards are in PRB01?

111+30 card types per the official product page. Our database has 319 card printings in the English Premium Booster.

What are the chase cards in PRB01?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP01-016 Nami Manga; OP03-122 Sogeking Manga.

What's new in PRB01?

Selection of popular cards from previous booster packs, many as new full-art Alt-Art cards. Brand new Sanji Leader card. 30 new DON!! cards, some with gold foiling.

Collector notes

Reprints are separate cards

PRB-01 (ONE PIECE CARD THE BEST) reprints popular cards from earlier sets. (Source: official product page) PSA files them under the PRB01 set line, so they are separate specs from the originals KBGR.

Versions in this product

Our card database lists most numbers here in several finishes: plain reprint, foil reprint (Jolly Roger foil), Alternate Art, Full Art and Manga. Each is its own product and, when graded, its own PSA spec GR.
