ST-01: Starter Deck 1 Straw Hat Crew (ST-01) is an English One Piece Card Game deck first listed on 2022-12-02 TCG. Our database has 17 card printings of 17 card numbers in it (17 Normal, 1 DON!!), plus 18 event-stamped cards from ST01-PRE TCG. The most-graded card is ST01-001 Monkey.D.Luffy (Pre-Release) with 1,383 PSA 10 copies GR.
