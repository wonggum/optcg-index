The Azure Sea's Seven (OP14) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2026-01-16 TCG. Our database has 205 card printings of 160 card numbers in it (158 Normal, 35 Parallel (AA), 10 SP, 4 DON!!, 2 Gold DON!!), plus 89 event-stamped cards from OP14-RE TCG. The most-graded card is PRB02-006 Roronoa Zoro (SP) with 2,940 PSA 10 copies GR.

Collector notes

EB-04 split

In English, the Japanese EB-04 (EGGHEAD CRISIS) cards were split across OP14 and OP15-EB04; there is no stand-alone English EB-04 KBTCG. EB04 card numbers therefore appear on both set pages.
