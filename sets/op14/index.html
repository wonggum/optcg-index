The Azure Sea's Seven (OP14) was released in English on 2026-01-16. The Japanese version came out on 2025-11-22.

How many cards are in OP14?

160 card numbers in our card database. Our database has 205 card printings in the English booster, plus 89 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP14?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP14-119 Dracule Mihawk Manga.

Collector notes

EB-04 split

In English, the Japanese EB-04 (EGGHEAD CRISIS) cards were split across OP14 and OP15-EB04; there is no stand-alone English EB-04 KB. EB04 card numbers therefore appear on both set pages.
