500 Years in the Future (OP07) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2024-06-28 TCG. Our database has 151 card printings of 126 card numbers in it (119 Normal, 24 Parallel (AA), 6 SP, 3 DON!!, 1 Manga), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP07-PRE TCG. The most-graded card is OP07-051 Boa Hancock (Manga · Parallel) with 2,614 PSA 10 copies GR.
