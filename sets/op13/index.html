Carrying On His Will (OP13, OP-13) is an English One Piece Card Game booster with an English release date of November 7, 2025 and 175 cards in its full card list, counting each parallel, manga and SP version separately. Start with the top chase cards below, or jump to the full card list.

Carrying On His Will (OP-13) is the English 3rd-anniversary booster, released November 7, 2025. It debuted Gol.D.Roger and Imu as Leaders and is the only booster with Red Manga Alt Art cards, for Luffy OP13-118, Ace OP13-119 and Sabo OP13-120.

FAQ

When was OP13 Carrying On His Will released in English?

Carrying On His Will (OP13) was released in English on 2025-11-07 (official product page). The Japanese version came out on 2025-08-23.

How many cards are in OP13?

Red Manga Alt Art vs Manga Alt Art

Bandai's official card list gives OP13-118 Luffy, OP13-119 Ace and OP13-120 Sabo each a base entry and four parallel entries with their own suffixes (_p1 to _p4, with _p4 listed as SP CARD); they are different cards, so always check the variety line on the slab OFFKB.
