Carrying On His Will (OP-13) is the English 3rd-anniversary booster, released November 7, 2025 OFF. It debuted Gol.D.Roger and Imu as Leaders and is the only booster with Red Super Alt-Art cards, for Luffy OP13-118, Ace OP13-119 and Sabo OP13-120 OFFTCG. It also has non-red Super Alt-Arts, four new Wanted cards, Character Edition SPs and the 3rd Anniversary Shanks SP in Gold and Silver OFFGR.

Collector notes

Red Super Alt-Art vs Super Alt-Art

OP13-118 Luffy, OP13-119 Ace and OP13-120 Sabo each have a Red Super Alt-Art (OP13-only, official product page) and a non-red Super Alt-Art. PSA files the red ones as RED MANGA ALTERNATE ART and the non-red ones as MANGA ALTERNATE ART; these are different specs with different populations and prices OFFGRKB. The exact slab wording of the red version has not been checked against a physical label KB.

3rd Anniversary SPs

Shanks OP09-004 has 3rd Anniversary SP Gold and Silver versions in OP13. PSA files them as 3RD ANNIVERSARY-GOLD and 3RD ANNIVERSARY-SILVER GR.

Reprint

An OP-13 reprint was confirmed for 2026. Reprints add copies to the same PSA spec, so they are not a separate version KB.
