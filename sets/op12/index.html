Legacy of the Master (OP12) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2025-08-22 TCG. Our database has 155 card printings of 127 card numbers in it (119 Normal, 26 Parallel (AA), 6 SP, 3 DON!!, 1 SP Silver), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP12-RE TCG. The most-graded card is OP12-037 Demon Aura Nine Sword Style Asura Blades Drawn Dead Man's Game (Alternate Art (AA)) with 3,829 PSA 10 copies GR.
