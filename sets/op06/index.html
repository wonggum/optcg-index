Wings of the Captain (OP06) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2024-03-15 TCG. Our database has 151 card printings of 126 card numbers in it (119 Normal, 24 Parallel (AA), 6 SP, 3 DON!!, 1 Manga), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP06-PRE TCG. The most-graded card is ST01-007 Nami (Treasure Rare) with 5,065 PSA 10 copies GR.
