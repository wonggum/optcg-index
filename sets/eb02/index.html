Extra Booster: Anime 25th Collection (EB02, EB-02) is an English One Piece Card Game Extra Booster with an English release date of May 9, 2025 and 105 cards in its full card list, counting each parallel, manga and SP version separately. Start with the top chase cards below, or jump to the full card list.

Anime 25th Collection (EB-02) is an English Extra Booster released May 9, 2025 for the anime's 25th anniversary; every card has new art by artists from the TV anime. It has 87 card types, including a new Monkey D. Luffy Leader and 26 Special Cards of older Leaders and Characters. The EB02-061 Luffy Manga and the OP05-060 Luffy Special are its best-known PSA 10 cards.

FAQ

When was EB02 Extra Booster: Anime 25th Collection released in English?

Extra Booster: Anime 25th Collection (EB-02) was released in English on 2025-05-09 (official product page). The Japanese version came out on 2025-01-25.

How many cards are in EB02?

87 card types per the official product page. Our database has 105 card printings in the English Extra Booster.

What are the chase cards in EB02?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP05-060 Monkey.D.Luffy SP; EB02-061 Monkey.D.Luffy Manga.

What's new in EB02?

Commemorates the 25th anniversary of the anime. All cards feature new illustrations by artists from the TV anime. New Leader card Monkey D. Luffy.

Collector notes

Card count

The official product page lists 87 card types (Leader 1, Common 29, Rare 21, Super Rare 9, Secret Rare 1, Special Card 26). Our database has 61 base cards and 26 SP cards in the English booster, which matches OFF.

Special (SP) versions

EB02 SPs are older Leaders and Characters with new anime-art Special frames. PSA labels them with the variety SPECIAL (not SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART), e.g. OP05-060 Luffy GRKB.
