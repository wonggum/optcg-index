Anime 25th Collection (EB-02) is an English Extra Booster released May 9, 2025 for the anime's 25th anniversary; every card has new art by artists from the TV anime OFF. It has 87 card types, including a new Monkey D. Luffy Leader and 26 Special Cards of older Leaders and Characters OFFTCG. The EB02-061 Luffy Manga and the OP05-060 Luffy Special are its best-known PSA 10 cards KBGR.

Collector notes

Card count

The official product page lists 87 card types (Leader 1, Common 29, Rare 21, Super Rare 9, Secret Rare 1, Special Card 26). Our database has 61 base cards and 26 SP cards in the English booster, which matches OFFTCG.

Special (SP) versions

EB02 SPs are older Leaders and Characters with new anime-art Special frames. PSA labels them with the variety SPECIAL (not SPECIAL ALTERNATE ART), e.g. OP05-060 Luffy GRKB.

How many cards are in EB02?
