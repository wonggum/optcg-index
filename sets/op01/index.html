Romance Dawn (OP-01) is the first English One Piece Card Game booster, released December 2, 2022 together with Starter Decks ST-01 to ST-04 OFF. It has 121 card types, 8 of them Leaders, and introduced multi-color Leaders and box-topper parallels (1 of 6 per box) OFF. Collector favourites include the OP01-120 Shanks Manga, the only manga-art card in the set, and the alternate arts of Leaders such as OP01-003 Luffy, where pre-errata and errata copies are different PSA specs TCGKB.

Collector notes

Box waves

English OP-01 boxes exist in several print waves; first-print boxes carry big premiums. The exact wave list is not verified KB.

Errata

OP-01 had an 'Up to' errata on 2022-11-11. PSA has separate ERRATA varieties for some cards (e.g. OP01-003 Luffy); the pre-errata first print is usually the more valuable one KB.

FAQ

When was OP01 Romance Dawn released in English?

Romance Dawn (OP01) was released in English on 2022-12-02 (official product page; TCGplayer group publishedOn). The Japanese version came out on 2022-07-22.

How many cards are in OP01?

121 card types per the official product page. Our database has 154 card printings in the English booster.
