Romance Dawn (OP01, OP-01) is an English One Piece Card Game booster with an English release date of December 2, 2022 and 154 cards in its full card list, counting each parallel, manga and SP version separately. Start with the top chase cards below, or jump to the full card list.

Romance Dawn (OP-01) is the first English One Piece Card Game booster, released December 2, 2022 together with Starter Decks ST-01 to ST-04. It has 121 card types, 8 of them Leaders, and introduced multi-color Leaders and box-topper parallels (1 of 6 per box) (Source: official product page). Versions to tell apart include the OP01-120 Shanks Manga, the only manga-art card in the set, and the alternate arts of Leaders such as OP01-003 Luffy, where pre-errata and errata copies are different PSA specs.

FAQ

When was OP01 Romance Dawn released in English?

Romance Dawn (OP01) was released in English on 2022-12-02 (official product page). The Japanese version came out on 2022-07-22.

How many cards are in OP01?

121 card types per the official product page. Our database has 154 card printings in the English booster.

What are the chase cards in OP01?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP01-003 Monkey.D.Luffy Alternate Art (AA); OP01-120 Shanks Manga · Parallel; OP01-025 Roronoa Zoro Alternate Art (AA); OP01-070 Dracule Mihawk Alternate Art (AA); OP01-060 Donquixote Doflamingo Alternate Art (AA); OP01-120 Shanks Alternate Art (AA).

What's new in OP01?

Multi-color Leader cards debut. Character cards are the same as in the Starter Decks released December 2, 2022. Numerous parallel cards with different illustrations. Box topper parallel card (1 of 6 types at random).

Collector notes

Box waves

English OP-01 boxes exist in several print waves. The exact wave list has not been checked KB.

Errata

OP-01 had an 'Up to' errata on 2022-11-11. PSA has separate ERRATA varieties for some cards (e.g. OP01-003 Luffy), so pre-errata and errata prints are different PSA specs KB.
