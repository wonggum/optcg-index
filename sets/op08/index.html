Two Legends (OP08) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2024-09-13 TCG. Our database has 151 card printings of 126 card numbers in it (119 Normal, 24 Parallel (AA), 6 SP, 3 DON!!, 1 Treasure Rare), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP08-PRE TCG.

When was OP08 Two Legends released in English?

Two Legends (OP08) was released in English on 2024-09-13 (TCGplayer group publishedOn). The Japanese version came out on 2024-05-25.

How many cards are in OP08?

126 card numbers in the tcgplayer catalog. Our database has 151 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP08?
