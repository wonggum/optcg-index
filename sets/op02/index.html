Paramount War (OP02) is an English One Piece Card Game booster first listed on 2023-03-10 TCG. Our database has 154 card printings of 121 card numbers in it (121 Normal, 26 Parallel (AA), 6 Box Topper, 2 DON!!, 1 Manga), plus 75 event-stamped cards from OP02-PRE TCG. The most-graded card is OP02-013 Portgas.D.Ace (Manga · Parallel) with 1,582 PSA 10 copies GR.
