Awakening of the New Era (OP-05) is the English One Piece Card Game booster in which Gear 5 Luffy (OP05-119) debuts. It commemorates the game's 1st Anniversary with a ST01-012 Luffy card featuring an original illustration by Eiichiro Oda OFF. It introduced six Leaders, among them Sakazuki (OP05-041), Enel (OP05-098) and Monkey.D.Luffy (OP05-060), and three Super Parallel (manga-art) cards of the 'three captains' Law, Kid and Luffy OFFTCG.

FAQ

When was OP05 Awakening of the New Era released in English?

Awakening of the New Era (OP-05) was released in English on 2023-12-08 (official product page; TCGplayer group publishedOn). The Japanese version came out on 2023-08-26.

How many cards are in OP05?

126+1 card types per the official product page. Our database has 154 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP05?

By OPChase PSA 10 anchors: OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy Manga ($17,440); ST01-012 Monkey.D.Luffy Signature · Gold-Stamped Signature ($16,453); OP05-069 Trafalgar Law Manga ($2,150); OP05-074 Eustass"Captain"Kid Manga ($1,169); OP01-016 Nami SP ($601); OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy Alternate Art (AA) ($451); OP03-092 Rob Lucci SP ($128).
