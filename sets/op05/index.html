FAQ

When was OP05 Awakening of the New Era released in English?

Awakening of the New Era (OP-05) was released in English on 2023-12-08 (official product page). The Japanese version came out on 2023-08-26.

How many cards are in OP05?

126+1 card types per the official product page. Our database has 154 card printings in the English booster, plus 75 event-stamped cards.

What are the chase cards in OP05?

Versions OPChase tracks with checked eBay listings include: OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy Manga; ST01-012 Monkey.D.Luffy Signature · Gold-Stamped Signature; OP05-069 Trafalgar Law Manga; OP05-074 Eustass"Captain"Kid Manga; OP01-016 Nami SP; OP05-119 Monkey.D.Luffy Alternate Art (AA); OP03-092 Rob Lucci SP.

What's new in OP05?

Includes a 1st Anniversary card featuring an original illustration. GEAR5 Luffy debuts. 3 types of Super Parallel cards (the 3 captains); previous booster had 1 type. Enel and Sabo debut; Skypia and Revolutionary Army characters. 1st Anniversary Tournament starts Dec. 8th with exclusive prizes.
