ST-10: Ultra Deck The Three Captains (ST-10) is an English One Piece Card Game deck first listed on 2023-11-10 TCG. Our database has 17 card printings of 17 card numbers in it (17 Normal, 1 DON!!) TCG. The most-graded card is ST10-002 Monkey.D.Luffy (Normal) with 498 PSA 10 copies GR.
