One Piece Promotion Cards (OP-PR) is an English One Piece Card Game promo catalogue first listed on 2022-09-30 TCG. Our database has 1293 card printings of 594 card numbers in it (864 Promo, 202 Winner / Top prize, 188 Finalist / Top cut, 50 DON!!, 13 Release Event) TCG. The most-graded card is P-055 Monkey.D.Luffy (Promo · College Basketball US Voyage) with 35,273 PSA 10 copies GR.

Collector notes

Same number, many cards

A promo number identifies the artwork, not the version. P-001 alone has 8+ English versions (Promotion Pack 2022, Super Pre-Release, Super Pre-Release Winner, Store Championship Winner, Gift Collection, 25th Edition PCC and more). Always identify by number plus distribution; Winner, Finalist and participation versions are different PSA specs KBGR.
