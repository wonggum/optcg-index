About OPChase

Who we are

OPChase is an independent fan project for collectors of English-edition One Piece Card Game cards graded PSA 10. We are not affiliated with Bandai or PSA. The site helps you find the exact card you want, in the exact version and language, among current eBay listings.

Who it is for

OPChase is for collectors who want an English-edition PSA 10 copy of a specific One Piece card and want to be sure the listing matches the version they mean. If you collect Japanese-edition cards, or ungraded or lower-graded copies, most of the site is not built for you.

Where our card data comes from

The base of our Card Database is Bandai's official English site: the official card list for card numbers, names and printings, and the official product pages for which set or product a card was released in. For whether a card can be played in official events, we link to Bandai's Banned/Restricted Card List.

We are attaching an official source to every line, and lines already tied to a Bandai page carry a source tag. If you spot something that looks wrong, please report it.

How we organize it

We organize cards the way a collector shops for them, not the way a player builds a deck.

  • Versions and printings. One card number can exist as several printings, such as a standard version, a parallel or alternate art, and promo releases. Each card page lists every English printing we know of, so you can tell which one a listing is actually selling.
  • Which card to chase. Set pages, character hubs and tier lists group the printings collectors most often look for, so you can narrow down to one version before you start comparing listings.
  • Avoiding listing pitfalls. Listings often mix up versions, editions and grades. We check each listing for the exact version, English language and PSA 10 grade before showing it, and our guides explain how to tell similar printings and the two language editions apart. Our checks can miss things, so always check the PSA label and cert on eBay yourself before you buy.

What we do not do

  • We do not estimate, suggest or predict prices, and we do not value cards. Any price you see on OPChase is the seller's own eBay listing price.
  • We do not sell cards and we do not hold stock. All buying happens on eBay, between you and the seller.
  • We do not host card images. Listing photos you see come from eBay sellers.
  • Nothing on OPChase is financial advice.
