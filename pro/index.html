FAQ

Is OPChase Pro available now?

No. Pro is not launched yet and nothing can be bought. This page describes what we plan to build so we can see whether enough collectors want it.

What stays free?

Everything on the site today: the OPChase 100 index, tiers, card pages with price history, the Card Database, guides and the live eBay listings.

Are price alerts part of Pro?

No. OPChase Index price alerts are free for everyone (coming soon): one email when the OPChase Index for a card falls below or rises above a level you set, checked after each weekly index update. They use only the OPChase Index. OPChase does not send alerts about eBay listings.

Where does Pro data come from?

From the same data behind the free OPChase Index: completed English PSA 10 sales (via PriceCharting) and PSA population data (via GemRate). eBay listing data is never part of a paid feature.

How do I join the waitlist?

Open a waitlist issue on GitHub (free account). Issues are public, so please do not post your email address there. We reply on your issue when Pro opens.

Could the plan or price change?

Yes. Features, price and the trial are planned, not final. Waitlist members hear about any change before launch.

Is Pro financial advice?

No. Like the rest of OPChase, Pro shows estimates from public sales data. It is not financial advice.
