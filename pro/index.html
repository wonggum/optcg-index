Frequently Asked Questions

When will OPChase Pro launch?

We're gauging interest first. If enough collectors join the waitlist, we'll prioritize development. Early waitlist members will get priority access and special launch pricing.

Will the free features stay free?

Yes, absolutely. The OPChase 100 index, live eBay listings, card database, and all current free features will remain free forever. Pro is optional for collectors who want advanced tools.

How do price alerts work?

You'll set a target price for any card. When a PSA 10 listing appears at or below your target, you'll get an instant notification via Discord or email. You control how often and which channels.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes, we plan to offer a 7-day free trial so you can test all Pro features risk-free.

Do you support international collectors?

Pro features work worldwide, but our data focuses on US eBay listings and PSA grading. If you collect English PSA 10 cards from anywhere, Pro will help you track them.

Will there be a Formspree signup form?

Yes. The owner needs to provide the Formspree endpoint URL, which will be added to this page as a simple config constant. Until then, use GitHub or email.

What payment methods will you accept?

We'll support major credit cards and PayPal. Stripe will handle all payment processing securely.
